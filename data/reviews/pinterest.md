## Summary Card

**App:** Pinterest: Lifestyle Ideas
**Overall Risk:** Risky (Grade D)
**Score:** 44/100

### Red Flags
- **Your offsite activity tracked for ads** - Pinterest tracks your activity on websites with Pinterest features (like the Save button) and receives information about visits to advertiser sites through the Pinterest Tag
- **Data shared with Facebook and Google ad platforms** - Pinterest discloses your cookie IDs, IP address, and hashed email to Facebook Ads, Google Marketing Platform, and other third parties
- **Your Pins train AI models** - Pinterest uses your images and activity to train machine learning models including body type technology and generative AI, regardless of when Pins were posted
- **Age verification uses facial recognition** - Age verification may include facial age estimation and matching your face against a government ID
- **Activity logs kept for 25 months** - Log data retained for 25 months to provide services, improve features, and deliver targeted ads

### Green Flags
- **You can opt out of personalized ads** - Privacy and Data Settings allow opting out of targeted advertising and data sharing for ad purposes
- **Full account deletion available** - You can delete your account and data through settings
- **Strong rights for EU/UK users** - GDPR rights including access, portability, and objection for EEA, UK, and Swiss residents
- **Global Privacy Control respected** - Opt-out preference signals like GPC are respected for certain U.S. state residents

### Verdict
Pinterest is a popular visual discovery platform that engages in extensive data collection for advertising. It tracks your activity across websites, shares data with major ad platforms, and uses your content to train AI models. While opt-out options exist and GDPR rights are strong for European users, the platform's business model relies heavily on behavioral profiling and data sharing.

## Analysis Notes

### Key Findings

**1. Extensive Cross-Site Tracking**
Pinterest tracks your behavior both on and off the platform:
- Activity on websites with Pinterest features (Save button)
- Visits to advertiser sites via Pinterest Tag
- Information from ad partners and third parties about your activity
- Inferences about your interests, education, and professional experience

**2. Data Sharing with Ad Platforms**
Pinterest shares data with:
- Facebook Ads and Google Marketing Platform
- Advertisers (via lead ads and ad reporting)
- Service providers (security, analytics, hosting)
- Corporate family of companies
- Law enforcement when required

**3. AI Model Training**
Your content is used to train:
- Machine learning models (regardless of when Pins were posted)
- Body type technology (categorizes images into different body types)
- Generative artificial intelligence features

**4. Age Verification with Facial Recognition**
Age verification processes include:
- Facial age estimation
- Matching your face against a government ID
- Used for safety and compliance purposes

**5. Data Retention**
- Log data: 25 months (for service provision, safety, ads)
- Communications: 2 years
- Data subject request correspondence: 2 years
- Some data retained for legal obligations after account deletion

**6. Strong GDPR Framework**
For EEA, UK, and Swiss users:
- Joint data controllers: Pinterest Europe Ltd. and Pinterest, Inc.
- Legal bases: legitimate interests, consent, legal obligations
- Data Privacy Framework certification for US transfers
- Standard contractual clauses for third-party transfers

### User Rights
- **Access/Know** - Request information about data collected in portable format
- **Delete** - Delete account and data through settings
- **Correct** - Update information in your profile
- **Opt-out of targeted ads** - Privacy and Data Settings menu
- **GDPR rights** - Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection
- **Right to complain** - Lodge complaint with supervisory authority

### Privacy Controls
- Privacy and Data Settings for ad personalization
- Device settings for precise location, photos, notifications
- Browser controls for cookies
- Global Privacy Control signal support (certain U.S. states)
- Account deletion option

## Verbatim Policy Excerpts

**On Offsite Tracking:**
> "we customize the ads we show you by identifying your interests based on your onsite and offsite activities, as well as by using information we receive from ad partners or other third parties... activity on websites you visit that incorporate Pinterest features (like the 'Save' button)"

**On Data Sharing:**
> "we disclose information such as cookie IDs, your IP address, or a hashed version of your email address to third parties, such as Facebook Ads, Google Marketing Platform, and others who may combine that information with other information they already have about you and deliver ads about Pinterest to you."

**On AI Training:**
> "using information to train, develop and improve our technology such as our machine learning models, regardless of when Pins were posted... Pinterest uses machine learning technology to categorise images in Pins into different body types."

**On Age Verification:**
> "Conduct age verification, as appropriate. This may include facial age estimation and matching your face against a government ID."

**On Log Data Retention:**
> "We generally keep log data for a period of 25 months to provide our Services to you, to make Pinterest safe, improve our product features, and to deliver ads that are relevant, interesting and personal to you."

**On Contacts:**
> "If you previously chose to sync your contacts with your account, we will continue to process certain information about your contacts who are Pinterest users to help you find one another on our Services."

**On Location:**
> "We use your IP address to infer your approximate location, even if you don't choose to share your precise location, and photos you share may include information about the location where they were taken."

**On Data Privacy Framework:**
> "For transfers of personal data of EEA, Switzerland, and UK (and Gibraltar) residents to the U.S., Pinterest relies on and has certified to the U.S. Department of Commerce that it adheres to: the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (EU-U.S. DPF), the UK (and Gibraltar) Extension to the EU-U.S. DPF (UK Extension), and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Swiss-U.S. DPF)"
