# Privacy Review — Be My Eyes

**App:** Be My Eyes  
**Developer:** Be My Eyes ApS  
**Platform:** iOS  
**Scanned:** 2026-05-22  
**Policy URL:** http://www.bemyeyes.com/privacy  
**Policy Last Updated:** 2026-02-12  

---

## Summary Card

- **Overall Risk:** Caution
- **Score:** 75 / 100
- **Grade:** B
- **Recommendation:** Recommended — with caveats

### Red Flags
- **Videos may be shared for AI research.** The app may share recordings of your video calls with companies and organizations that are building AI tools for blind and low-vision people. While they try to remove personal details from the file, anything you say or show in the video—like your name, address, or face—could still be included.
- **Call recordings kept indefinitely.** The app stores recordings of your video calls forever unless you specifically ask them to delete them. Even then, they only promise to delete within 30 days of your request.
- **Cookies and analytics track website visits.** Their website uses cookies from Google Analytics, YouTube, and HubSpot to track how you browse. This is separate from the app itself, but it does mean your web visits are monitored.
- **Video sharing may count as data sale.** The company admits that sharing your videos with third parties for research could legally be considered a sale of personal data under privacy laws, even though they do not receive payment specifically for your data.

### Green Flags
- **No selling data for ads or tracking.** The app explicitly promises it does not sell your personal information to advertisers, marketers, or tracking companies. That is rare and worth noting.
- **Videos encrypted in transit and storage.** Your video calls and photos are scrambled with encryption while they are being sent and while they are stored. This makes it much harder for hackers to view them if they break in.
- **Strong deletion and privacy rights for all users.** No matter where you live, you can ask to see your data, fix it, move it, restrict its use, or delete it entirely. You can also opt out of having your videos shared with third parties.
- **AI photos and queries not used to train AI.** When you use the AI photo description feature, your pictures and questions are only used to answer your specific question. They are not saved or used to teach AI systems.

### Verdict
Be My Eyes is a genuinely useful and well-designed app with transparent privacy practices and strong security measures. However, the indefinite storage of video recordings and their potential sharing with third-party researchers is a meaningful privacy trade-off that users should understand before signing up. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information during calls.

## Detailed Category Breakdown

### Camera & Microphone — Caution
The app uses your camera and microphone during video calls, which is the entire point of the service. However, these calls are recorded and stored on the company's servers indefinitely unless you ask for deletion.

> "We collect and store the video calls, photos, audio that pass through our Service (i) to provide our Services; (ii) to enforce our Terms of Service, (iii) to promote and preserve safety, and (iv) to improve our Services and create new Services."

Concerns:
- Calls recorded and stored indefinitely by default
- May be shared with third parties for AI research

### Location & GPS — Caution
The app collects your country and time zone to match you with volunteers who speak your language and are awake when you are. It also gets a rough idea of your location from your internet address.

> "We use location and language data to make appropriate connections between blind or low-vision users and volunteers."

Concerns:
- IP address used for general location inference

### Contacts & Phonebook — Safe
The app does not ask for or access your phone contacts.

### Storage & File Access — Caution
You can choose to send photos to the AI for description. These photos are processed and then deleted after 60 days, or sooner if you ask.

> "Be My Eyes deletes Be My AI inquiries 60 days after creation, or within 30 days from receiving request from Blind or Low Vision user."

Concerns:
- Photos processed by third-party AI provider in the United States

### Data Sharing with 3rd Parties — Risky
The company may share your video call recordings with outside companies and organizations for AI research. They remove file details like your name, but anything visible or said in the video itself could still be seen by those partners.

> "We may license or otherwise share recorded video calls to other organizations that are performing research or working to develop products and services, including AI... We strip personal information from the file data associated with the videos we share. We also use available tools to try to identify videos with sensitive personal information and exclude them."

Concerns:
- Shared with for-profit companies
- Personal info visible in videos may be shared
- Opt-out is manual via email only

### Account & Identity Data — Caution
You need an account with your name, email, and language preferences to use the app. You can also sign in with Facebook, Google, or Apple if you prefer.

> "When you register for Be My Eyes through our app, we collect the following information to create your account, provide our Services, provide customer support, and contact you: Your name, Your email address, Your preferred language(s), Your country of residence and time zone, The model(s) and operating system information of the device(s) you use."

Concerns:
- Data stored in the United States
- Phone number may be required for SMS verification (hashed)

### Behavioural & Ad Tracking — Caution
The app itself does not track your behavior for ads. However, their website uses Google Analytics and other tools to see how people browse, though they say this is not connected to your account.

> "Be My Eyes does not sell the personal data we collect to third parties for advertising, marketing, or tracking... The analytics data we receive are not connected to your account or correlated with your personal information."

Concerns:
- Website analytics via Google Analytics
- YouTube and HubSpot cookies on website

### Children's Data Handling — Safe
The app is not for children under 18. If they learn a child has signed up, they will delete that child's information.

> "Our services are not directed to, and we do not intend to, or knowingly, collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 18... If we learn that any personal information we collect has been provided by a child under the age of 18, or the applicable higher age of digital consent, we will promptly delete that personal information."

### Network & Internet Activity — Caution
The app logs your internet address and how you use the service to keep things running smoothly and securely. This browsing data is not linked to your personal account.

> "We use cookies, pixels, SDKs, and similar technologies to collect usage and analytics data when you use our Services. This information is primarily used to keep our Services running securely and reliably, and to help us understand how they are being used so we can make improvements."

Concerns:
- Browsing behavior tracked via third-party analytics

### Device Info & Fingerprinting — Caution
The app collects basic details about your device like its model and operating system to help match you with volunteers and keep the app working.

> "The model(s) and operating system information of the device(s) you use to make or answer calls using our app."

Concerns:
- Device identifiers collected

### Health & Biometric Data — Safe
The app does not collect health or biometric data directly. They actually warn you not to share sensitive health information during calls.

> "Don't share information about your medicines, health conditions, or other sensitive personal information."

### Financial & Payment Data — Safe
The app is completely free and does not handle any payment information.

### Data Deletion & User Rights — Safe
You have excellent control over your data. You can ask to see, fix, move, restrict, or delete your information at any time by emailing them. You can also opt out of having your videos shared.

> "Be My Eyes applies the privacy rights required by GDPR and various US state laws to all our users, volunteers, and visitors to our website. You can find those rights below under the heading 'Control Over Your Information.'"

### Policy Change Notification — Caution
If they make important changes to this policy, they will tell you by email or post a notice on the app. If you keep using the app after that, it means you agree to the new rules.

> "If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will notify you by email to your registered email address, by prominent posting on our Services, or through other appropriate communication channels. All changes shall be effective from the date of publication unless otherwise provided."

Concerns:
- Continued use equals acceptance of changes
