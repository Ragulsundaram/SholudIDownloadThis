## Summary

Tinder's privacy policy is written in a casual, conversational tone, but the data collection practices are extensive and invasive. As part of the Match Group family, Tinder shares data across all Match Group dating platforms. The app collects sensitive personal data including sexual orientation, sexual life details, health information, political beliefs, face geometry biometrics, and government-issued ID. Behavioral tracking is comprehensive, covering every swipe, match, and message.

Key positives: The app requires users to be 18+, which is stronger than the 13+ standard. Precise geolocation is opt-in. Account deletion is available.

Key negatives: Extensive sensitive data collection, biometric face geometry capture, government ID requirements for verification, cross-platform data sharing with all Match Group apps, and comprehensive behavioral tracking for ads and profiling.

## Methodology

This review was conducted by analyzing the Tinder Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, accessed via jina.ai text extraction on 2026-05-12. The privacy policy was updated April 4, 2026.

### Data Collection Observed

- **Account Data:** Phone number, email, date of birth
- **Profile Data:** Gender, interests, preferences, approximate location, sexual orientation, sexual life, health, political beliefs
- **Content:** Photos, videos, audio, text, chats, search queries
- **Purchase Data:** Transaction details, credit card info (when not using Apple/Google billing)
- **Third-Party Data:** Contacts (Friends in Common), reports involving other members
- **Social Media Data:** From linked Facebook, Instagram, Spotify, Apple, LinkedIn accounts
- **Usage Data:** Activity, interactions, features used, ads interacted with, referring pages
- **Technical Data:** IP, device ID, device type, OS, network info, sensor data, advertising IDs, cookies
- **Geolocation Data:** Precise GPS (with permission), approximate location from IP
- **Face Data:** Face geometry for Photo Verification, Photo Check, ID + Photo Verification
- **ID Data:** Government-issued ID for identity verification

### Notable Excerpts

> "When you complete your profile, you share additional details about you, such as your gender, interests, preferences, approximate location, etc. Some of this data may be considered sensitive or special in certain countries, such as details about sexual orientation, sexual life, health, or political beliefs."

> "You may choose to participate in certain of our features like Photo Verification, Photo Check, or ID + Photo Verification, that involve the collection of face geometry data, which may be considered biometric data in some jurisdictions."

> "We are part of the Match Group family of businesses. Match Group considers the safety and security of members a top priority. If you were banned from another Match Group service, your data can be shared with us to allow us to take necessary actions, including closing your account or preventing you from creating an account on our service."

> "Using the service generates data about your activity, including how you use it (e.g., when you logged in, features you've been using, actions taken, information shown to you, referring webpages, ads you interacted with) and how you interact with others (e.g., searching, matching, communicating)."
