## Summary Card

- **Overall risk:** Risky (Score: 51, Grade: D)

### Red Flags
- **May collect sensitive profile details.** Tinder states that some profile data you choose to provide may be considered sensitive, such as details about sexual orientation, sexual life, health, or political beliefs.
- **Collects face geometry for verification.** Certain features like Photo Verification, Photo Check, and ID + Photo Verification involve the collection of face geometry data, which may be considered biometric data.
- **May require government-issued ID.** Tinder states that you may provide a copy of your government-issued ID to help it verify your identity.
- **Shares your data with Match Group companies.** Tinder shares data about you with Match Group affiliates, who also share data about you with Tinder, for safety enforcement and service improvement.
- **Collects precise GPS location if permitted.** Tinder collects your geolocation latitude and longitude if you give permission; if you decline, some features become unavailable.
- **Pulls data from linked social media accounts.** You may share data with Tinder through accounts on other platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, Spotify, Apple, and LinkedIn.

### Green Flags
- **Requires users to be at least 18.** Tinder restricts its service to individuals who are 18 or older.
- **You can delete your account.** You can delete your account and data, though a safety retention window applies.
- **You can decline precise geolocation.** Tinder asks for permission before accessing your precise GPS location, and you can decline.

### Verdict
Tinder collects deeply personal data including optional biometric verification and sensitive profile details, then shares it broadly across Match Group companies and with advertisers. While the app offers account deletion and some controls, the scale of data collection and sharing is significant. Consider carefully whether the privacy trade-offs are acceptable for a dating app.
