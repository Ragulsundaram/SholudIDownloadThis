## Summary Card

**App:** Facebook  
**Overall Risk:** Dangerous — **Score: 30 / Grade: F**

### Red Flags
- **Everything you do is tracked for ads.** Facebook collects your posts, messages, photos, videos, location, device info, and browsing history. All of this is used to build a profile of you so advertisers can show you targeted ads.
- **Your content may be reviewed by humans.** Facebook uses both computers and real people to review your posts, messages, photos, and videos. This includes content you send in private messages.
- **Data shared with hundreds of partners.** Your information is shared with advertisers, analytics companies, measurement firms, marketing vendors, service providers, external researchers, and AI integrations. They also give data to governments and law enforcement when asked.
- **Tracked across all your devices and apps.** Meta tracks you across Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger, and even other apps and websites you visit. They combine this into one big profile to target ads at you everywhere.
- **Your content is used to train AI.** Meta uses the photos, videos, messages, and posts you create to train and improve their artificial intelligence systems. They do not clearly ask for your permission each time they do this.

### Green Flags
- **You can download and delete your data.** Facebook provides tools to download a copy of your data or delete your account. However, they keep some information even after deletion for legal and safety reasons.
- **Claims it does not sell your data.** Facebook states that they do not sell your personal information to anyone. However, they still share it widely with partners for advertising and other purposes.

### Verdict
The world's biggest data-collection machine: everything you do, say, and share is tracked, analyzed, and used to sell ads. Facebook's business model is built on maximizing data collection for advertising. The sheer volume of data gathered, the breadth of third-party sharing, and the use of content for AI training make this one of the most privacy-invasive apps available.

## Category Breakdown

### Camera & Microphone — Risky
The app uses your camera and microphone when you take photos, record videos, or use voice features. Meta stores this content and may use it to train AI or show you ads.

### Location & GPS — Risky
Facebook collects your location from your device settings, IP address, and the content you share. It uses this to show local content, ads, and recommendations.

### Contacts & Phonebook — Dangerous
Facebook collects your friends, followers, and connections to suggest people you may know, show you relevant content, and target ads.

### Storage & File Access — Risky
The app can access your camera roll when you choose to share photos or videos. Meta stores this content and may use it to train AI or show you ads.

### Data Sharing with 3rd Parties — Dangerous
Meta shares your information with advertisers, analytics companies, measurement firms, marketing vendors, service providers, external researchers, and AI integrations. They also give data to governments and law enforcement when asked.

### Account & Identity Data — Risky
You must create an account with your real name and information. Meta links this identity across all its products and uses it to personalize ads and content.

### Behavioural & Ad Tracking — Dangerous
Meta tracks what you do across its products and devices to show you personalized ads. They also track your activity on other apps and websites you visit.

### Children's Data Handling — Risky
The app is not meant for children under 13, but teenagers commonly use it. Meta does not have special protections for teen data beyond the general policy.

### Network & Internet Activity — Risky
Meta logs your IP address, network information, and device activity to understand how you use their products and to target ads.

### Device Info & Fingerprinting — Risky
Meta collects detailed information about your device, what you're doing on it, and identifiers that tell it apart from other users. This helps them track you across devices and apps.

### Health & Biometric Data — Safe
The app does not collect health or biometric data as a core feature.

### Financial & Payment Data — Risky
Meta tracks your purchases and transactions on their platform, including truncated credit card information. They use this to understand your spending habits and show you relevant ads.

### Data Deletion & User Rights — Caution
You can download and delete your data, but Meta keeps some information even after deletion for legal and safety reasons.

### Policy Change Notification — Caution
Meta says they will notify you before making big changes to the privacy policy, and you'll have a chance to review the new terms.
