**Developer:** Airbnb, Inc.  
**Platform:** iOS  
**Category:** Travel  
**Analyzed:** 2026-06-01

## Summary Card

**Score:** 53 / 100 (Grade D — Risky)  
**Overall Risk:** Risky  
**Recommendation:** Think twice

### Red Flags
- **Asks for government ID and selfie.** Airbnb may require you to upload images of your passport, driver's license, or national ID card, plus a selfie, to verify your identity before you can book or host.
- **Stores facial recognition data.** Airbnb extracts facial recognition data from the photos and ID documents you submit for verification. This biometric information is stored and processed on their servers.
- **Your messages are scanned and analyzed.** Airbnb reviews, scans, and analyzes your emails, calls, messages, and uploaded photos on the platform for fraud prevention, safety, support, and product improvement purposes.
- **Tracks what you do outside Airbnb.** Airbnb collects information about pages you view and engage with before and after using the platform, even if you haven't created an account or logged in.
- **Data shared with hosts, buildings, and governments.** Your personal information is disclosed to hosts, building management, third-party vendors, corporate affiliates, tax authorities, and government agencies — including in China where data may be shared without further notice.

### Green Flags
- **30-day notice for major policy changes.** If Airbnb makes material changes to its privacy policy, it will email you at least 30 days before the changes take effect, giving you time to decide whether to cancel your account.
- **Detailed privacy rights for many regions.** Airbnb provides specific privacy rights and contact information for residents of the EU, UK, Brazil, US, China, and Korea, including GDPR and LGPD rights where applicable.
- **Account cancellation option exists.** You can cancel your Airbnb account if you disagree with policy changes or no longer want to use the service. Some controls are also available within your Account settings.
- **AI training uses de-identified data.** When Airbnb uses your personal information to develop and improve AI models, it says it removes references to your name and other identifying information beforehand.

### Verdict
Airbnb is a widely used travel platform, but its privacy practices are extensive. Government ID verification, facial recognition storage, message scanning, and off-platform tracking make it a data-intensive service. Use it with awareness that your identity, location, and behavior are deeply profiled.

## Category Breakdown

### Camera & Microphone — Caution
Users voluntarily upload photos, videos, and other content for listings and profiles. The platform may also record calls and communications for customer support and fraud prevention purposes.

> Excerpt: "User-Generated Content. Such as any photos, videos, reviews, and other content you choose to provide."

### Location & GPS — Risky
Airbnb collects precise or approximate location from GPS and IP address. This may occur even when the app is not in use if device permissions allow. Location is used for bookings, personalization, and advertising.

> Excerpt: "We may also collect this information when you're not using the app if you enable this through your settings or device permissions."

### Contacts & Phonebook — Caution
Users may voluntarily import or manually enter address book contacts. Co-travelers may also be added by trip organizers. This is user-initiated and not automatic.

> Excerpt: "import or manually enter address book contacts"

### Storage & File Access — Safe
File access is limited to user-initiated uploads of photos, videos, and documents for listings, profiles, and verification. No automatic scanning or broad file access is described.

### Data Sharing with 3rd Parties — Risky
Airbnb discloses personal information to hosts, building management, third-party vendors, corporate affiliates, events providers, and government/tax authorities. Data may also be shared with Chinese government agencies for users in China.

> Excerpt: "Airbnb Singapore and Airbnb China may disclose your personal information to Chinese government agencies without further notice to you."

### Account & Identity Data — Risky
Airbnb collects standard contact info plus government-issued ID, passport, driver's license, tax ID, and selfie photos for identity verification. This identity data is linked to advertising and personalization efforts.

> Excerpt: "we may ask you for an image of your government-issued ID document(s), passport, national ID card, tax ID, or driving license"

### Behavioural & Ad Tracking — Risky
Airbnb collects searches, bookings, pages viewed, device identifiers, and off-platform activity. This is used to personalize advertising, send marketing messages, and analyze characteristics for promotional content.

> Excerpt: "show, personalize, measure, and improve our advertising... analyze characteristics and preferences to send promotional messages, marketing, advertising, and other information"

### Children's Data Handling — Safe
The app is a travel booking platform for adults. The policy does not specifically address children's data or COPPA/GDPR-K compliance.

### Network & Internet Activity — Caution
Airbnb collects IP addresses, access dates/times, pages viewed, searches, links clicked, and off-platform activity. This information may be collected even without an account.

> Excerpt: "the pages you've viewed or engaged with before or after using the Airbnb Platform, and other actions on the Airbnb Platform"

### Device Info & Fingerprinting — Risky
Airbnb collects device and advertising identifiers, hardware/software information, device event information, other unique identifiers, crash data, and read receipts. This may be collected even without an account.

> Excerpt: "device and advertising identifiers, hardware and software information, device information, device event information, other unique identifiers"

### Health & Biometric Data — Risky
Airbnb collects biometric information, specifically facial recognition data derived from photos and identification documents submitted for verification. This is stored on Airbnb servers and shared with third-party verification providers.

> Excerpt: "Biometric Information. Such as facial recognition data derived from photos and identification documents you submit for verification, where offered and with your consent where required by applicable law."

### Financial & Payment Data — Caution
Airbnb collects payment account details, credit card information, bank account information, PayPal email, IBAN, billing address, and transaction history. Payment processing involves multiple Airbnb Payments entities depending on your country.

> Excerpt: "payment account, credit card information, bank account information, payment instrument used, date and time, payment amount"

### Data Deletion & User Rights — Caution
Users can access and update some personal information through Account settings and can cancel their account. However, reviews and forum postings may remain publicly visible after account cancellation. Residual copies may persist in backup systems.

> Excerpt: "Personal information you have shared with other Members, such as Reviews and forum postings, may continue to be publicly visible on Airbnb, even after your Airbnb Account is canceled."

### Policy Change Notification — Caution
Material policy changes trigger email notice at least 30 days in advance. Users can cancel their account if they disagree. However, continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

> Excerpt: "In case of material changes, we will also provide you with notice of the modification by email at least thirty (30) days before the effective date."
