## Summary Card

### Red Flags
- **Your exact location is shared with other users.** Grindr collects your precise location and shows other users how far away you are. Even if you hide the distance number, the app admits that clever users can still figure out exactly where you are by moving around and checking your relative position.
- **HIV status and health data are public by default.** Grindr lets you add HIV status, last tested date, PrEP usage, and vaccination status to your profile. If you do, this information becomes visible to every other user on the platform. Grindr does not verify this information.
- **AI reads and analyzes your private messages.** Grindr uses artificial intelligence (including from OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic) to analyze the content of your messages to suggest chat replies, summarize past conversations, and recommend people to reconnect with.
- **Data shared with ~30 third-party companies.** Grindr shares your data with a large network of partners including OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Meta, Amazon Web Services, AppsFlyer, Braze, AppLovin, and many others for AI development, advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and customer support.
- **Your entire profile is public to all users.** Your photos, age, height, weight, body type, ethnicity, gender, pronouns, sexual preferences, and social media links are all visible to every Grindr user by default.
- **Biometric data collected for age checks.** In some countries like the UK, Grindr may require you to submit a video selfie of your face, or pair it with an official government photo ID, to prove you are over 18.
- **Tracked for ads on other apps and websites.** Grindr shares your device advertising ID with partners like AppsFlyer and Meta to show you ads for Grindr on other apps like Instagram and Snapchat.

### Green Flags
- **Health data not shared with advertisers.** Grindr explicitly says your HIV status, last tested date, and vaccination status are never shared with advertising companies. They are only shared with necessary service providers like Amazon Web Services and Braze.
- **Notified before major policy changes.** Grindr promises to notify you in advance if they make important changes to the privacy policy.
- **Consent management platform for ads.** Grindr provides a consent preference screen where you can control whether your data is used for behavioral advertising.
- **Can opt out of AI training.** You can opt out of having your personal information used to develop and train Grindr's AI technology.

### Verdict
Grindr collects some of the most sensitive personal data of any social app: your precise location (displayed to other users), HIV status, health practices, sexual preferences, photos, messages, and even biometric data for age verification. All profile information is public by default. Your data is shared with approximately 30 third-party partners including OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Meta, and numerous advertising networks. Messages are analyzed by AI for personalization. The app also acknowledges that sophisticated users can determine your exact location even if you hide your distance.
