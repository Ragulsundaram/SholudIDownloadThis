## Summary

YouTube's privacy practices are governed by Google's umbrella privacy policy, which applies across all Alphabet/Google services. The app collects extensive behavioral data including watch history, search queries, interactions with content and ads, and activity on third-party sites using Google services. This data is used to build comprehensive user profiles for ad targeting across Google's ecosystem and partner sites.

Key positives: Google does not sell personally identifying information (name, email) to advertisers directly; users have meaningful controls to turn off personalized ads, pause watch/search history, and delete data via Google Takeout. Anonymous viewing is possible without an account.

Key negatives: Cross-site tracking via Google's ad and analytics services creates a comprehensive behavioral profile. Passive location inference from IP and other signals is always active. Policy changes are accepted by continued use rather than active consent.

## Methodology

This review was conducted by analyzing the Google Privacy Policy (applicable to all Google services including YouTube) and YouTube Terms of Service. The Google policy was accessed via jina.ai text extraction on 2026-05-12. Policy last updated per Google: July 2024.

### Data Collection Observed

- **Identifiers:** Name, email, phone number, unique device/browser identifiers
- **Activity:** Videos watched, search terms, ad interactions, purchase activity, communication patterns
- **Location:** GPS (if enabled), IP-based inference, WiFi/Bluetooth/cell tower signals
- **Device:** Model, OS, carrier, app version, crash reports, system activity
- **Content:** Uploaded videos, comments, playlists, channel subscriptions
- **Cross-site:** Activity on third-party sites/apps using Google ad/analytics services

### Notable Excerpts

> "We collect information about your activity in our services, which we use to do things like recommend a YouTube video you might like. The activity information we collect may include: terms you search for, videos you watch, views and interactions with content and ads, voice and audio information, purchase activity, people with whom you communicate or share content, activity on third-party sites and apps that use our services."

> "We don't show you personalized ads based on sensitive categories, such as race, religion, sexual orientation, or health. We don't show you personalized ads based on your content from Drive, Gmail, or Photos. We don't share information that personally identifies you with advertisers, such as your name or email, unless you ask us to."

> "Google does not sell your personal information. Google also does not 'share' your personal information as that term is defined in the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)."
