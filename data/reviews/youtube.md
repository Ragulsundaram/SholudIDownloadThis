## Summary Card

**App:** YouTube
**Overall Risk:** Caution (Grade C)
**Score:** 65/100

### Red Flags
- **Cross-site tracking** — Collects activity on third-party sites and apps that use Google services
- **Behavioral ad targeting** — Uses videos watched and searches made to show personalized ads across Google services
- **Passive location collection** — Estimates location from IP, Wi-Fi access points, cell towers, and Bluetooth devices
- **Extended data retention** — Some data kept until account deletion; longer retention for legal and security purposes

### Green Flags
- **No PII sharing with advertisers** — Google states it does not share name/email with advertisers unless you ask it to
- **Strong ad controls** — My Ad Center allows you to control ad personalization and turn off personalized ads
- **History controls** — You can pause and delete YouTube Search History and Watch History
- **Policy protection** — Google explicitly states it will not reduce your rights without explicit consent
- **Sensitive category exclusion** — No personalized ads based on race, religion, sexual orientation, or health

### Verdict
YouTube tracks extensive viewing activity across Google services and third-party sites for ad targeting. However, Google provides strong privacy controls including history deletion, ad personalization settings, and clear data management tools. The explicit commitment to not reducing user rights without consent is a significant positive.

## Analysis Notes

### Key Findings

**1. Comprehensive Activity Tracking**
Google collects extensive activity data across services:
- Terms you search for
- Videos you watch
- Views and interactions with content and ads
- Voice and audio information
- Purchase activity
- People with whom you communicate or share content
- Activity on third-party sites and apps using Google services

**2. Cross-Site Activity Association**
Google may link activity from other sites to your account:
> "Depending on your account settings, your activity on other sites and apps may be associated with your personal information in order to improve Google's services and the ads delivered by Google."

**3. Strong Privacy Controls**
Google provides comprehensive user controls:
- **My Activity** — Review and delete specific items by date and topic
- **My Ad Center** — Control ad personalization, modify interests, turn off personalized ads
- **YouTube History** — Pause and delete Search History and Watch History
- **Privacy Checkup** — Review and adjust important privacy settings
- **Data Export** — Download a copy of your Google Account data
- **Account Deletion** — Delete specific products or entire Google Account

**4. No PII Sharing with Advertisers**
Google explicitly states:
> "We don't share information that personally identifies you with advertisers, such as your name or email, unless you ask us to."

**5. Location Collection**
Multiple location signals collected automatically:
> "GPS and other sensor data from your device, IP address, Activity on Google services, such as from your searches or places you label like home or work, Information about things near your device, such as Wi-Fi access points, cell towers, and Bluetooth-enabled devices"

**6. Policy Change Protection**
Strong user rights commitment:
> "We will not reduce your rights under this Privacy Policy without your explicit consent."

**7. Data Retention**
Different retention periods based on data type:
- Some data: deletable anytime by user
- Some data: auto-deleted after set period
- Some data: kept until account deletion
- Some data: retained longer for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations

**8. Sensitive Category Exclusion**
Google excludes sensitive categories from ad targeting:
> "We don't show you personalized ads based on sensitive categories, such as race, religion, sexual orientation, or health."

**9. No Drive/Gmail/Photos Ad Targeting**
Content from these services not used for ads:
> "We don't show you personalized ads based on your content from Drive, Gmail, or Photos."

**10. Apps Installed Tracking**
Google tracks which apps you have installed:
> "which apps you've installed"

## Verbatim Policy Excerpts

**On Cross-Site Activity:**
> "Activity on third-party sites and apps that use our services... Depending on your account settings, your activity on other sites and apps may be associated with your personal information in order to improve Google's services and the ads delivered by Google."

**On Ad Personalization:**
> "Depending on your settings, we may also show you personalized ads based on your interests and activity across Google services. For example, if you search for 'mountain bikes,' you may see ads for sports equipment on YouTube."

**On PII Sharing:**
> "We don't share information that personally identifies you with advertisers, such as your name or email, unless you ask us to."

**On Sensitive Categories:**
> "We don't show you personalized ads based on sensitive categories, such as race, religion, sexual orientation, or health."

**On Policy Changes:**
> "We will not reduce your rights under this Privacy Policy without your explicit consent."

**On Deletion Controls:**
> "You can export a copy of content in your Google Account... Delete your content from specific Google services... Delete specific Google products... Delete your entire Google Account."

**On Location Collection:**
> "GPS and other sensor data from your device, IP address, Activity on Google services, such as from your searches or places you label like home or work, Information about things near your device, such as Wi-Fi access points, cell towers, and Bluetooth-enabled devices."

**On Apps Installed:**
> "unique identifiers, browser type and settings, device type and settings, operating system, mobile network information including carrier name and phone number, and application version number... which apps you've installed"
