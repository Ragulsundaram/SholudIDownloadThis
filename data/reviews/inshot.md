## Summary

InShot is a video and photo editing app from SHANTANU PTE. LTD. It stands out for its notably privacy-friendly approach: no account required, all media stays on your device, and explicit promises not to collect face data or photos.

Key concerns: third-party ad SDKs (Admob, AppLovin) collect device identifiers for personalized ads, and some AI features temporarily upload photos to cloud servers for processing.

Key positives: no account registration needed, all content stored locally, explicit no-face-data policy, anonymized ad data, easy opt-out for personalized ads, strong children's data protection, and both CCPA and GDPR rights available.

---

## Methodology

This review was conducted by analyzing the InShot Privacy Policy and Terms of Use, accessed via jina.ai text extraction on 2026-05-12.

### Data Collection Observed

- **Media:** Local storage access only (photos/videos selected by user for editing)
- **Audio:** Microphone for audio recording, drafts stored locally
- **Cloud processing:** Photos temporarily uploaded for AI features (auto captions, advanced remove, precise cutout), deleted after processing
- **Device:** Unique device identification code collected by ad SDKs
- **Customer service:** Email address, username, email content (only when contacting support)
- **No PII:** No registration, no tracking of visits, no collection of personal info by default

### Notable Excerpts

> "Generally, we do not collect any personal identifiable information ('PII') from you when you download our applications. To be specific, we do not require the consumers to get registered before downloading the application, nor do we keep track of the consumers' visits of our application, and we don't store such PII."

> "InShot will never collect and store your photos or face data when you use our application. All videos and photos on your device will be stored locally."

> "Admob, AppLovin, as advertisers of InShot, may collect information generated during your use of our products and services, extract your preferences, and use them to show you ads that are more in line with your individual needs. We will use technical means to anonymize the data."
