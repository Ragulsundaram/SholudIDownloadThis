## Summary Card

**Overall Risk:** Safe  
**Score:** 83 / 100 (Grade A)

### Red Flags
- **Data may be stored in the US or India:** YogiFi says your personal data may be transferred to servers located in the United States or India, which means different data protection laws could apply.
- **Privacy policy can change without direct notice:** YogiFi posts revised privacy policies with an updated date, and your continued use of the app counts as acceptance. There is no promise of advance notification.
- **Microsoft Clarity tracks usage pathways:** YogiFi uses Microsoft Clarity to capture how users navigate the platform, though it claims this data does not personally identify you.

### Green Flags
- **No cross-app tracking:** The App Store privacy label shows YogiFi does not use any data to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies.
- **All collected data is unlinked to your identity:** According to the App Store privacy label, all data YogiFi collects — including Health & Fitness, Contact Info, and Identifiers — is kept unlinked to your identity.
- **Health data not used for advertising or mining:** YogiFi explicitly states that HealthKit data is used only to provide health, motion, and fitness services, and not for promotional mining or advertising.
- **Pose camera data processed locally and not stored:** TrueDepth camera inputs used for yoga pose feedback are processed momentarily on your device without being stored remotely or linked to your identity.
- **Strong user rights including erasure and portability:** You can request access to, correction of, erasure of, portable copies of, or restrictions on your personal information.
- **Deletion requests handled within 90 days:** YogiFi promises to erase your data within 90 days of a deletion request when possible, unless records must be kept for legal or safety reasons.

### Verdict
YogiFi's privacy profile is unusually strong for a health app. The App Store privacy label confirms that all collected data — including Health & Fitness, Contact Info, and Identifiers — is not linked to your identity. The app does not track you across other apps or websites. HealthKit data is used only for health and fitness services, not promotional mining. TrueDepth camera data for pose detection is processed momentarily on-device without remote storage. The privacy policy provides clear user rights including access, correction, erasure, and data portability.
