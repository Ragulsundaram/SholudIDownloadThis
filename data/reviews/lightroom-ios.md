## Summary

Adobe Lightroom is a professional photo and video editing app with cloud sync and AI-powered features. While it offers powerful editing tools, it comes with significant privacy trade-offs: your photos are uploaded to Adobe's cloud where they may be analyzed by AI, and Adobe shares your usage data with advertising partners.

Key concerns: photos uploaded to Adobe cloud and analyzed by machine learning, extensive data sharing with advertising partners and social media platforms, behavioral tracking across Adobe services, biometric features (face/voice grouping), and 10-year data retention for some records.

Key positives: Adobe explicitly does not sell your personal information, offers strong privacy controls including GDPR and CCPA rights, and provides encryption and two-step verification for account protection.

## Methodology

This review was conducted by analyzing the Adobe Privacy Policy and Terms of Use, accessed via jina.ai text extraction on 2026-05-12.

### Data Collection Observed

- **Photos/Videos:** Uploaded to Adobe cloud for syncing and editing across devices
- **AI Analysis:** Cloud photos analyzed using machine learning for recommendations and service improvements
- **Identity:** Name, email, phone, address, date of birth, company/school info (Adobe ID required)
- **Device:** Device ID, browser type, settings, unique identifiers, language, configuration
- **Behavior:** Clicks, navigation, content interactions, browsing history across Adobe services
- **Network:** IP address, search terms, referring webpage, usage patterns
- **Biometric:** Optional faceprints and voiceprints for photo grouping features (off by default)
- **Location:** Precise geolocation when adding location data to photos
- **Payment:** Billing information for paid subscriptions

### Notable Excerpts

> "our Services and Software will access your content stored locally on your device ('Local Content') and content that you have uploaded to our servers or create using our cloud-based Services ('Cloud Content')."

> "The types of third parties your information may be disclosed to include: our resellers and other sales and advertising partners, retailers, advertisers, ad agencies, advertising networks and platforms, information service providers."

> "Biometric identifiers or information defined under United States laws or other applicable laws (e.g., faceprints and voiceprints, etc.)... These features are off by default and, should you choose to enable them, you can always disable these features. Where we process biometric identifiers or biometric information to deliver a feature requested by you, we delete this information once you turn off the feature."

> "We delete certain personal information we collect about you when we no longer have a business reason to retain it. Additionally, there is some personal information we need to retain even after you close your account to comply with business and legal requirements, such as personal information related to our contract and business transactions with you, which we retain for ten years after your last interaction with us."
