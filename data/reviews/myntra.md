## Analysis Notes

### Key Findings

**1. Privacy Policy Inaccessible (Major Concern)**
Myntra's privacy policy URL (myntra.com/privacypolicy) returns an HTTP2 error and cannot be accessed. This makes it impossible to verify their complete data practices.

**2. Broad Content License (Concerning)**
When you upload content to Myntra:
- You grant Myntra "worldwide, perpetual and transferable rights"
- Myntra can use your content for "any type of use forever"
- Includes promotional and advertising purposes
- Includes creation of derivative works

**3. Data Sharing with Sellers**
For customization services:
- Your name and contact details are shared with sellers or brands
- You may be required to share additional personal data directly with sellers
- Myntra is not responsible for how sellers handle your data

**4. PAN Card Collection**
For purchases over ₹2 lakh:
- Scanned copy of PAN card required within 4 days
- Order will be cancelled if not provided
- Name on PAN card must match account name

**5. Payment Processing**
- Payment facility is "merely a facilitator" not a banking service
- Payments processed through third-party gateways (ICICI, Razorpay, etc.)
- Myntra not acting as trustee or fiduciary
- Government ID may be requested for verification

**6. Data Retention**
- Accounts not accessed for 2 years: data deleted
- Transaction details preserved for tax/regulatory compliance
- No clear mechanism for on-demand account deletion

### User Rights
- **Limited deletion rights** - Data deleted only after 2 years of inactivity
- **No clear access rights** - Privacy policy unavailable to verify
- **No promotional calls** - Explicitly stated they won't use data for promotional calls/SMS

### Privacy Controls
- Account settings to manage orders and addresses
- Option to cancel alteration requests
- No information about advertising opt-outs (privacy policy missing)

## Verbatim Policy Excerpts

**On Content License:**
> "Such Content will become Our property and You grant Us the worldwide, perpetual and transferable rights in such Content. We shall be entitled to, consistent with Our Privacy Policy as adopted in accordance with applicable law, use the Content or any of its elements for any type of use forever, including but not limited to promotional and advertising purposes and in any media whether now known or hereafter devised, including the creation of derivative works."

**On Data Sharing with Sellers:**
> "You consent to share your name and contact details with the seller or brand to facilitate this service. You may be required to share some additional personal data directly with the seller, Myntra shall not be responsible for any use or handling of your additional personal data shared with the seller or brand."

**On PAN Card Collection:**
> "As required by applicable law, if the Customer makes a purchase of an amount equal to or above INR 2 00 000.00, the Customer will be required to upload a scanned copy of his/her PAN card on the Platform, within 4 days of making the purchase, failing which, the purchase made by the Customer will be cancelled."

**On Data Deletion:**
> "If You have not accessed Platform for a period of 2 (two) Years, Your data (including transactional data) will be deleted from Our record and You will not be able to access such data."

**On Payment Processing:**
> "You understand, accept and agree that the payment facility provided by Myntra is neither a banking nor financial service but is merely a facilitator providing an electronic, automated online electronic payment..."

**On Policy Changes:**
> "We reserve the right, at Our sole discretion, to change, modify, add or remove portions of these Terms of Use, at any time without any prior written notice to You. Your continued use of the Platform following the posting of changes will mean that You accept and agree to the revisions."
