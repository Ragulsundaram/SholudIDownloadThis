## Summary

Cult.fit's privacy policy reveals extensive collection of sensitive health data for a fitness app. It gathers biometric information, workout data, nutrition logs, sleep habits, mental wellbeing information, and even vaccination status and past medical history. The app uses your device camera for 'live energy' tracking during workouts, accesses your mobile address book for the 'squads' social feature, and collects CCTV footage at physical premises. Data is shared with affiliates, service providers, and may be transferred in a business acquisition. The policy is notably dated (August 2021) and does not detail encryption standards.

Key concerns: extensive health and biometric data collection including medical history and vaccination status, camera used for live workout tracking, address book accessed for squads feature, CCTV footage collected at physical premises, data may be transferred in business acquisition, sensitive data may be transferred outside India.

Key positives: explicitly does not sell personal information, express consent required for third-party sharing, has a dedicated grievance officer, not directed at children under 18, some information editable in account settings.

## Summary Card

### cult.fit Gym Workout & Fitness
- **Overall Risk:** Caution
- **Score:** 59/100 (Grade C)

**Red Flags:**
- **Collects biometric and medical history.** Cult.fit collects biometric information, vaccination status, past medical history, mental wellbeing, disabilities, and other health-related data for safety and service provision.
- **Camera used for live workout tracking.** Cult.fit uses your device camera to capture outputs for 'live energy' tracking and conducting live workout sessions.
- **Accesses your mobile address book.** Cult.fit accesses data in your mobile address book to help users find other users who have enabled 'squads' and set their profiles as public.
- **CCTV footage collected at physical premises.** Cult.fit collects CCTV footage of common areas at their physical premises including fitness centres and gyms.
- **Data may be transferred in business acquisition.** If Cult.fit is acquired, goes out of business, or enters bankruptcy, your personal information would be one of the assets transferred to or acquired by a third party.
- **Sensitive data may be transferred outside India.** Cult.fit may transfer your sensitive personal data (SPDI) to body corporates located outside India, subject to applicable law.

**Green Flags:**
- **Does not sell personal information.** Cult.fit explicitly states they neither rent nor sell your personal information in personally identifiable form to anyone.
- **Express consent required for third-party sharing.** For Diverse Services, Cult.fit shares information with third parties only in relation to whom you provide express consent.
- **Has a dedicated grievance officer.** Cult.fit has appointed Mr. Srinivas Reddy as Grievance Officer with email (grievance@diverseretails.com) and physical address in Bangalore.
- **Not directed at children under 18.** Cult.fit does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under age 18 and will delete such information if discovered.
- **Some information editable in account settings.** Through your account settings, you may access, and in some cases edit or delete, your name, password, email address, and profile information.

**Verdict:** Cult.fit collects extensive health and biometric data including medical history and vaccination status, uses your camera for live workout tracking, accesses your address book, and shares data with affiliates and third parties with your consent. If you are concerned about extensive health and biometric data collection, camera access during workouts, address book access, and broad data sharing with affiliates and third parties, consider the trade-off before installing.
