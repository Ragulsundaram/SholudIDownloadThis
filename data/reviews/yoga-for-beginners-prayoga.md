## Summary Card

### Yoga for beginners | Prayoga
**Risk Level:** Caution | **Score:** 69/100 | **Grade:** C

#### Red Flags
- **Third-party advertisers track you with cookies:** Prayoga allows selected third-party advertising companies to place tracking technology on the app so they can collect data about how you interact with it and show you targeted ads.
- **Data may transfer in a sale or merger:** If Prayoga is sold, merges with another company, or is acquired, your personal information may be transferred to the new owner as part of the deal.
- **Do-Not-Track signals are ignored:** Prayoga explicitly says it does not respond to Do-Not-Track browser signals, meaning your request not to be tracked online is not honoured.
- **Camera and storage permissions requested:** The app asks for access to your camera and device storage. While this may be for body tracking, the policy does not clearly limit what data is accessed beyond these permissions.

#### Green Flags
- **Data kept for maximum 30 days only:** Prayoga promises to keep your personal information for no longer than 30 days, which is one of the shortest retention periods we have seen in any app.
- **Apple Health data never used for ads:** Prayoga explicitly promises that Apple HealthKit data is used only for health and fitness services, and never for marketing, advertising, or data mining — even by third parties.
- **Data deletion request form available:** You can submit a request form to review, update, or delete your personal information, and Prayoga promises to respond within 30 days.
- **Material policy changes are notified:** If Prayoga makes important changes to its privacy policy, it says it will either post a prominent notice or send you a direct notification.
- **Does not sell or rent your information:** Prayoga states it does not share, sell, rent, or trade your information with third parties for their promotional purposes.

#### Verdict
Prayoga's actual privacy policy (a Termly-generated template) reveals data practices that go beyond the App Store's 'Data Not Collected' label. The app requests camera and storage permissions, shares data with third-party vendors and advertisers who use tracking cookies, and reserves the right to transfer data in a merger or acquisition. On the positive side, the developer caps data retention at 30 days, promises Apple Health data is never used for marketing, offers a data-deletion request form, and says it will notify users of material policy changes.
