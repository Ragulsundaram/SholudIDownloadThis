## Summary Card

**Overall Risk:** Caution  
**Score:** 64 / 100 (Grade C)

### Red Flags
- **Cross-app tracking for ads:** H&M's App Store label shows that your purchases, search history, identifiers, and usage data may be used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies.
- **Data shared with major ad platforms:** H&M shares your personal identifiers and activity data with advertising partners such as Facebook, Instagram, Google, TikTok, and Snapchat for personalised advertising.
- **Device identifiers used for advertising:** H&M collects device identifiers and may share encrypted email addresses or device IDs with advertising partners to target and measure ads.
- **Financial info linked to your identity:** The app collects financial information and links it to your identity, which is standard for shopping apps but increases risk if data is breached.
- **Data may be processed outside your region:** H&M's cookie settings note that in some cases your data may be processed outside the EU or EEA, which could mean weaker data protection laws apply.

### Green Flags
- **Multiple opt-out options available:** H&M offers a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Data" form, recognises browser opt-out preference signals, and respects iOS tracking permission settings.
- **Privacy portal for data rights:** H&M provides a "My Privacy Portal" where you can view your online interactions and exercise rights like access, deletion, and correction of your data.
- **Location data is strictly opt-in:** H&M only processes your location data within the app if you actively turn on location-based services in your device settings.
- **User content not linked to identity:** Photos and other user content you upload are collected but are not linked to your identity according to the App Store privacy label.

### Verdict
H&M's App Store privacy label reveals that your purchases, search history, device identifiers, and usage data may be used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. The app also collects your contact info and financial information linked to your identity. On the positive side, H&M provides a "My Privacy Portal" where you can exercise data rights, offers a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Data" option, and respects iOS device tracking opt-outs. Location-based services are strictly opt-in.
