# Privacy Review: GetYourGuide: Plan & Book

## Summary Card

- **Score:** 70 / 100
- **Grade:** B
- **Risk Level:** Caution
- **Category:** Travel
- **Developer:** GetYourGuide AG
- **Analyzed:** 2026-06-01

GetYourGuide is a travel booking platform that offers tours, activities, and experiences worldwide. This privacy review examines their data collection practices, third-party sharing, and user rights under EU and US privacy frameworks.

---

## What It Collects

**Account & Profile Data:**
- Name, email address, password (for registered accounts)
- Or social login data (Facebook: name, email, photo; Google/Apple: basic profile)
- Booking details: name, address, phone, wishlists, reviews, ratings
- For certain tours: passport number, government ID, or other identification required by the activity provider

**Device & Technical Data:**
- Device type, operating system, browser, app version
- Language settings, app crashes, other systematic activity
- Device ID and advertising ID (via SDKs)
- IP address (encrypted, deleted after 30 days)

**Usage Data:**
- Pages visited, time spent, clicks, scrolling behavior
- Wishlists, reviews, support interactions
- Customer service communications (including via AI-powered chatbots)

**Payment Data:**
- Partial credit card digits (first 6, last 4 via Adyen)
- Payment method type, confirmation of success/failure
- For alternative payments: PayPal, Stripe, Checkout.com, Klarna, J.P. Morgan

---

## What It Does With Your Data

**Extensive Third-Party Sharing:**
GetYourGuide operates a large partner ecosystem. Your data flows to:

- **Hosting & Infrastructure:** Amazon Web Services (AWS)
- **Customer Service:** Zendesk (tickets), Transcom, Teleperformance, Zowie (AI chatbot), Automaited (AI assistant)
- **Marketing & CRM:** Braze (newsletters, notifications), Google Campaign Manager, Google Ads, Meta Pixel, TikTok Ads, Criteo, Microsoft Bing, Snapchat
- **Analytics:** Google Analytics, Hotjar, Adjust
- **Fraud Prevention:** Sift Science
- **AI Translation:** DeepL, OpenAI
- **Payment Processors:** Adyen (primary), PayPal, Stripe, Checkout.com, Klarna, J.P. Morgan
- **Activity Providers:** Direct booking details shared with tour operators

**Behavioral Tracking:**
- Cookies, web beacons, SDKs, and tracking URLs monitor your activity across the platform
- Interest-based advertising enabled through Google, Meta, TikTok, Criteo, Microsoft, and Snapchat
- Marketing partners use cookies to retarget you with ads on other websites and apps
- SDKs in the mobile app collect device identifiers and in-app behavior

**AI Usage:**
- Zowie and Automaited AI assistants help process customer service requests
- OpenAI and DeepL may translate your messages in some cases

---

## The Good

1. **Strong GDPR Compliance:** Based in Germany, GetYourGuide follows strict EU privacy laws. You have clear rights to access, correct, delete, and download your data.
2. **IP Address Protection:** Your IP address is encrypted and deleted after just 30 days — better than most travel platforms.
3. **No Data Sales:** Explicitly states they do not "sell" personal information under CCPA.
4. **Complete Account Deletion:** Deleting your account completely and permanently removes your profile from their systems.
5. **Cookie Consent Manager:** You can customize which types of tracking and marketing you allow, including turning off marketing and analytical tracking separately.
6. **Limited Payment Visibility:** For credit card payments, they only see partial digits; full numbers handled by Adyen.
7. **No Camera/Microphone:** Doesn't access your camera or microphone.
8. **No Contacts Access:** Doesn't read your phonebook.

---

## The Concerning

1. **Broad Marketing Partner Network:** Data flows to Google, Meta, TikTok, Criteo, Microsoft, Snapchat, and various affiliate networks for personalized advertising.
2. **Cross-Site Tracking:** Interest-based ads follow you around the web unless you explicitly opt out.
3. **Passport/ID Requirements:** Some tours require government ID or passport numbers, which are shared with third-party activity providers.
4. **AI Reads Support Messages:** Customer service messages may be processed by AI tools from Zowie, Automaited, OpenAI, and DeepL.
5. **Complex Ecosystem:** With so many partners, it's hard to track exactly where your data ends up and how each partner uses it.

---

## Verdict

GetYourGuide handles privacy reasonably well for a travel booking platform. The company demonstrates strong GDPR compliance, encrypts and quickly deletes IP addresses, and provides clear user rights including full account deletion. The extensive third-party marketing and advertising partner ecosystem is typical for the industry but represents the main privacy trade-off. Users should adjust cookie preferences to limit cross-site tracking if desired.

**Risk Level:** Caution — Proceed with awareness of the marketing tracking ecosystem.
