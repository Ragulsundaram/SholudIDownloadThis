## Summary Card

**App:** AllTrails: Hike, Bike & Run  
**Overall Risk:** Risky  
**Score:** 59/100  
**Grade:** D

### Red Flags
- **Your profile and activity are public by default** — When you create an AllTrails account, everything you add—your profile, hiking history, photos, reviews, and community posts—is visible to anyone on the internet unless you manually change your privacy settings.
- **Activity data shared with advertising partners for targeted ads** — AllTrails shares your browsing behavior, device info, and usage patterns with advertising companies to show you targeted ads on other websites and apps. Under some state laws, this may be classified as selling your data.
- **Precise geolocation tracked continuously while you hike** — If you enable location services, AllTrails collects your exact latitude and longitude in real time to direct you to trails. This reveals exactly where you hike, run, and bike.
- **Your phone contacts are synced to find friends** — AllTrails regularly reads your phone's address book to see which of your contacts also use the app, then suggests you connect with them.
- **Your AI chat inputs may be used to train AI models** — When you use AllTrails' AI features—like asking questions about trails or using the plant identifier—your text prompts and queries may be used to train the underlying AI models.
- **Your data could be sold or transferred in a business deal** — If AllTrails is acquired, merges with another company, or sells part of its business, your personal data may be included as part of the transferred assets.

### Green Flags
- **Respects Global Privacy Control opt-out signals** — If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control opt-out signal, AllTrails will automatically stop using cookies that result in selling or sharing your personal information for targeted advertising.
- **HealthKit and Google Fit data are walled off from marketing** — If you connect Apple HealthKit or Google Fit, AllTrails cannot read that data and promises not to use it for advertising or share it with ad platforms.
- **Full privacy rights for US and EEA/UK users** — Depending on where you live, you can request access to your data, ask for corrections, demand deletion, opt out of targeted ads, and lodge complaints with data-protection authorities.
- **Payment details handled by Stripe and PayPal, not AllTrails** — When you buy a subscription, your credit card details are collected directly by Stripe or PayPal. AllTrails never sees or stores your payment card information.

### Verdict
AllTrails is an incredibly useful app for outdoor enthusiasts, offering millions of trail maps and a vibrant community. However, its privacy practices are concerning: your profile and content are public by default, your precise location is tracked continuously, your contacts are synced, and your activity data is shared with advertising partners. If you choose to use AllTrails, immediately switch your profile to private, limit location permissions, and review your privacy settings carefully.
