- **Conversations not used for ad targeting** - Microsoft explicitly states they do not use what you say in Copilot chats, emails, or documents to target ads to you.

#### Verdict
Microsoft Copilot is a powerful AI assistant that collects extensive data to personalize your experience and improve its services. While Microsoft offers transparent privacy controls and opt-out options for AI training and targeted advertising, the breadth of data collection and sharing with advertising partners requires caution. Best suited for users already in the Microsoft ecosystem who are comfortable managing privacy settings. Children receive strong protections, but parents should still monitor usage.
