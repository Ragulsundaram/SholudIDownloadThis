### Red Flags
- **Policy can change without warning.** The developer says they may update the privacy policy anytime, and only promises to reflect changes on the page — not to notify you in advance.
- **Data shared with analytics providers.** Anonymous usage stats and purchase data are processed by third-party services like analytics providers. While not personal data, it still leaves your device.
- **Several privacy categories not explained.** The privacy policy is shared across multiple apps and doesn't specifically say what data grug collects for things like contacts, storage, or network activity.
- **No formal data deletion process.** The only way to delete your data is to uninstall the app. There's no button or email process to request deletion from the developer's servers.

### Green Flags
- **Developer promises not to sell data.** The policy clearly states they do not sell or share your personal data with anyone.
- **No location tracking.** The developer explicitly says they do not track your location.
- **Collects minimal data only.** The developer says they only collect the bare minimum needed to run the app, and don't gather sensitive data unrelated to the app's purpose.
- **Payments handled by app store.** Any purchases go through Apple or Google, so the app developer never sees your credit card information.

### Verdict
Grug appears to be a low-risk app that collects very little personal information and makes a clear promise not to sell your data. However, the privacy policy is brief, shared across multiple apps, and doesn't address several important categories. Policy changes can also happen without advance notice. It's a reasonable choice for most users, but those who want full transparency should be aware of the gaps.
