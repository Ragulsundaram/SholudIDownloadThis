## Summary Card

### Microsoft Bing Search — Grade D (52/100) — Risky

**One-liner:** A powerful search app with extensive data collection and AI training practices that raises significant privacy concerns.

**Red Flags:**
- **Your data trains AI models** — Microsoft uses your search queries and interactions to train and improve their AI models, including Copilot. This means your questions and searches could be used to teach AI systems.
- **Personalized advertising with data sharing** — Your data is shared with third parties for personalized advertising purposes. Microsoft collects browsing data and combines it across services to target ads to you.
- **Tracks you across Microsoft services** — Microsoft combines data from Bing, Windows, Edge, Office, and other services to build a detailed profile of you. Your activity in one app affects what you see in others.
- **Location data collected continuously** — Your precise location is collected and can be used for search results, weather, local recommendations, and advertising purposes.
- **Search history stored and used** — All your search queries are logged and used to personalize results, improve services, and target ads. This creates a detailed record of your interests and activities.

**Green Flags:**
- **Can opt out of personalized ads** — Microsoft provides controls to opt out of personalized advertising, though this doesn't stop all data collection.
- **Some data deletion options** — You can view and delete some of your activity data through your Microsoft account privacy dashboard.
- **Complies with privacy frameworks** — Microsoft complies with EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and other international data protection standards.

**Verdict:** Microsoft Bing Search is a feature-rich search app that leverages AI to deliver curated results. However, it comes with significant privacy trade-offs. The app extensively tracks your searches, location, device info, and usage patterns—data that is then used to train AI models and target ads. While you can opt out of personalized ads and delete some data, the breadth of collection and cross-service tracking make this app a poor choice for privacy-conscious users. If you need a search engine, consider more private alternatives like DuckDuckGo or Brave Search.

## Category Analysis

### Camera & Microphone — Caution
**Access:** On-demand only  
**Required for core function:** No  

Camera and microphone are only accessed when you use features like voice search or visual search. Not used in background. However, uploaded images may be used to improve Bing image processing services.

### Location & GPS — Risky
**Access:** Continuous  
**Required for core function:** No  

Your precise location is continuously collected for local search results, weather, and advertising. This includes GPS and IP-based location. Location history may be stored.

### Contacts & Phonebook — Safe
**Access:** Never  

Bing Search does not request or access your contacts or phonebook.

### Storage & File Access — Caution
**Access:** On-demand  

File access is only used when you upload images for Visual Search. Files are not continuously scanned. Uploaded images may be retained for service improvement.

### Data Sharing with 3rd Parties — Risky
**Access:** Continuous  

Your data is shared with vendors, affiliates, and third parties for personalized advertising and service operation. This includes data brokers and ad networks.

### Account & Identity Data — Risky
**Access:** On-demand  

A Microsoft account is optional but links all your activity across Microsoft services. Your identity is tied to searches and rewards when signed in.

### Behavioural & Ad Tracking — Risky
**Access:** Continuous  

Your search queries, browsing, and app usage are tracked to show you personalized ads. This tracking happens across Microsoft services and partner sites.

### Children's Data Handling — Caution

Microsoft states services are not intended for children under 13 without parental consent. Some data collection restrictions apply for child accounts. App is rated 17+ in App Store.

### Network & Internet Activity — Risky
**Access:** Continuous (core function)  

All your internet searches and browsing through Bing are logged. Your IP address is collected and used for location inference and security.

### Device Info & Fingerprinting — Risky
**Access:** Continuous  

Detailed device information is collected including device ID, operating system, and browser type. This is used to identify your device across services.

### Health & Biometric Data — Safe

Bing Search does not collect health or biometric data. Face ID/Touch ID is handled locally by iOS if used for app unlock.

### Financial & Payment Data — Safe

No payment features within the Bing Search app. Microsoft Rewards credits are managed separately through your Microsoft account.

### Data Deletion & User Rights — Caution

You can view and delete some activity data through your Microsoft account privacy dashboard, but not all data may be deleted.

### Policy Change Notification — Caution

Policy changes are posted on the website with an updated date. Microsoft may notify users of significant changes, but continued use equals acceptance.

## Recommendations

1. **Use without signing in** — Avoid linking your Microsoft account to prevent activity from being tied to your identity.
2. **Opt out of personalized ads** — Visit Microsoft's ad preferences page to disable personalized advertising.
3. **Clear search history regularly** — Use the privacy dashboard to delete your search activity periodically.
4. **Consider alternatives** — For privacy-focused search, consider DuckDuckGo, Brave Search, or Startpage.
