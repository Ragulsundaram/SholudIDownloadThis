## Summary Card

**App:** Taimi: LGBTQ+ Dating & Meet Up  
**Overall Risk:** Dangerous — **Score: 38 / Grade: F**

### Red Flags
- **Sexual orientation data shared with ad networks.** Taimi collects and shares information about your sexual orientation and preferences with third-party advertising partners like Facebook and Snapchat to show you targeted ads.
- **Humans review your photos and videos.** Your uploaded photos and videos are reviewed by both computer systems and real people at Taimi and third-party moderation companies to check if they break the rules.
- **Your private messages are stored on company servers.** When you send messages, photos, or videos to other users through the app, Taimi stores the content of those messages on their servers.
- **Data shared with dozens of companies.** Your data is shared with at least 15 different companies including Facebook, Snapchat, Google, AWS, and multiple advertising and analytics firms.
- **You may need to upload your passport or license.** To verify your age or identity, Taimi may ask you to upload photos of your driver’s license or passport, which they store and process.

### Green Flags
- **Asks for explicit consent for sensitive data.** Before collecting sensitive information like sexual orientation, the app asks for your clear, explicit permission.
- **Strictly 18 and older.** The app does not allow anyone under 18 to use it, and will delete accounts if they discover an underage user.
- **You can request full account deletion.** You can delete your account, and most of your data will be removed within 7 days.

### Verdict
An LGBTQ+ dating app that collects and shares an extraordinary amount of personal, sensitive, and location data with dozens of third-party advertisers, moderators, and social media platforms. The combination of highly sensitive identity data, extensive third-party sharing with ad networks, manual human review of private content, and server-side storage of messages makes this a high-risk app for user privacy.

## Category Breakdown

### Camera & Microphone — Risky
You can upload photos and videos to your profile and messages. These are stored on Taimi's servers and reviewed by both computer systems and human moderators.

### Location & GPS — Risky
The app collects your location to show how far away other users are. If you don't allow location access, some features won't work. Your location data is also shared with advertisers.

### Contacts & Phonebook — Risky
If you sign in with Facebook, Snapchat, or Apple, the app can access your friends list and contacts from those services.

### Storage & File Access — Risky
You can upload photos, videos, and documents to your profile and messages. These are stored on Amazon's cloud servers.

### Data Sharing with 3rd Parties — Dangerous
Your data is shared with at least 15 different companies including Amazon, Facebook, Snapchat, Google, and multiple advertising and analytics firms. Some of them get your sexual orientation and location data.

### Account & Identity Data — Risky
You must provide your real name, email, phone number, date of birth, gender identity, and photos. For verification, you may need to upload your passport or driver's license.

### Behavioural & Ad Tracking — Dangerous
Taimi tracks everything you do in the app and shares it with advertisers to show you personalized ads. They use your location, interests, gender, age, and device information for ad targeting.

### Children's Data Handling — Safe
The app is strictly for adults 18 and older. If they find an underage user, they delete the account and data.

### Network & Internet Activity — Risky
Taimi logs your IP address, what websites you visited before and after using the app, and details about your internet connection.

### Device Info & Fingerprinting — Risky
Taimi collects extensive information about your device including model, operating system, screen size, battery level, memory, and whether you have headphones connected. This is shared with multiple analytics and advertising companies.

### Health & Biometric Data — Safe
The app does not collect health or biometric information as a core feature.

### Financial & Payment Data — Safe
Payments are handled by third-party processors like Apple, Google Play, and Solidgate. Taimi does not store your full credit card number.

### Data Deletion & User Rights — Risky
You can delete your account, and most data is removed within 7 days. However, some information like purchase records, support tickets, and banned account identifiers are kept for up to 5 years.

### Policy Change Notification — Safe
Taimi says they will email you or notify you in the app before making big changes to the privacy policy.
