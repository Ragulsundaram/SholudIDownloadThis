## Summary Card

**App:** Tira: Online Beauty Shopping  
**Overall Risk:** Caution  
**Score:** 67 / 100 (Grade C)

### Red Flags
- **Data shared with advertisers and partners.** The app shares your information with advertising companies, sponsors, and other business partners. They can also place cookies on your device to track you for targeted ads.
- **Browsing data used for targeted ads.** The app collects your IP address, browser info, and where you came from to build a profile and show you targeted advertisements.
- **Data may be stored outside India.** Your personal information can be transferred, stored, or processed in countries outside India, where data protection laws may be different.
- **Policy can change without direct notice.** The company can change this privacy policy at any time by posting a new version on the website. They do not promise to email or notify you directly when changes happen.
- **Uploaded photos may be processed by third parties.** When you upload a photo for product search or your profile, that image may be processed by outside companies, and Tira says it is not responsible for how they handle it.

### Green Flags
- **Payments handled by secure third parties.** Your credit card and bank details are collected by trusted payment gateways using encryption. Tira does not store your raw payment info itself.
- **Does not knowingly collect data from children.** The app says it is not meant for users under 18, and if they learn a child signed up, they will delete that information.
- **You can opt out of promotional emails.** You can unsubscribe from marketing emails and newsletters using the unsubscribe link or by contacting their support team.
- **Uses encryption and security measures.** The company says it uses industry-standard security tools like firewalls and encryption to protect your personal information from unauthorized access.

### Verdict
Tira is a standard e-commerce app for beauty products, but its privacy practices are middling. While payments are secure and children's data is handled responsibly, the broad sharing of your data with advertisers and the use of tracking cookies for targeted ads are significant downsides. If you care about privacy, think twice before creating an account.
