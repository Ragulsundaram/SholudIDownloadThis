## Summary

Google Maps collects your precise GPS location, sensor data, Wi-Fi access points, cell towers, and Bluetooth devices continuously. This data is used for driving directions, search results, and personalized ads based on your location. Your data is shared across Google services and may be retained even after deletion. Some data is shared with affiliates, partners, and for legal reasons.

Key concerns: continuous precise location tracking, location used for targeted ads, cross-service data sharing, sensor-based movement tracking, and data retention after deletion.

Key positives: You can export and delete some data, and you can manage location settings on your device.

## Methodology

This review was conducted by analyzing the Google Privacy Policy (April 2, 2026), accessed via verification files on 2026-05-16.

### Data Collection Observed

- **Location:** GPS, sensor data, IP address, Wi-Fi access points, cell towers, Bluetooth-enabled devices
- **Identity:** Name, email, phone, password, payment info (Google Account optional)
- **Device:** Unique identifiers, browser type, device type, OS, carrier, application version, accelerometer, gyroscope
- **Activity:** Searches, clicks, navigation, content interactions, purchase activity
- **Network:** IP address, referrer URL, crash reports, system activity
- **Communications:** Call and message logs if using Google communication services

### Notable Excerpts

> "We collect location information when you use our services, which helps us offer features like driving directions, search results for things near you, and ads based on your location. Depending on the products you're using and settings you choose, Google may use different types of location information to help make some services and products you use more helpful. These include: GPS and other sensor data from your device; IP address; Activity on Google services, such as from your searches or places you label like home or work; Information about things near your device, such as Wi-Fi access points, cell towers, and Bluetooth-enabled devices."

> "Your device may have sensors that can be used to better understand your location and movement. For example, an accelerometer can be used to determine your speed and a gyroscope to figure out your direction of travel."

> "We don't show you personalized ads based on your content from Drive, Gmail, or Photos. Depending on your settings, we may also show you personalized ads based on your interests and activity across Google services."

> "We do not share your personal information with companies, organizations, or individuals outside of Google except in the following cases: With your consent; With domain administrators; For external processing; For legal reasons."
