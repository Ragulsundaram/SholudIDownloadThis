---

## Summary

ChatGPT's privacy practices reflect the tension between building powerful AI and protecting user privacy. The core concern is that all User Content (prompts, files, images, audio, video) is logged and used to train models by default. Users must actively opt out to prevent this. Free users receive personalized ads from multiple partners.

Key positives: users retain ownership of AI-generated Output; can opt out of model training; Temporary Chats are not saved; can delete conversations or account; can export data; de-identified data is not re-identified.

Key negatives: contact sync uploads full address book; business account admins can access employee Content; content is monitored for safety; advertisers share purchase data; extensive usage tracking.

---

## Methodology

This review was conducted by analyzing the OpenAI Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, accessed via jina.ai text extraction on 2026-05-12. The privacy policy was last revised April 1, 2026.

### Data Collection Observed

- **Account:** Name, email, DOB, credentials, payment info, transaction history, profile picture, username
- **User Content:** All prompts, files, images, audio, video, connected services data, social interactions (posts, comments, messages)
- **Communication:** Name, contact, message contents
- **Contacts:** Full device address book uploaded via Contact Sync
- **Log Data:** IP address, browser type/settings, date/time, interaction patterns
- **Usage Data:** Content viewed, features used, actions taken, feedback, people interacted with, timezone, country, dates/times, user agent, device type
- **Device:** Device name, OS, device identifiers, browser
- **Location:** General area from IP, optional precise GPS
- **Cookies:** Session maintenance, authentication, customer support
- **Other Sources:** Security partners, marketing vendors, advertisers (purchase data), publicly available internet data

### Notable Excerpts

> "We may use Content you provide us to improve our Services, for example to train the models that power ChatGPT."

> "If you choose to connect your device contacts, we upload information from your device address books and check which of your contacts also use our Services."

> "We may receive information from advertisers and other data partners, which we use for purposes including to help us measure and improve the effectiveness of ads shown to Free and Go users on our Services."

> "When you join a ChatGPT Enterprise or business account, the administrators of that account may access and control your OpenAI account, including being able to access your Content."
