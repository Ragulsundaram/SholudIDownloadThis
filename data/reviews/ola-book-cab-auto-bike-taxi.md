## Summary Card

**Score:** 64 / 100 (Grade C — Caution)  
**Overall Risk:** Caution  
**Recommendation:** Think twice

### Red Flags
- **Your ride location fuels promotions.** Ola uses your GPS location not just to match you with drivers, but also to send you promotions and offers. Your whereabouts help decide which ads you see.
- **Trip history kept forever after account closure.** Even if you delete your Ola account, the company explicitly says it keeps your location history, trip records, and payment history indefinitely for legal reasons and other business purposes.
- **Follows you around the web with targeted ads.** Ola tracks what pages you visit and what you search for, then uses that behavior to show you ads on other websites outside of Ola's own apps and site.
- **Wide sharing with partners and advertisers.** Your data is shared with driver companies, payment processors, co-branded partners, contest sponsors, market research firms, and advertisers. The policy also allows your information to be transferred to a new owner if Ola is ever sold.
- **Account deletion requires emailing support.** You cannot delete your account yourself inside the app. You have to email Ola support and ask them to do it, and even then they keep most of your data.

### Green Flags
- **Most data is tied to ride logistics.** The majority of data Ola collects, like your name, phone number, and pickup address, is genuinely needed to connect you with a driver and complete your ride.
- **Emergency contacts are optional.** You choose whether to add emergency contacts to share ride details with. Ola does not automatically scan or upload your entire phonebook.
- **Notifies you of major policy changes.** If Ola makes material changes to this privacy policy, they say they will notify you by email or through the app before the changes take effect.

### Verdict
Ola works well for getting around Indian cities, but its privacy policy is broader than the ride itself. Your location, trip history, and browsing behavior are kept permanently and used for ads. Use cash payments and share minimal profile data if you want to limit your exposure.

## Category Breakdown

### Camera & Microphone — Safe
The privacy policy does not mention accessing your camera or microphone. Users may upload photos manually (e.g., in reviews), but the app does not claim to access the camera directly for core functionality.

### Location & GPS — Risky
GPS coordinates are sent to Ola's servers throughout the ride via the driver's phone. The app also collects your geolocation automatically. This data is shared with driver companies and used to send promotions and offers. Location data is retained indefinitely even after account termination.

> Excerpt: "The driver's mobile phone will send your GPS coordinates, during the ride, to our servers. Most GPS enabled mobile devices can define one's location to within 50 feet."

### Contacts & Phonebook — Caution
Ola does not scan or automatically upload your full contact list. It only collects contact names and phone numbers that you explicitly provide for emergency contacts, referrals, or booking rides for others. This data is stored on Ola's servers.

> Excerpt: "In case you upload an emergency contact (name and phone number) on your account / on the Mobile Application, we will be collecting and storing such contact information."

### Storage & File Access — Safe
The policy does not mention accessing your phone's files, photo gallery, or storage beyond optional manual uploads.

### Data Sharing with 3rd Parties — Risky
Ola shares data with driver companies, third-party service providers (hosting, analytics, payments), OEM partners, co-branded services, contest sponsors, and market research firms. The policy also allows disclosure to government agencies and in business transfers (mergers/acquisitions).

> Excerpt: "We may share your information, including your Protected Information and Usage Information with third parties for any purpose, including but not limited to undertaking market research/ study."

### Account & Identity Data — Caution
Ola collects standard account information including full name, email, password, address, mobile phone number, and zip code. This is necessary for ride booking, driver contact, and payment. No government ID or social media linking is mentioned.

### Behavioural & Ad Tracking — Risky
Ola uses information about your web and mobile browsing behavior (pages visited, searches made) to select ads displayed to you on websites other than Ola's own sites. This is linked to an anonymous cookie ID. Third-party advertisers on Ola's platforms may also set their own cookies and tracking technologies outside Ola's control.

> Excerpt: "We may use the information collected from you for targeted advertising... to select which advertisements should be displayed to a particular individual on websites other than our web site(s)."

### Children's Data Handling — Safe
This is a ride-booking app for adults. The privacy policy does not mention children, age limits, or COPPA/GDPR-K compliance.

### Network & Internet Activity — Caution
Ola collects your IP address, browser type, referring URL, and Device Identifier. The IP address is used for server diagnostics and to help identify your device. Standard network logging for a connected service.

### Device Info & Fingerprinting — Caution
Ola collects device manufacturer, model, operating system, browser type, preferred language, geolocation, and app engagement metrics. This is used for software updates, troubleshooting, and personalizing services. The policy does not explicitly state this is used for advertising fingerprinting.

### Health & Biometric Data — Safe
No health, medical, or biometric data is mentioned for ride bookings. The only health-related note is that Ola Foundation CSR activities may collect medical reports if you voluntarily request charitable services.

### Financial & Payment Data — Caution
Payment processing is handled by third-party providers. Ola does not claim to store raw credit card numbers. However, it does collect and retain transaction history permanently. For Ola Cars purchases, insurance and financial documents may also be collected.

### Data Deletion & User Rights — Risky
Users can request account cancellation by emailing support. However, Ola explicitly states it will retain Protected Information and Usage Information, including geo-location, trip history, and transaction history, indefinitely after account termination for legal obligations, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, and other business reasons.

> Excerpt: "Even after your account is terminated, we will retain your Protected Information and Usage Information (including geo-location, trip history, and transaction history) as needed to comply with our legal and regulatory obligations, resolve disputes... and for other business reason."

### Policy Change Notification — Caution
Ola states that any changes are effective immediately upon posting. For material changes, they will notify users by email or through a notice on the service prior to the change becoming effective. Non-material changes may not trigger individual notice.
