## Summary Card

### ZARA
**Risk Level:** Caution | **Score:** 65/100 | **Grade:** C

#### Red Flags
- **Shares data with parent company and marketers:** ZARA shares your personal information with its parent company Inditex, affiliate organisations, and third-party companies for their own marketing purposes. These third parties can also share your data with others.
- **Tracks your behaviour across platforms:** ZARA uses cookies and web beacons to track what you do on their websites and apps over time. They also let third parties collect information about you this way.
- **Data used for third-party interest-based ads:** Your information is shared with third parties who may use it to show you interest-based advertising, and these companies may further share your data with others.
- **No clear data deletion process mentioned:** The privacy policy does not explain how you can delete your account or request that your data be removed. This makes it hard to take back your information.

#### Green Flags
- **Marketing emails can be opted out:** You can stop receiving promotional emails by following the unsubscribe instructions in any marketing message ZARA sends you.
- **States it is not for children under 13:** ZARA says its platforms are meant for adults and that it does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
- **Browser cookie controls mentioned:** ZARA notes that you can control cookies and tracking tools through your browser settings, though blocking cookies may break some features.
- **Some mobile privacy controls suggested:** ZARA suggests you can turn off Bluetooth, GPS, and push notifications in your phone settings to limit some tracking.

#### Verdict
ZARA is a popular fast-fashion shopping app with typical e-commerce privacy practices. It collects contact details, payment information, device data, and browsing behaviour. The main concerns are its broad data-sharing practices with parent company Inditex, third-party marketing partners, and the use of cookies and web beacons for tracking. While you can opt out of marketing emails and control some tracking through browser settings, the default data-sharing footprint is wider than necessary for a shopping app.

**Concerns:**

**Policy excerpt:** *"We collect information passively. We use tracking tools like browser cookies and web beacons. We use these tools on our websites and in emails we send to you."*

**Concerns:**

### Children's Data Handling — Caution

**Concerns:**

ZARA collects your browser type, the sites you came from or go to after visiting, and device information about how you access their platforms.

**Policy excerpt:** *"We also collect information about the type of browser you are using. And, we may look at what site you came from or what site you go to when you leave us."*

ZARA collects your device type, browser, device identification number, app usage frequency, and where you downloaded the app from.

**Policy excerpt:** *"We collect the type of device you use to access our Platform... If you are using our apps, we may also collect your device identification number."*

**Concerns:**

### Financial & Payment Data — Caution
ZARA collects your debit or credit card number when you make a purchase. It is unclear whether this is processed directly by ZARA or passed to a payment processor.

**Policy excerpt:** *"We collect payment information. If you buy product we will collect the relevant data as your debit or credit card number."*

**Concerns:**

**Policy excerpt:** *"You can opt out of receiving our marketing emails... You can control cookies and tracking tools."*

**Concerns:**

**Policy excerpt:** *"From time to time, we may change our privacy policy. We will notify you of any material changes to our Policy as required by law. We will also post an updated copy on our Platforms."*
