## Summary

Bharat Matrimony is India's largest matrimonial app operated by Matrimony.com Ltd, a company that has been in business since 2000. The app collects extensive personal information required for matrimonial matchmaking, including photos, videos, name, date of birth, educational qualifications, government ID documents, and contact details.

The privacy policy is a basic template that lacks detail on many modern data practices. Key concerns include: data sharing with affiliates who may market to you; collection of government ID documents; policy changes without user notification; and extensive personal data exposure through public profiles.

Key positives: the company is well-established with physical branches across India; has a designated grievance officer as per Indian IT law; uses secure servers for payments; and provides EU users with data protection rights.

## Summary Card

**App:** Bharat Matrimony® - Shaadi App
**Overall Risk:** Caution — 62/100 (Grade C)

### Red Flags
- **Shares data with affiliates who may market to you.** Bharat Matrimony may share your personal information with its associates, affiliates, and subsidiaries. These companies may then send marketing messages to you.
- **Collects government ID documents for verification.** The app asks for identity proof documents that you voluntarily submit for verification purposes. This means sensitive government ID data is stored on their servers.
- **Privacy policy can change without any notice to you.** The policy explicitly states it may be changed from time to time without any notice to you. Changes are only updated on the privacy policy page.
- **Collects extensive personal and family information.** The app collects your photos, videos, name, date of birth, education, mailing address, phone number, and more. Much of this is displayed on your public profile.
- **Collects data from your social media accounts when you log in.** If you register using Facebook, Google, or LinkedIn, the app collects personal data you choose to allow through their APIs. The policy doesn't specify what data is accessed.

### Green Flags
- **Has a designated grievance officer as per Indian law.** Bharat Matrimony has appointed a Grievance Officer as required by Indian IT rules. You can contact them with privacy concerns.
- **Uses secure server for credit card transactions.** The policy states they use a secure server for credit card transactions to protect your payment information.
- **EU users have data protection rights.** If you're in the EU, you have rights to access, erase, and withdraw consent for your data. You can contact them to exercise these rights.
- **Well-established company with long track record.** Matrimony.com has been operating since 2000 and claims millions of successful marriages. They have physical branches across India.

### Verdict
Bharat Matrimony is a well-established Indian matrimonial service with a long track record and physical presence across India. However, be aware that it collects extensive personal and family information, may share your data with affiliated companies for marketing, and can change its privacy policy without notifying you. The privacy policy is basic and lacks transparency on modern data practices like camera permissions, specific cookie usage, and detailed retention periods. If you're looking for a traditional matrimonial service in India, this is a legitimate option, but be mindful of how much personal information you share on your public profile.
