## Summary Card

**App:** Grammarly: AI Keyboard & Notes
**Overall Risk:** Caution
**Score:** 59 / 100
**Grade:** C

### Red Flags
- **AI trains on your writing.** The app uses your emails, documents, and other writing to train its AI models, unless you manually turn this off in settings.
- **Your data fuels targeted ads.** The company turns your email address and phone number into special codes that advertising partners use to show you ads on other websites.
- **Data shared with many partners.** Your data is shared with advertising partners, marketplace apps, and other companies in the Superhuman corporate family.
- **Tracks your device in detail.** The app collects a detailed list of what other apps you have installed, your browser plugins, and other device information.
- **Everything typed goes to their servers.** Everything you type, upload, or dictate in the app is sent to company servers for processing.

### Green Flags
- **Writing not used for ads.** The company explicitly promises it does not use your actual writing or documents to show you advertisements.
- **You can opt out of AI training.** You can choose whether the app uses your content to train its AI models by adjusting a setting in your account.
- **Targeted ad opt-out available.** You can opt out of targeted advertising and cookie tracking through the "Your Privacy Choices" link.
- **Uses encryption and security.** The company uses encryption, secure networks, and limits access to protect your data.

### Verdict
Grammarly offers genuinely useful writing assistance, but the privacy trade-offs are significant. Your content is processed on remote servers and may be used to train AI unless you opt out. The company also engages in substantial behavioral tracking and targeted advertising using your personal information. If you value privacy, take the time to configure the opt-out settings and consider whether cloud-based keyboard access is worth the risk.

## Audit Notes

**Policy Source:** Scraped from Grammarly/Superhuman privacy policy (effective October 29, 2025). The policy is a unified Superhuman Platform Inc. privacy policy covering products formerly offered under the Grammarly, Coda, and Superhuman brands.

**Key Observations:**
- The company explicitly states it does **not** use user content (emails, documents, writing) for marketing or advertising purposes. This is a meaningful positive.
- AI training on user content is opt-out, not opt-in. Users must actively disable this in account settings.
- Targeted advertising involves hashing email addresses and phone numbers into unique identifiers shared with advertising partners. This is a significant privacy concern.
- The breadth of technical information collected (apps installed, browser plugins, etc.) is unusually extensive for a keyboard app.
- Data may be transferred to corporate family companies and in merger/sale scenarios.
- Strong European data protection framework compliance (Data Privacy Framework, VeraSafe representative).
