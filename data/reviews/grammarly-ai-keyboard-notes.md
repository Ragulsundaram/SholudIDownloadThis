# Privacy Review: Grammarly: AI Keyboard & Notes

**Review Date:** 2026-05-18  
**App:** Grammarly: AI Keyboard & Notes  
**Developer:** Grammarly, Inc (Superhuman Platform Inc.)  
**Policy URL:** https://www.grammarly.com/privacy-policy  
**Policy Last Updated:** October 29, 2025

---

## Summary Card

### Grammarly: AI Keyboard & Notes
**Risk Level: CAUTION | Score: 75/100 | Grade: B**

#### Red Flags
- **AI training enabled by default** — Everything you write is used to train Grammarly's AI models unless you manually turn this off in settings. This includes emails, documents, and any text you type.
- **Collects contacts and data about non-users** — If you give Grammarly access to your contacts, it uploads this information. They also collect information about people you email who don't use Grammarly.
- **Extensive device and app tracking** — Grammarly collects detailed technical information about your device including what apps you have installed, browser plugins, IP address, and uses this to build a profile about you.
- **Data shared with advertising partners** — While your actual writing isn't shared, identifiers like your email and phone number are converted to unique codes and shared with ad networks to show you Grammarly ads on other websites.

#### Green Flags
- **Your writing is never sold or monetized** — Grammarly explicitly states they never rent or sell your user content. Your words stay yours and aren't used to make money through selling data.
- **AI training can be disabled** — You can turn off AI training in your account settings, preventing your content from being used to improve their models.
- **Strong data deletion and export options** — You can request to see, download, or delete your personal data. Grammarly provides clear ways to exercise your privacy rights.
- **Writing never used for advertising** — Grammarly promises they do not use the content you write for marketing or advertising purposes. Only technical data is used for ads.
- **Enterprise accounts have separate data handling** — If you use Grammarly through work or school, your employer controls the data policy, and Grammarly processes content according to your organization's rules.

#### Verdict
Grammarly offers strong privacy protections and a clear commitment to never sell your writing. However, the app collects extensive data including all text you type, your contacts, device information, and uses this for AI training by default. While opt-outs are available, users should disable AI training immediately and be mindful of what they type. Best suited for professional writing where the productivity benefits outweigh the privacy tradeoffs.

## Category Analysis

### Camera & Microphone — Safe
Grammarly does not access your camera or microphone. The app works with text input only.

**Policy Excerpt:** Not mentioned in privacy policy.

---

### Location & GPS — Caution
Grammarly guesses your approximate location from your IP address to set your timezone, but doesn't use precise GPS tracking.

**Policy Excerpt:** *"We collect technical information about your device... including your IP address, device type... time zone setting and location..."*

**Concerns:**
- Location inferred from IP address
- Used for timezone and service optimization

---

### Contacts & Phonebook — Risky
If you allow access, Grammarly uploads your contacts to make spelling suggestions better. They also collect information about people you email who don't use Grammarly.

**Policy Excerpt:** *"Your user content may include information about others... if you choose to give our products access to your contacts so our editing products make appropriate spelling suggestions."*

**Concerns:**
- Contacts uploaded to servers
- Information about non-users collected
- Non-user data could potentially be retained

---

### Storage & File Access — Caution
Grammarly needs access to files and documents you choose to edit. Cloud storage is used to sync your documents across devices.

**Policy Excerpt:** *"User content: We receive the content you upload or enable our products to access, such as emails and drafts, text, documents, files..."*

**Concerns:**
- All user content uploaded to cloud servers
- Cloud-based document storage

---

### Data Sharing with 3rd Parties — Caution
Your data is shared with service providers who help run Grammarly (like cloud storage and AI), but your actual writing is never sold or shared with advertisers.

**Policy Excerpt:** *"We use trusted service providers to help power our products... We do not make user content available to these companies [advertising partners]."*

**Concerns:**
- Multiple service providers have access to data
- Advertising partners receive hashed identifiers
- Third-party marketplace integrations share data

---

### Account & Identity Data — Caution
You need an account with email, name, and potentially job information. Grammarly may infer things about you like your interests and writing style.

**Policy Excerpt:** *"Account information: When you create an individual or organizational account, we collect information such as your email address, password, name, email preferences, job titles, and phone numbers."*

**Concerns:**
- Profile inferences built from usage
- Information from other sources combined with account data
- Licensed data from third parties may be added

---

### Behavioural & Ad Tracking — Caution
Grammarly tracks what you click on their website and uses cookies to show you ads for Grammarly on other sites. You can opt out of this.

**Policy Excerpt:** *"We engage other companies to serve advertisements promoting our products... These companies may use Cookies to collect limited information about your interactions with our websites."*

**Concerns:**
- Cross-site tracking for advertising
- Email/phone hashed for ad targeting
- Cookie-based tracking

---

### Children's Data Handling — Caution
Grammarly says their service is for ages 13+, but there's no strong system in place to verify ages or protect children who might use it.

**Policy Excerpt:** *"We do not engage in sales, sharing, or targeted advertising using personal information about anyone we know to be under the age of 18."*

**Concerns:**
- Service features appealing to students
- No robust age verification
- Unclear COPPA compliance specifics

---

### Network & Internet Activity — Safe
Grammarly logs basic information like your IP address for security and to keep the service running, which is standard practice.

**Policy Excerpt:** *"We collect usage data about how you interact with us and our products, including your activity on our websites and apps... security logs..."*

**Concerns:**
- IP address logged and used for location inference

---

### Device Info & Fingerprinting — Caution
Grammarly collects detailed information about your device including what apps you have installed, which could be used to identify you across different services.

**Policy Excerpt:** *"Technical information: We collect technical information about your device and how you access our products. This includes your IP address, device type, browser type and version, apps installed on your device..."*

**Concerns:**
- List of installed apps collected
- Browser plugins catalogued
- Comprehensive device fingerprint possible

---

### Health & Biometric Data — Safe
Grammarly doesn't collect any health or biometric information.

---

### Financial & Payment Data — Safe
When you pay, Grammarly uses third-party processors and doesn't store your credit card details themselves.

**Policy Excerpt:** *"Payment information: If you make a purchase or payment with us, we collect information about the transaction, and our third-party payment processor will handle your payment information."*

---

### Data Deletion & User Rights — Safe
You have good control over your data. You can view, download, correct, or delete your information, and opt out of AI training and advertising.

**Policy Excerpt:** *"Depending on where you live, you may have rights under privacy laws to access, correct, or delete your personal data..."*

**Concerns:**
- Enterprise users must contact their organization for rights requests

---

### Policy Change Notification — Caution
Grammarly will email you about big privacy policy changes, but continued use of the app counts as accepting smaller updates.

**Policy Excerpt:** *"We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time by publishing an updated version. If we make material changes, we will provide you additional notice, such as by sending you an email."*

**Concerns:**
- Non-material changes may not be prominently notified
- Continued use constitutes acceptance

## Review Notes

- **Company Structure Change:** The privacy policy now covers "Superhuman Platform Inc. (formerly Grammarly)" and includes Coda and Superhuman Mail products. This represents a significant corporate restructuring.
- **AI Training Opt-Out:** Users should be directed to immediately disable AI training in account settings if privacy is a concern.
- **Enterprise vs Personal:** Strong distinction between enterprise (organization-controlled) and personal accounts. Enterprise users have different data handling.
- **Data Privacy Framework:** Company is certified under EU-U.S., UK, and Swiss Data Privacy Frameworks for international data transfers.
- **Non-User Data:** Policy acknowledges collection of information about people who don't use Grammarly (email recipients), though claims this is only used to provide the service.
