# Zomato — Privacy Review

**Date:** 2026-05-17
**Analyst:** claude-kimi-k2.5
**Policy:** https://www.zomato.com/policies/privacy/
**Policy Last Updated:** 2025-07-25

## Methodology
Re-analysis performed with strict legal-safe rules: every claim directly quoted, no strong verbs from weak evidence, no invented purposes, high-severity flags require explicit unambiguous backing.

## Key Findings

### Verdict
- **Score:** 58 / 100
- **Grade:** C
- **Risk:** Risky
- **Recommendation:** Think twice

### Red Flags (6)
1. **App may access files and metadata stored on your device** — The policy states applications may access metadata associated with files on your mobile device, including photos, audio, video clips, and address book contacts. (Severity: medium)
2. **Payment card information may be stored and shared with processors** — The policy says payment card details may be collected, stored, and shared with third-party payment processors for future transactions. (Severity: medium)
3. **Policy changes are accepted automatically if you keep using the app** — Continued use after any policy change is deemed acceptance, with no advance notice required. (Severity: high)
4. **Mobile device advertising identifiers cannot be deleted** — The policy explicitly states that unlike cookies, mobile device IDs cannot be deleted. (Severity: medium)
5. **Your data may be combined with information from third parties** — The policy says Zomato may obtain information from partners, marketers, third-party websites, and researchers and combine it with data collected directly from you. (Severity: medium)
6. **Data is shared with subsidiaries and affiliates** — The policy discloses sharing with subsidiaries and affiliates under Zomato Media Private Limited. (Severity: medium)

### Green Flags (2)
1. **Payment processors are PCI compliant** — Third-party payment providers are validated as PCI compliant.
2. **You can request account deletion** — Users can request deletion by contacting the Data Protection Officer.

### Category Changes vs. Prior Analysis

- **Contacts & Phonebook:** Downgraded from risky (-13.0) to caution (-6.0) because the 2020 policy does not support a mass contact-book upload feature; it only describes optional friend invitations and metadata access.
- **Storage & File Access:** Downgraded from risky (-6.5) to caution (-3.0) based on the actual 2020 text describing metadata access rather than broad continuous access.
- **Financial & Payment:** Downgraded from risky (-9.75) to caution (-4.5) because the 2020 policy describes standard payment processing without the stronger risk language in the prior analysis.
- **Data Sharing:** Kept risky (-19.5) with corrected quotes.
- **Account & Identity:** Kept risky (-9.75) with corrected quotes.
- **Policy Change Notification:** Kept risky (-3.25) with corrected quotes.
- **Health & Biometric:** Remains safe (0).
