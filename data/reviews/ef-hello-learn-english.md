# EF Hello: Learn English — Privacy Review

**App:** EF Hello: Learn English  
**Developer:** Signum International AG  
**Category:** Education / Language Learning  
**Analyzed:** 2026-05-18  
**Policy URL:** https://hello.ef.com/privacy-policy

## Summary Card

### EF Hello: Learn English
**Overall Risk:** Caution (C Grade) — Score: 61/100  
**Recommendation:** Think Twice

#### Red Flags
- **Data shared with many third parties for marketing:** Your personal data is shared with advertising platforms, corporate affiliates, business partners, social media companies, and IT providers. With your consent, it can also be shared with affiliates for their own marketing.
- **Extensive behavioral and marketing tracking:** The app uses cookies, pixel tags, and tools like Salesforce Marketing Cloud to track what you read, click, and forward. This data is used to send you targeted ads and tailor marketing messages based on your behavior.
- **Data kept for up to 5 years after you leave:** Your personal data is kept for 2-5 years after you close your account. Marketing data can be kept for up to 5 years after your last use, unless you explicitly withdraw consent.
- **Camera and microphone access not mentioned:** The privacy policy does not say whether the app accesses your camera or microphone. For a language learning app, this is a notable omission that makes it unclear what permissions the app requests.
- **Physical location is collected when enabled:** If you turn on location services, the app collects your physical location to provide personalized content and redirect you to local websites. Your location may also be collected during app crashes via Instabug.

#### Green Flags
- **Explicit no-sale policy under CCPA:** The privacy policy explicitly states that EF does not sell your personal information to third parties as defined under California privacy law.
- **User rights under GDPR and CCPA:** You can request access to your data, correct inaccurate information, ask for deletion, restrict processing, and receive a portable copy of your data. You can also object to direct marketing.
- **16+ age requirement with deletion promise:** The app requires users to be at least 16 years old. If EF discovers a user under 16, they promise to delete the data as soon as possible.
- **Marketing unsubscribe option available:** You can opt out of marketing communications using unsubscribe links in emails or by contacting efhello@ef.com.

#### Verdict
EF Hello collects extensive personal information including identity data, location, device fingerprints, and payment details. The app shares this data broadly with advertising platforms, corporate affiliates, and numerous third-party service providers for marketing purposes. While they provide some user rights and data deletion options, the extensive tracking and data sharing practices make this a concerning choice for privacy-conscious users. If you're looking for a language learning app with stronger privacy protections, consider alternatives that don't engage in extensive behavioral tracking and marketing data sharing.

## Detailed Analysis

### Data Collection Practices

EF Hello collects extensive personal data across multiple categories:

**Identity & Contact Information:**
- Name and contact details (postal address, email, phone number)
- Account information and user ID
- Username, profile picture, social media account IDs
- Country of residence/citizenship, birth date, gender

**Technical & Device Information:**
- IP address, MAC address
- Browser type and version
- Device ID, device model, screen resolution
- Operating system information
- Server log data, app usage statistics

**Behavioral Data:**
- Page views and website navigation
- App usage patterns and preferences
- Shopping habits and course interests
- Cookie and pixel tag data
- Marketing email engagement (opens, clicks, forwards)

**Financial Information:**
- Credit and debit card numbers
- Payment data
- Bank account details

**Location Data:**
- Physical location (when enabled by user)
- Location at time of app crashes

### Data Sharing Practices

The app shares data extensively with multiple categories of recipients:

**Corporate Affiliates:**
Local EF companies that promote services, provide customer service, and offer shared IT/legal services. With consent, data is also shared with affiliates for their own marketing purposes.

**Third Party Service Providers:**
- IT system providers for customer relations management
- Payment processing providers
- Digital marketing automation and analytics providers
- Advertising platforms
- Customer service, business analytics, and fraud prevention providers

**Business Partners:**
When promoting joint programs or using local sales agents, data is shared with third-party business partners.

**Social Media Platforms:**
Data is shared with Facebook, Instagram, Snapchat, Musically (TikTok), LinkedIn, Twitter, YouTube, WhatsApp, and Facebook Messenger when users connect their social media accounts.

**Other Sources:**
Data is also received from public databases, joint marketing partners, and from friends/connections on social media platforms.

### Tracking & Analytics

**Cookie-Based Tracking:**
The app uses cookies and pixel tags to track website activity, social media network members, and user behavior across sessions.

**Mobile App Tracking:**
- App usage data including date/time of server access
- Downloaded information and files
- Device number tracking
- Statistics on malfunctions and crashes

**Crash Reporting:**
Uses Instabug to collect information about bugs and crashes, including device ID, device model, time of failure, and physical location at time of crash.

**Email Marketing Analytics:**
Uses Salesforce Marketing Cloud to track email opens, clicks, and forwards to modify email messages based on recipient behavior.

### Marketing Practices

EF Hello engages in extensive marketing activities:

**Channels:**
- Email marketing (via Salesforce Marketing Cloud)
- SMS messaging
- WhatsApp and Facebook Messenger
- Chat bots
- Telephone calls (telemarketing)
- Traditional mail

**Personalization:**
Marketing is tailored based on user interests, past activities, and browsing behavior. The app analyzes usage trends and promotional campaign effectiveness to customize content.

**Consent Requirements:**
- Marketing requires user consent
- Users can opt out via unsubscribe links or by contacting efhello@ef.com
- Changes may not be effective immediately

### Data Retention

**Account Data:**
Personal data is retained for 2-5 years after account expiry to allow for reactivation.

**Marketing Data:**
Limited data for marketing is kept until consent is withdrawn, but no longer than 5 years after the last completed service.

**Legal Retention:**
Further retention may occur for compliance with legal obligations or for establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims.

### User Rights

EF Hello provides several user rights under GDPR and CCPA:

**Right to Access:**
Users can request copies of their personal data.

**Right to Correction:**
Users can request correction or completion of inaccurate or incomplete data.

**Right to Erasure:**
Users can request deletion of their personal data where required by law.

**Right to Restriction:**
Users can request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.

**Right to Portability:**
Users can request their data in a machine-readable format or transfer to another service.

**Right to Object:**
Users can object to processing based on legitimate interests and direct marketing.

**Right to Withdraw Consent:**
Where processing is based on consent, users can withdraw it at any time.

**California-Specific Rights:**
- Right to know what personal information is collected
- Right to know categories of sources and third parties shared with
- Right to request deletion (with exceptions)
- Right to data portability
- Right to non-discrimination for exercising rights

### Age Requirements

The app requires users to be at least 16 years old. The company states they are not responsible for age verification but sometimes perform verification checks. If they learn a user is under 16 and collected information without parental consent, they promise to delete the information.

### International Data Transfers

Personal data may be transferred to countries outside the user's own country, including countries outside the EEA/Switzerland. EF uses European Commission standard data protection clauses for international transfers.

### Security Measures

The policy mentions using "appropriate organizational, technical and administrative measures" to protect personal data against unauthorized processing and accidental loss, destruction, or damage.

## Comparison with Industry Standards

Compared to other language learning apps, EF Hello's privacy practices are more aggressive in terms of:
- **Marketing tracking:** Uses sophisticated tools (Salesforce Marketing Cloud) to track email behavior
- **Data sharing:** Broader sharing with advertising platforms and social media companies
- **Retention periods:** Longer retention (up to 5 years) compared to industry average
- **Multi-channel marketing:** Extensive use of messaging apps (WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger) for marketing

The app does provide good user rights compliance and explicit consent requirements, which are positive aspects. However, the extensive data collection and sharing practices significantly impact the privacy score.
