### Red Flags
- **Collects passport and identification information:** The app asks for your passport or other ID information when you book travel, which includes your name, address, gender, nationality, and date of birth.
- **Your booking details are shared with airlines, hotels, and travel agents:** When you book through Skyscanner, your name, contact details, payment info, passport data, and travel itinerary are sent to the airline, hotel, or travel agent you choose.
- **Tracks your activity and shares data with advertising partners:** The app uses cookies and works with partners like Facebook to track how you use the platform, then shows you targeted ads on other websites and apps based on your travel searches.
- **Builds a profile of your interests using machine learning:** The app combines data about your searches, bookings, and clicks to create a profile of your travel preferences, then uses this to personalize results and show you targeted travel deals.
- **Shares your IP address with car rental companies:** When you search for car rentals, your IP address may be shared with certain providers so they can show prices available in your region and detect fraud.

### Green Flags
- **Does not routinely collect sensitive health or religious data:** The company explicitly says it does not routinely collect special category data like health information or religious beliefs, unless you voluntarily provide it for specific travel needs.
- **Strong data rights with an easy-to-use request form:** You can request a copy of your data, ask for corrections, request deletion, or restrict how your data is used through a dedicated data privacy request page.
- **Security and privacy built into company culture:** The company trains all employees on data protection and privacy, uses data minimization techniques like anonymization, and implements technical security measures to keep your information safe.
- **You can manage cookie and marketing preferences:** You can choose which cookies to accept, opt out of marketing emails, and manage your privacy settings at any time through the app or website.

### Verdict
Skyscanner is a popular travel metasearch engine that compares prices across airlines, hotels, and car rental providers. It collects extensive personal data including passport information, payment details, and detailed usage behavior, then shares this with travel providers and advertising partners. While the privacy policy is transparent and user-friendly, and the company provides strong rights and security measures, the breadth of data collection and sharing warrants caution for privacy-conscious travelers.

## Detailed Analysis

### Data Collection Overview

Skyscanner collects personal data across multiple categories:

**Contact Information:** Name, address, email address, telephone number

**Identification Information:** Passport or other ID details including name, address, gender, nationality, date of birth

**Payment Information:** Payment card number, expiry date, verification code (tokenized storage for fraud detection)

**Travel and Booking Information:** Booking references, PNR, passport info, travel itinerary, booking history, passenger details

**Account Information:** Name, date of birth, gender, place of birth, identification info, payment info, loyalty scheme info

**Device and Location Information:** IP address, device make/model/OS, browser, non-specific location from IP, specific location if granted

**Usage Information:** Time spent on platform, searches performed, pages accessed, features used, links clicked, Travel Provider redirects

**Communications Information:** Content of emails and support requests, open rates, click-through rates

**User-Generated Content:** Comments, photos, videos, reviews, AI prompts

**Third-Party Login Information:** Data from Apple, Google, Facebook logins

**Profiling Information:** Combined data to create inferences about preferences and interests

### Data Sharing

The policy discloses sharing with multiple parties:

**Travel Providers:** Airlines, hotels, car rental companies, travel agents receive full booking data including contact details, payment info, passport data, and itinerary

**Third-Party Processors:**
- Payment processors (secure storage of card details)
- Email management and distribution tools
- Security and fraud prevention services
- Data aggregation and analytics software
- Tracking tools (to identify Travel Provider redirects)
- Customer service platforms (may use machine learning)
- Customer satisfaction and market research services
- Cloud storage and IT support services
- Other Skyscanner group companies (Brazil, China, India, Japan, Singapore, Spain, UK, US)

**Advertising Partners:** Facebook, affiliate networks, marketing services providers. Activities include creating "lookalike" audiences and custom audiences for targeted advertising.

**Fraud Prevention Partners:** Information shared for fraud detection and verification purposes

**Brand Collaborations:** Joint promotions and competitions

**Legal Disclosures:** Law enforcement, corporate transactions, legal claims

### Automated Decision-Making

The policy mentions automated decisions for fraud prevention:
- "For these purposes, we might make automated decisions about you which may result in us or the relevant Travel Provider blocking your booking or purchase."
- Users can contact Skyscanner to discuss or appeal automated decisions
- Machine learning algorithms are used for personalization (e.g., relevant car hire results based on driver age-range)
- Profiling activities "do not have legal or other significant effects on you"

### Cookies and Tracking

Cookies are used in three categories:
- **Functional:** Required for basic operation
- **Analytical:** Usage analytics for platform improvement
- **Marketing:** Personalized advertising, including on third-party sites

Users can manage cookie preferences, and consent is obtained where required.

### User Rights

Comprehensive rights are provided:
- **Access:** Request copy of personal data
- **Correction:** Correct inaccurate data
- **Deletion:** Request erasure in certain circumstances
- **Restriction:** Block or restrict processing
- **Portability:** Obtain data in structured, machine-readable format
- **Objection:** Object to processing in some circumstances
- **Withdrawal of Consent:** Withdraw consent at any time

Exercise methods: Skyscanner account settings, "Make a Data Privacy Request" page, or writing to the Data Protection Officer.

### Security Measures

- Security and privacy training for all employees
- Data minimization, anonymization, and pseudonymization techniques
- Technical, organizational, and administrative security measures
- Contractual safeguards for third-party processors
- Security review required for third-party processors
- Data stored in secure servers (Ireland, Frankfurt, Tokyo, Canada, Singapore)
- Standard Contractual Clauses for international transfers

### Policy Change Handling

"If the updates are significant, we'll notify you of these changes when you next access our services, or by other communications."

### Children's Data

- Platforms not intended for children under 18
- Data deleted if discovered to have been collected without parental authorization
