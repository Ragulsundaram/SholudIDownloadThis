## Summary Card

### Tiimo: To-Do List & AI Planner
**Overall Risk:** Caution  
**Score:** 86/100 (Grade A)

#### Red Flags
- **Marketing cookies for targeted ads**: The app uses cookies from YouTube, Google, and Facebook to track you across websites and show targeted ads. You can opt out, but this tracking is enabled by default.
- **Analytics tracking across services**: Your app usage is tracked by Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how you interact with the service. This is used to improve the app but does involve sharing data with these third parties.

#### Green Flags
- **Explicit no-selling policy**: Tiimo clearly states they do not sell your personal data to anyone. This is a strong privacy commitment.
- **Full GDPR rights available**: As a Danish company, Tiimo fully supports GDPR. You can access, correct, delete, export, or restrict processing of your data at any time.
- **Strong children's privacy safeguards**: Accounts for children under 13 must be created by a parent or guardian. Tiimo does not knowingly collect data from children without consent.
- **Limited data retention period**: Your data is kept only while you have an active subscription, plus up to 2 years afterward. After that, it's deleted.

#### Verdict
Tiimo is a privacy-respecting productivity app from a Danish company with strong GDPR compliance. They explicitly commit to not selling your data and provide comprehensive user rights. The main concerns are standard marketing and analytics cookies, which you can opt out of. For a subscription-based productivity app, this is above-average privacy protection.
