## Summary Card

**App:** Tiimo: To-Do List & AI Planner  
**Overall Risk:** Caution (Score: 61 / Grade: C)  

### Red Flags

- **Marketing cookies for targeted ads:** Tiimo uses cookies from Google, YouTube, and Facebook to track you across websites and show you targeted ads. This means your browsing activity on their site is being used to build an advertising profile.
- **Data may leave the EU:** Your data might be processed in countries outside the EU/EEA. Tiimo says they use legal safeguards called Standard Contractual Clauses, but your info is still moving across borders.
- **Keeps your data for 2 years after you quit:** Even after you cancel your subscription, Tiimo keeps your personal data for up to two years in case you come back. After that, they say they delete it.
- **Continued use counts as accepting policy changes:** If Tiimo updates their privacy policy, they will notify you, but just continuing to use the app means you automatically agree to the new terms. You don't have to actively re-accept.

### Green Flags

- **Does not sell your data:** Tiimo explicitly states that they do not sell your personal data to anyone. This is a strong positive commitment.
- **Full GDPR rights available:** You can access, correct, delete, restrict, port, or object to the processing of your data. You can also withdraw consent at any time by emailing them.
- **Strong children's privacy protections:** If a child under 13 uses the app, a parent or guardian must create the account and give consent. Tiimo promises to delete any data collected from children without parental consent.
- **Payments handled by third parties:** Tiimo does not store your full payment details. All payment processing is handled securely by trusted third-party providers like the App Store and Google Play.

### Verdict

Tiimo is a well-intentioned GDPR-compliant planner that explicitly refuses to sell your data and gives you real rights over your information. However, its cookie-heavy website uses marketing trackers from Google, YouTube, and Facebook for targeted ads, and it hangs onto your data for two years after you leave. If you're sensitive to ad tracking, it's worth thinking twice before signing up.
