## Full Analysis

### What it does
Wingo is a simplified daily calendar and visual planner app designed for kids, especially children with special needs, cognitive issues, and developmental disorders. It allows parents and children to create visual timelines of activities showing yesterday, today, and tomorrow's events, with alarms for upcoming activities. It also includes a family activity tracking section and positive affirmation audio.

### Data collected
- **Personal Data:** Name (during registration), potentially other details
- **Usage Data:** App usage information (implied but not detailed)
- **Non-PII Visitor Information:** May be collected for analytics and shared for marketing

### Who it is shared with
- **Hosting partners and service providers** — To operate the website/app, with confidentiality agreements
- **Third parties** — Non-personally identifiable visitor information may be provided for marketing, advertising, or other uses
- **Gateway provider** — For payment processing (Apple In-App Purchases)

### COPPA Compliance
The policy includes a COPPA section with some positive elements:
- Claims not to let third-parties collect PII from children under 13
- Claims not to monetize child-targeted products with ads
- Mentions human review of any future ads for kid-suitability
- States parents can review, delete, manage, or refuse data sharing by contacting the developer
- For kid-focused apps, parents are prompted to explicitly allow or deny collection of usability data

However, the statement "We market to, but do not collect information from children under 13" directly contradicts the app's purpose and registration flow.

### Retention
Not explicitly addressed in the privacy policy.

### User Rights
- Parents can contact the developer to review, delete, manage, or refuse data sharing for their child
- Users can change personal information by logging into their account
- No self-service data deletion or automated export mentioned
- No response timeframe specified
