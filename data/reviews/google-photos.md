## Summary

Google Photos is a cloud backup and photo organization service from Google. It automatically backs up your entire photo library and uses AI to organize, search, and group your photos. While it offers powerful features like face grouping and object search, using it means giving Google access to every photo and video you take. Google scans your visual content with AI, uses your activity for advertising across all Google services, and stores face grouping data on its servers.

Key concerns: all photos are scanned by AI for face grouping and object recognition, your photo activity is used to build an advertising profile across Google services, location metadata from photos is extracted and used for ads, and face geometry data is stored on Google's servers.

Key positives: encryption in transit and at rest, your photos remain your property, comprehensive privacy controls and sharing settings, and the ability to download or delete your data through Google Takeout and Account settings.

## Methodology

This review was conducted by analyzing the Google Privacy Policy, accessed via the staged policy text on 2026-05-13.

### Data Collection Observed

- **Photos & Videos:** All uploaded photos and videos stored on Google servers and scanned by AI
- **Face Geometry:** Face grouping data analyzed and stored on Google servers
- **Location:** GPS metadata from photos, IP-based location, device sensor data
- **Identity:** Name, email, phone (Google Account required)
- **Device:** Unique identifiers, browser type, device type, OS, carrier, app version
- **Activity:** Photo views, album creation, searches, sharing activity, content interactions
- **Network:** IP address, referrer URL, crash reports, system activity
- **Contacts:** Optional access for sharing photos with friends

### Notable Excerpts

> "We also collect the content you create, upload, or receive from others when using our services. This includes things like email you write and receive, photos and videos you save, docs and spreadsheets you create."

> "Photos and videos you upload to Google Photos are used to help you create albums, collages, and other creations that you can share."

> "Google processes information to provide advertising, including online identifiers, browsing and search activity, and information about your location and interactions with advertisements."

> "The information we collect includes unique identifiers, browser type and settings, device type and settings, operating system, mobile network information including carrier name and phone number, and application version number."
