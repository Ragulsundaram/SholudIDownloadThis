# Google Photos Review

**Analyzed:** 2026-05-16  
**Policy:** http://www.google.co.uk/policies/privacy  
**Policy Updated:** 2026-04-02  
**Score:** 64 / Grade: C / Risk: Caution  

## Red Flags

- **Google uses automated systems to analyze your uploads** — Google's privacy policy states that it uses automated systems that analyze your content to provide features tailored to how you use its services. It also says photos and videos you upload to Google Photos are used to help you create albums and other creations.
- **Your Photos activity is linked to your Google Account** — Google's privacy policy states that when you are signed in, information is collected and stored with your Google Account and treated as personal information.
- **Location information is collected when you use Google services** — Google's privacy policy states that it collects location information when you use its services, including for ads and search results. It does not clearly explain whether Google Photos specifically extracts location from your photo metadata.
- **Your activity across Google services may be used for ads** — Google's privacy policy states that depending on your settings, it may show you personalized ads based on your interests and activity across Google services. However, the policy explicitly states that Google does not show personalized ads based on your content from Photos.

## Green Flags

- **Encryption protects data in transit** — Google's privacy policy states that it uses encryption to keep your data private while in transit.
- **You can delete your Photos content and account** — Google's privacy policy states that you can delete your content from specific Google services, delete specific Google products, or delete your entire Google Account. You can also export a copy of your content.
- **Google does not use your Photos content for personalized ads** — Google's privacy policy explicitly states that it does not show you personalized ads based on your content from Photos.

## Verdict

Google Photos is convenient for cloud backup, but Google analyzes your uploaded content with automated systems and links your activity to your Google Account. The good news is that Google explicitly says it does not use your Photos content for personalized ads. However, your general activity across Google services may still be used for ads depending on your settings. If privacy matters, consider Apple Photos with iCloud instead.
