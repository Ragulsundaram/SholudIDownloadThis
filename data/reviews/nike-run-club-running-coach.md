## Summary

Nike Run Club's privacy policy reveals extensive collection of Workout Info including GPS location, heart rate, weight, height, body measurements, and fitness activity data. Location data is required for core features like run route logging. The app shares personal data with Nike entities, service providers, and third-party advertising providers — including sharing your email address or phone number with advertising partners to create custom audiences for personalized ads across the internet and social media. Data is stored in the USA and other countries. On the positive side, Nike uses encryption and authentication tools, provides a dedicated 'My Workout Info' setting to opt out of personalization, deletes inactive member profiles permanently after five years, and obtains express consent for Workout Info processing in certain jurisdictions. The app also allows using default height and weight values instead of real ones.

Key concerns: extensive health and fitness data collection, GPS required for core features, email/phone shared with advertising partners, international data transfers to USA, data may be transferred in business sale.

Key positives: My Workout Info opt-out setting, default values for height/weight, inactive profiles deleted after 5 years, encryption used, material changes get prior notice, direct marketing opt-out available.

## Summary Card

### Nike Run Club: Running Coach
- **Overall Risk:** Caution
- **Score:** 58/100 (Grade C)

**Red Flags:**
- **Extensive workout and health data collected.** Nike Run Club collects your Workout Info including heart rate, GPS location, weight, height, body measurements, accelerometer data, calories burned, pace, stride, and fitness activity data.
- **Precise GPS location required for core features.** The app requests access to your phone's location data to log your run route, which is a core feature of the running experience.
- **Email and phone shared with advertising partners.** Nike shares certain personal identifiers such as your email address or phone number with advertising partners to create custom audiences and personalize advertising across the internet and social media.
- **Data transferred to the USA and other countries.** The personal data Nike collects is stored in the USA and other countries. Some data recipients may be located in countries that do not provide the same level of data protection.
- **Data may be transferred in a business sale.** Nike may transfer your personal data in the event it sells or transfers all or a portion of its business or assets, including in a reorganization, spin-off, dissolution, or liquidation.

**Green Flags:**
- **My Workout Info setting to opt out of personalization.** Nike provides a 'My Workout Info' setting that lets you control whether your workout data is analyzed to personalize content, training programs, offers, and recommendations.
- **Can use default height and weight instead of real ones.** If you do not want to provide your actual height and weight, Nike allows you to use default values instead, letting you use core features while keeping your real measurements private.
- **Inactive profiles deleted after five years.** Nike permanently deletes inactive member profiles and personal data after five years. Your profile is considered active if you have logged in within the last two years.
- **Encryption and authentication tools used.** Nike states it uses a variety of technical and organizational security measures, including encryption and authentication tools, to maintain the safety of your personal data.
- **Material policy changes get prior notice.** If Nike materially changes the way it processes your personal data, it will provide prior notice, or request your consent where legally required, before implementing such changes.
- **Direct marketing opt-out available.** You can opt out of Nike's marketing communications by modifying preferences in your account settings, clicking unsubscribe links in messages, or contacting Nike directly.

**Verdict:** Nike Run Club collects extensive workout and health data including GPS, heart rate, and body measurements, shares data with advertising partners using your email and phone, and transfers data internationally, but offers strong privacy controls and deletes inactive accounts after five years. If you are concerned about extensive health and fitness data collection, sharing of personal identifiers with advertising partners, and international data transfers to the US, review your privacy settings before using the app.
