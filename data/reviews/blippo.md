## Summary Card

**App:** Blippo+  
**Overall Risk:** Safe (Score: 90/100, Grade: A)

### Red Flags
- **Support emails kept indefinitely:** If you email Blippo+ for help, they keep that email forever unless you specifically ask them to delete it. They use it to remember past conversations and spot trends in support questions.
- **Server logs collect your IP and device info:** When you use the Blippo+ website or when the game talks to their servers, they save your IP address, what you requested, and what software you're using. They keep these logs for two weeks.
- **No promise to notify you of policy changes:** The privacy policy may change at any time, and the developer does not say they will email you or alert you when it does.

### Green Flags
- **Explicitly says your data is "none of our business":** The privacy policy opens with a clear promise: they make money selling games, not mining your personal information. They prefer not to collect data at all.
- **No ads and no behavioural tracking:** Blippo+ does not show ads inside the app and does not track your behaviour across other apps or websites to build an advertising profile.
- **Honors GDPR and CCPA deletion rights:** If you live in the EU or California, you can ask Blippo+ to show you what data they have or delete it. They provide direct email contacts for these requests.
- **Server logs automatically deleted after two weeks:** Any metadata they collect from your web requests is only kept for two weeks before being removed. This is much shorter than most apps.
- **Privacy policy is short and easy to read:** Unlike many privacy policies that are dozens of pages long and full of legal jargon, Blippo+'s policy is just a few short sections written in plain English.

### Verdict
Blippo+ is a rare example of a paid game with a genuinely minimal data footprint. Panic, Inc. has a long reputation for ethical software development, and the privacy policy backs it up with plain-language promises and very limited collection. If you are looking for a creative, offline-friendly game and value your privacy, Blippo+ is an excellent choice.
