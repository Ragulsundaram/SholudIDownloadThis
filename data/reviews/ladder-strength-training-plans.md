## Summary

Ladder's privacy policy reveals extensive data collection typical of a fitness coaching app. It gathers your workout metrics, nutrition logs, and health data from Apple Health, along with granular browsing behavior including mouse movements, scrolling, clicks, and keystrokes. The app shares data with advertising networks and social networks for targeted advertising, which may qualify as a "sale" under U.S. state privacy laws. User-generated content like workout selfies can be reshared by other users. On the positive side, Ladder does not retain payment card numbers directly, provides multiple opt-out mechanisms for targeted ads, and outlines detailed privacy rights for U.S. state residents.

Key concerns: granular behavioral tracking down to mouse movements and keystrokes, health data shared with advertising partners, user content can be redistributed by others, call recordings stored, data may be transferred in a business transaction.

Key positives: does not retain payment card numbers, multiple opt-out options for targeted advertising, detailed U.S. state privacy rights outlined, not directed at children under 18, email unsubscribe available.

## Summary Card

### LADDER Strength Training Plans
- **Overall Risk:** Caution
- **Score:** 56/100 (Grade C)

**Red Flags:**
- **Tracks mouse movements, clicks, and keystrokes.** Ladder uses third-party technologies that record your mouse movements, scrolling, clicks, and keystroke activity on their services to analyze your browsing behavior.
- **Shares data for targeted advertising.** Ladder shares your personal data with advertising networks and social networks to deliver targeted advertisements. Under U.S. state privacy laws, this may qualify as a "sale" of personal data.
- **User content can be reshared by others.** When you post content on Ladder's services, other users who view it can reshare or redistribute it both on and off the platform, and may save or copy it outside the service.
- **Health data shared with integrated apps and advertisers.** Ladder collects workout and health data including heart rate, calories burned, and weight, and shares data with integrated third-party apps and advertising partners.
- **Call recordings stored.** If you contact Ladder support by phone, your calls may be recorded and stored for training and quality purposes where permitted by law.

**Green Flags:**
- **Does not retain payment card numbers.** Ladder uses third-party payment providers to process payments and does not retain any personally identifiable financial information such as payment card numbers.
- **Multiple opt-out options for targeted ads.** Ladder provides multiple ways to opt out of targeted advertising, including device-specific settings, Digital Advertising Alliance, and platform-specific opt-outs.
- **Detailed U.S. state privacy rights.** Ladder outlines detailed privacy rights for residents of various U.S. states including California, Nevada, and others, with instructions on how to exercise them.
- **Not directed at children under 18.** Ladder states its services are not directed to children under 18 and will promptly delete personal data if learned to have been provided by a child.
- **Can unsubscribe from marketing emails.** You can stop receiving promotional emails by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email communication.

**Verdict:** Ladder collects extensive workout and health data, tracks your browsing behavior down to mouse movements and keystrokes, and shares data with advertising networks for targeted ads, but provides detailed opt-out controls. If you are concerned about granular behavioral tracking, health data sharing with advertisers, and the possibility of your workout content being redistributed by other users, consider the trade-off before installing.
