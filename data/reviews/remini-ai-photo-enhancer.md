## Verdict
- **Score:** 65 / Grade: C / Risk: caution
- **Recommendation:** think_twice

## Red Flags (5)
1. **Your photos and face data are uploaded to external servers** (high)
2. **Face data is shared with third-party AI providers** (high)
3. **App profiles you to measure ad effectiveness without ad consent** (medium) — Profiling for ad campaign measurement is done under "legitimate interest," not consent.
4. **Targeted ads use third-party trackers with your consent** (medium)
5. **Your data is kept for up to 2 years, and some for up to 10 years** (medium) — 10-year retention applies to legal defense/corporate transactions.

## Removed Flag
- **Removed `red_no_explicit_policy_change_notification`:** The privacy policy explicitly states: "We'll notify users of any modification, integration, or update in accordance with applicable privacy laws."

## Green Flags (5)
1. **Remini explicitly promises not to sell your images or data**
2. **Enhanced images are deleted automatically after 1 day**
3. **You keep ownership of your images and videos**
4. **You can exercise privacy rights directly inside the app**
5. **Your images are not used to train AI unless you explicitly agree**

## Key Changes in This Re-analysis
- **Upgraded `policy_change_notification` from unknown to caution:** Policy explicitly commits to notifying users of changes under applicable privacy laws. Deduction reduced from 2.5 to 1.5.
- **Fixed `network_internet_activity` plain_english:** Removed unsupported claim that network logs are used "to build your user profile for service improvement and ad measurement." The policy states network activity is used for troubleshooting and quality assurance; it does not clearly say network logs are used for ads.
- **Fixed `financial_payment` plain_english:** Removed unsupported claim that "Apple processes all payments, so the app itself never sees your credit card details." The policy mentions "Information about your subscription" and "in-app purchases" but does not explain payment processing.
- **Updated `policy_change_notification` concerns:** Removed outdated concerns; added "Does not specify notification method" and "Does not specify advance notice period."

## Scoring
`policy_change_notification` deduction changed from 2.5 to 1.5. Total deductions: 72.0 (was 73.0). Score increased from 64 to 65. Grade remains C.
