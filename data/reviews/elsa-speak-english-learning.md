# ELSA Speak - English Learning — Privacy Review

**Review Date:** 2026-05-18  
**App:** ELSA Speak - English Learning  
**Developer:** ELSA, Corp.  
**Platform:** iOS  
**Policy URL:** https://elsaspeak.com/en/privacy/

---

## Summary Card

**Overall Rating: D (48/100) — Caution**

**Red Flags:**
- **Voice data shared with advertisers** — ELSA records your voice to check pronunciation, but they also share personal information with advertising networks and marketing companies
- **Admits to "selling" your data** — The privacy policy explicitly acknowledges selling/sharing personal information under California privacy laws for targeted advertising
- **Many third-party recipients** — Data is shared with marketing services, social networks, content enrichment providers, and advertising networks
- **International data storage** — Personal data is stored across multiple countries including US, Europe, Japan, India, Vietnam, and Singapore
- **No age verification** — Claims to be 16+ but relies entirely on user self-declaration without any verification

**Green Flags:**
- **Provides data rights (GDPR/CCPA)** — You can request access, correction, deletion, and export of your data via privacy@elsanow.io
- **Clear data retention policy** — Explains how long data is kept and allows deletion requests
- **Core feature requires voice** — Recording your voice is genuinely necessary for pronunciation training

**Verdict:**  
ELSA Speak offers genuine educational value for English pronunciation improvement, but users should be aware that their voice data and personal information are extensively shared with advertising and marketing partners. The app explicitly acknowledges "selling" data under California privacy laws. While they provide good data rights and deletion options, the extent of data sharing for an educational app is concerning. Consider this carefully if you're sensitive about your voice data being used for advertising profiles.

## Review Notes

### Data Retention Policy
ELSA provides a reasonably clear data retention explanation:

> *"We retain personal data for as long as we deem it as reasonably necessary in order to maintain and expand our relationship and provide you with our Services; in order to comply with our legal and contractual obligations; or to protect ourselves from any potential disputes... Please note that except as required by applicable law or our specific agreements with you, we will not be obligated to retain your personal data for any particular period, and we are free to securely delete or anonymize it for any reason and at any time, with or without notice to you."*

### International Data Transfers
Data is stored across multiple jurisdictions:

> *"ELSA currently uses AWS's cloud storage service in Ireland, the US, Japan and Singapore to store personal data."*

Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) are used for transfers outside adequate jurisdictions.

### Research Sharing
Limited data sharing with researchers:

> *"We may also share limited pseudonymized personal data with approved not-for-profit researchers, for statistical research purposes."*

Users can opt out of this processing.

## Conclusion

ELSA Speak is a functional English pronunciation app that legitimately requires voice access for its core feature. However, the extent of data sharing with advertising networks and marketing services is excessive for an educational app. The explicit acknowledgment of "selling" data under CCPA is notable.

**Recommendation:** Suitable for users who prioritize the educational value over privacy concerns, but privacy-conscious users should consider alternatives or use the free tier without creating detailed profiles. Users should email privacy@elsanow.io to opt out of data sales and targeted advertising.
