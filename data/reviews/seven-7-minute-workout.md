## Summary Card

**App:** Seven: 7 Minute Workout  
**Overall Risk:** Caution  
**Score:** 78/100  
**Grade:** B

### Red Flags
- **Your usage is tracked to promote the app on other websites** — Seven and its service providers collect information about how you use the app over time so they can show you promotional content for Seven on other websites and services you visit.
- **Health and workout data stored on company servers** — With your consent, Seven stores sensitive health information like your weight, height, heart rate, and workout history on its servers in Germany.
- **Some data kept after you delete your account** — Even after you delete your account, Seven may keep some of your information for up to 30 days to comply with legal requirements, prevent fraud, or resolve disputes.
- **Your data could be transferred in a future sale or merger** — If Perigee sells the company, merges with another business, or partners with someone else, your user information may be included as part of the transferred assets.

### Green Flags
- **Health data only collected with your clear consent** — Seven asks for your permission before collecting any health information like weight, height, or heart rate. You can withdraw this consent at any time.
- **You can fully delete your account and all activities** — Seven lets you delete your account directly from the app, which removes your profile, all workouts, comments, and leaderboard placements permanently.
- **You can download a copy of all your personal data** — Seven gives you the option to download all the personal information they have about you, including every workout you've ever completed.
- **You can hide your profile and activities from other users** — Seven has privacy settings that let you make your profile private so other users cannot see your workouts or progress in the app.
- **Marketing emails are opt-in only** — Seven only sends you marketing emails if you explicitly agree to receive them, and you can unsubscribe at any time with clear instructions.

### Verdict
Seven is a solid workout app that handles health data responsibly through explicit consent and offers strong user controls including full account deletion and data export. However, it shares usage data with third-party service providers for analytics and cross-service promotion, and some data is retained even after account deletion. It's a reasonable choice for fitness tracking, but privacy-conscious users should review the settings to make their profile private and limit data sharing.
