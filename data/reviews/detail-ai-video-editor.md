## Summary Card

**Overall Risk:** Caution  
**Score:** 64 / 100 (Grade C)

### Red Flags
- **Privacy policy is 5 years old.** Last updated July 2021. Since then, the app has added AI Auto-Edit, AI Director, auto-captions in 50+ languages, green screen AI, and transcription — none of which are mentioned in the policy.
- **Tracks your usage for marketing across other websites.** Detail hires service providers to monitor how you use the app over time so they can promote Detail and show "relevant" ads on other websites and services.
- **Your data could be transferred in a sale or merger.** If Detail is acquired, goes through a reorganization, or engages advisors for a potential transaction, your personal information may be shared with buyers, affiliates, or their advisors.
- **Stores your video and audio recordings on its servers.** When you record or upload content, your raw and edited video/audio files are stored on Detail's cloud servers for cross-device access and sharing.
- **Can upload your contacts.** If you connect your Facebook, Google, or Apple contacts, Detail copies and stores that contact information on its servers.

### Green Flags
- **No credit card storage.** Uses PCI-compliant third-party payment processors; Detail never sees or stores your payment details.
- **Strong GDPR rights.** As a Netherlands-based company, Detail offers full European privacy rights: access, rectification, deletion, restriction, objection, and data portability.
- **Encryption in transit and at rest.** Uses SSL for data in transit and encrypts collected information on secure servers.
- **You control social-login sharing.** When signing in via Facebook, Google, or Apple, you decide what information each platform shares with Detail.

### Verdict
Detail is a capable AI video editor with some genuinely useful features for creators, but its privacy practices are a mixed bag. The outdated policy, cloud storage of recordings, cross-site marketing tracking, and broad third-party sharing warrant caution. If you need a video editor and don't mind your recordings living on company servers, it's usable — but privacy-conscious creators should consider whether the AI conveniences are worth the data trade-offs.

## Review Notes

- The privacy policy was last updated on **July 15, 2021**. The current app version (5.5.20 as of May 2026) advertises AI Auto-Edit, AI Director, auto-captions in 50+ languages, transcription, green screen AI, and livestreaming. **None of these AI/ML data processing practices are disclosed in the policy.** This is a significant gap that warrants `needs_review: true`.
- The policy describes the app as a "video communications" and "event organization" tool, which suggests it was written for an earlier, narrower version of the product. The current marketing focuses heavily on content creation, social media publishing, and AI editing.
- Third-party sharing is broad: service providers may collect usage data over time for cross-site promotion, and affiliates/acquirors may receive user data in business transactions.
- Children's data is a notable concern: the app is rated 4+ on the App Store, yet the policy contains no age requirements, COPPA references, or child-specific safeguards.
- On the positive side, the company is GDPR-compliant, headquartered in Amsterdam, offers genuine user rights, and does not store payment card data.
