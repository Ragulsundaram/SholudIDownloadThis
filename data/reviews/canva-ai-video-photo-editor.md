## Summary Card

**App:** Canva: AI Video & Photo Editor
**Overall Risk:** Caution
**Score:** 56 / 100 (Grade: C)

### Red Flags
- **Your email and location are shared with ad networks for matching.** Canva shares your email address, location, and cookie data with advertising partners like Facebook, Google, and LiveRamp so they can match it with other data about you and show you personalized ads.
- **Extensive tracking for personalized ads across websites.** Canva uses cookies, device advertising IDs, and web beacons to track what you do on its app and website. It then uses this information to show you targeted ads on other sites like Facebook and Google.
- **Your uploaded designs and images are used to train AI models.** Canva analyzes your uploaded content, including designs and images, to train its algorithms and AI products. You can opt out of this in your privacy settings, but it is turned on by default.
- **Your device advertising ID is collected and used for targeted ads.** Canva collects your device's advertising identifier (like the iOS Advertising Identifier) to track how you use the app and deliver personalized advertisements.
- **Your data could be sold or transferred if Canva is acquired.** Canva says that if the company is sold, merged, or reorganized, your personal information and uploaded content may be shared or transferred as part of that deal.

### Green Flags
- **You can opt out of AI training, ad tracking, and marketing emails.** Canva gives you privacy settings where you can opt out of having your data used for AI training, third-party data enrichment, and marketing messages.
- **Canva Education is ad-free and COPPA/FERPA compliant for students.** Canva's separate Education product does not show ads to students and has been certified as compliant with child privacy laws like COPPA and FERPA in the United States.
- **You can request to access, correct, or delete your data.** Canva allows you to contact them to request access to your data, corrections, or deletion. Users in certain regions also have additional rights like data export and objection.
- **Your designs are private by default with granular sharing controls.** Designs you create in Canva are set to private by default. You can choose who can see or edit them, and there are multiple sharing options from invite-only to link-based access.

### Verdict
Canva is a powerful design app, but it shares lots of your data with advertisers and uses your uploads to train AI. If you use it, take advantage of the privacy settings to opt out of AI training and personalized ads.

## Review Notes

Camera and microphone access is not explicitly addressed in the privacy policy, despite the app being a photo and video editor. Please verify if the app requests these permissions.
