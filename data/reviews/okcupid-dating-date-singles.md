## Summary Card

- **App:** OkCupid Dating: Date Singles
- **Overall Risk:** Caution
- **Score:** 57 / 100
- **Grade:** C

### Red Flags
- **Sensitive profile data is public by default.** OkCupid collects and displays highly personal information like your sexual orientation, political views, and racial or ethnic origins on your public profile. Anyone using the app can see this.
- **Data shared across all Match Group dating apps.** Your OkCupid data flows to other Match Group platforms like Tinder, Hinge, and Match.com. If you get banned on one app, they can ban you on all of them. They also use your data to recommend matches across platforms.
- **Face geometry collected for photo verification.** If you use the photo verification feature, OkCupid scans and stores the shape of your face — what's called "face geometry data" — which is considered biometric data in some places.
- **Government ID may be requested.** OkCupid may ask you to upload a copy of your government-issued ID to prove you are who you say you are.
- **Data shared with advertising partners for targeting.** OkCupid shares your profile info, usage patterns, and device identifiers with advertising partners to show you targeted ads both on the app and on other websites and apps.
- **AI analyzes your behavior and content.** OkCupid uses machine learning and artificial intelligence to analyze how you use the app, read your customer support conversations, and build recommendation systems to suggest matches.
- **Data kept for years after you leave.** Even after you close your account, OkCupid keeps some of your data for up to 10 years for legal reasons. Banned account data is kept for 2 years.

### Green Flags
- **Notified before major policy changes.** OkCupid promises to tell you in advance before they make important changes to the privacy policy, giving you time to review.
- **Can download your data and close account.** You can request a copy of all your personal data and close your account at any time through the app settings.
- **Advertising and consent controls available.** You can opt out of certain data processing, withdraw consent, and restrict how your data is used for advertising in your account settings.
- **Strictly for adults 18+.** OkCupid does not allow anyone under 18 on the platform and has reporting tools to flag underage users.

### Verdict
OkCupid collects extensive personal data including your sexual orientation, political views, racial/ethnic origins, photos, chats, and precise location. Your profile is public by default, and your data flows across all Match Group platforms (Tinder, Hinge, Match.com, etc.). The app uses your face geometry for photo verification, may request government ID, and shares data with advertisers for targeted ads. On the positive side, OkCupid promises advance notice before material policy changes and provides tools to download, correct, or delete your data.
