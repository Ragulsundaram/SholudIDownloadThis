# Privacy Review — AJIO Online Shopping App

**App:** AJIO Online Shopping App  
**Developer:** Reliance Retail Limited  
**Analyzed:** 2026-05-18  
**Policy URL:** https://www.ajio.com/privacypolicy

---

## Summary Card

**Overall Risk:** Risky  
**Score:** 53 / 100  
**Grade:** D

### Red Flags
- **Broad data sharing with affiliates and ad networks** — AJIO shares your name, contact details, purchase history, shopping preferences, and browsing behavior with its parent company Reliance, other platforms it runs, and third-party advertisers to show you personalized offers and ads.
- **Background location tracking for marketing** — The app can collect your precise location even when it is closed or you are not using it, sending store updates and exclusive deals based on where you are. You can disable this in your phone settings, but it is enabled by default if you consent.
- **Behavioral tracking across services** — AJIO tracks what you browse and buy to recommend products and serve targeted ads. It also allows outside advertising companies to place tracking cookies on your device.
- **Data may transfer if company is sold** — If AJIO or Reliance sells part or all of its business, your personal information can be transferred to the new owner. You only get a chance to opt out if the new owner plans to use your data differently.
- **No easy way to delete your account data** — To request deletion of your information, you must email customer care. There is no self-service delete button in the app, and the policy does not clearly promise complete erasure of all your data.

### Green Flags
- **You can opt out of marketing emails** — AJIO lets you unsubscribe from promotional emails and newsletters by clicking an unsubscribe link or contacting customer care.
- **No health or biometric data collected** — The app does not ask for or store any health, medical, or biometric information beyond what your phone provides for basic security.
- **Claims not to collect data from minors** — AJIO states it is not meant for users under 18 and promises to delete a child's information if a parent or guardian reports it.
- **Payments handled by encrypted third-party gateways** — Your card and bank details are processed through external, encrypted payment gateways rather than being stored directly by the app in plain text.

### Verdict
AJIO is a large, well-known Indian fashion shopping app, but its privacy practices are weaker than you might expect. It aggressively tracks your shopping behavior, shares that data across the Reliance corporate family and with advertisers, and can follow your location in the background to push store deals. The payment process is secure and you can turn off promotional emails, but the overall data-sharing footprint is large for a shopping app. If you are privacy-conscious, consider using the mobile website with tracking protections instead of the app.

## Category Breakdown

### Camera & Microphone — Caution
The app can access your camera and microphone when you actively choose to use features like uploading product photos or voice search. It does not record in the background.

> *"When you use our voice, image and camera services, we use your voice input, images, videos, and other Information to respond to your requests, provide the requested Products to you, and improve our services or the Platform."*

### Location & GPS — Risky
AJIO collects your location through Bluetooth and Wi-Fi signals, and can track you in the background even when the app is closed to send nearby store deals and updates.

> *"We may collect precise location data from your device in the background to provide relevant services, such as location-based notifications on offline stores related updates, exclusive deals, and a smoother shopping experience."*

### Contacts & Phonebook — Unknown
The privacy policy does not say whether the app accesses your contacts or phonebook.

### Storage & File Access — Caution
The app may access your photos and files when you choose to upload an image, such as for a product review or profile picture.

### Data Sharing with 3rd Parties — Risky
AJIO shares your personal information widely with other companies in the Reliance group, outside advertisers, and service providers that handle deliveries and payments.

> *"The Information collected from you may be shared with our affiliates for providing a seamless/smoother and integrated shopping experience on our Platforms and/or on other online platforms and/or offline stores managed and/or operated by us."*

### Account & Identity Data — Risky
AJIO requires your full name, phone number, email, gender, and date of birth, then links that identity to your shopping behavior and shares it across other platforms for ads and recommendations.

> *"We may collect a variety of Information from you, including your name, mailing address, phone number, email address and contact preferences... Personal Information shall include but is not limited to your full name, personal contact numbers, residential address, email address, gender, date of birth."*

### Behavioural & Ad Tracking — Risky
AJIO tracks what you look at and buy to show you targeted ads and recommendations, and allows outside ad companies to place tracking cookies on your device.

> *"We may allow other companies or entities to serve advertisements that may be of interest to you. These companies or entities include third party advertisement servers, advertisement agencies, advertisement technology vendors and research firms."*

### Children's Data Handling — Caution
AJIO says it is not for kids under 18 and will delete a child's data if a parent reports it, but it does not explain how it actually checks ages.

> *"The Platforms are not directed towards minors. We do not knowingly collect Personal Information from any User who is under 18 (Eighteen) years."*

### Network & Internet Activity — Caution
The app automatically records your IP address, browser type, and internet provider to study overall user trends and improve its services.

> *"When you visit, access or browse the Platforms, we automatically receive the uniform resource locator of the site from which such you visit... the internet protocol address of each User's computer operating system, type of web browser the User is using, email patterns, and the name of the User's internet service provider."*

### Device Info & Fingerprinting — Risky
AJIO collects hardware identifiers, unique app IDs, and Bluetooth and Wi-Fi signals from your phone to recognize your device and deliver location-based services.

> *"Non-Personal Information includes data or information that doesn't personally identify you and may include hardware identifiers, unique systems or application software etc."*

### Health & Biometric Data — Safe
The app does not collect any health or biometric data.

### Financial & Payment Data — Caution
To buy products, your payment details are sent through secure, encrypted third-party payment gateways rather than being stored directly by AJIO.

> *"A third-party payment gateway provider may be required to collect certain financial information from you including, but not restricted to, your credit/debit card number or your bank account details... All Financial Information collected from the you by such third-party payment gateway providers will be used only for billing and payment processes."*

### Data Deletion & User Rights — Caution
You can request changes or deletion of your data by emailing customer care, but there is no simple delete button in the app and the policy does not promise complete removal of everything.

> *"You may access, amend, alter or require deletion of your information partially or fully by contacting us at customercare@ajio.com."*

### Policy Change Notification — Caution
AJIO can change its privacy policy at any time and says that if you keep using the app after changes are posted, you automatically agree to them.

> *"While we make all reasonable efforts to communicate any significant changes to this Privacy Policy to you on best effort basis, we encourage you to review this Privacy Policy whenever you visit our Platforms to understand how your Information is used. Your continued use of the Platforms after any changes, modifications and amendments to this Privacy Policy will be deemed as your acceptance to such changes, modifications and amendments."*
