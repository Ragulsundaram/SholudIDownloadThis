### Red Flags
- **Your data is shared with hotels, airlines, and other travel providers:** When you book a hotel or flight, your name, contact details, payment info, and preferences are sent directly to the trip provider. They may also receive summary info about your past bookings and reviews.
- **Data shared across Booking Holdings brands:** Your data is shared with other companies under the same parent company, including Agoda, Priceline, and OpenTable, for marketing, fraud detection, and analytics.
- **Tracks your browsing for targeted ads across the web:** The app uses cookies and shares hashed versions of your email and phone number with advertising partners to show you targeted ads on other websites and apps.
- **AI systems analyze your behavior and remember your preferences:** The app uses artificial intelligence to track your behavior, predict what trips you might like, and even remember personal preferences like "I always need a hotel with a gym" for future searches.
- **May collect passport or government ID for certain bookings:** For flight reservations, online check-in, or car rentals, the app may ask for your passport, national ID, or driver's license information.

### Green Flags
- **Strong user rights with multiple ways to exercise them:** You can access, correct, delete, or export your data through your account settings, a data request form, or by emailing the privacy team directly.
- **Comprehensive security protections including encryption:** The company uses encryption, data leakage prevention, fraud detection systems, identity management, and recommends two-factor authentication to protect your data.
- **Call recordings automatically deleted after 30 days:** Customer service calls may be recorded for quality control, but recordings are automatically deleted after 30 days unless needed for fraud or legal reasons.
- **AI decisions that significantly affect you include human review:** While the app uses AI for fraud detection and personalization, any decision that would significantly affect you is reviewed by a human, not made solely by a machine.

### Verdict
Booking.com is one of the world's largest travel platforms and collects extensive personal data to facilitate bookings. Your information is shared with hotels, airlines, car rental companies, and other travel providers as a necessary part of making reservations. However, the platform also shares your data with advertising partners, other Booking Holdings brands, and uses AI systems to track and personalize your experience. While the company provides strong security measures and comprehensive user rights, the sheer scale of data collection and sharing warrants caution for privacy-conscious travelers.

## Detailed Analysis

### Data Collection Overview

Booking.com collects a comprehensive set of personal data:

**Identifiers and Contact Data:** Name, email address, home address, telephone number, date of birth, payment information, current location

**Travel-Specific Data:** Names and dates of birth of co-travelers, dietary preferences, accessibility requirements, passport/national ID information (for flights and online check-in), driver's license info (for car rentals), reservation history, reviews

**Account Data:** Login credentials, travel preferences, uploaded photos, saved trip lists, reviews, identification documents (optional)

**Automatically Collected Data:** IP address, usage dates/times, clicks, page views, referral information, device details (OS, browser, app version, language settings), mobile device identifier, app operation and performance data including crashes

**Data from Other Sources:** Other BHI companies, trip providers, strategic partners, marketing partners, social media platforms, publicly available sources

### Data Sharing

The policy discloses extensive sharing across multiple parties:

**Trip Providers:** Full reservation details including name, contact, payment info, preferences, and summary account information (verification status, past bookings, cancellation history, reviews) are shared with hotels, airlines, and other travel providers.

**Booking Holdings Inc. Group:** Data is shared with Agoda, Priceline, OpenTable, and other BHI brands for services, customer service, fraud detection, analytics, and marketing.

**Advertising Partners:** Hashed identifiers (email, phone number) are shared for targeted ad matching. Data clean rooms and pseudonymized insights are used for advertising.

**Third-Party Service Providers:** Customer support, fraud detection (including anti-fraud screening), payment processing (Adyen, Stripe), insurance claims handling.

**Strategic Partners:** Partners who distribute Booking.com services receive reservation details and interaction data.

**Competent Authorities:** Law enforcement and government agencies when required by law or for fraud investigation.

### AI and Automated Decision-Making

The policy describes extensive AI use:
- **Fraud Detection:** AI scans transactions and user content for risk, flagging high-risk items for human review
- **Personalization:** AI predicts relevant products, ranks search results, and displays tailored offerings
- **AI Memory:** Stores preferences (travel style, budget, specific needs) for future personalization
- **Interactive Chats:** AI uses personal data and booking history to make recommendations
- **Customer Service:** AI transcribes and summarizes customer service interactions
- **Intelligent Voice Assistance:** AI-powered phone assistance using booking details
- **Training:** Personal data is used to develop, train, and fine-tune AI systems

The company states that significant decisions include human review, not solely automated systems.

### Cookies and Tracking

Three categories of cookies are used:
- **Functional:** Required for basic operation (account creation, sign-in, booking management)
- **Analytical:** Usage analytics for platform improvement
- **Marketing:** Browsing behavior tracking for personalized ads on and off the platform, social media cookies

Users can decline analytical and marketing cookies where required.

### User Rights

Comprehensive rights are provided:
- **Access:** Request copy of personal data held
- **Correction:** Update or correct personal data
- **Erasure:** Request deletion when no longer needed
- **Restriction:** Block or restrict processing in certain situations
- **Portability:** Receive data in transferable format
- **Withdrawal of Consent:** Withdraw consent where applicable
- **Objection:** Object to processing based on legitimate interest

Exercise methods: Account settings, Data Subject Request form, email (dataprotectionoffice@booking.com), or mail.

### Security Measures

- Comprehensive security policies, procedures, and protocols
- Dedicated cybersecurity and data protection personnel
- Staff security training and awareness
- Encryption and data leakage prevention technologies
- Fraud prevention/detection and continuous monitoring
- Identity and access management
- Incident response protocols
- Regular security system verification
- Equivalent measures imposed on third parties
- Two-factor authentication recommended for accounts
- Unique passwords recommended across services

### Data Retention

- Call recordings: 30 days by default (unless fraud/legal needs)
- Insurance-related calls: Up to 12 months
- General principle: As long as necessary to provide services, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes

### Children's Data

- Services not intended for under 18
- Data deleted if discovered to have been collected without valid consent
- Limited retention may occur for AI training related to content moderation
