## Summary Card

- **App:** Threads
- **Overall Risk:** Risky
- **Score:** 54 / 100
- **Grade:** D

### Red Flags
- **Collects vast amounts of personal data.** Threads collects everything you post, your messages, your camera roll, your location, what device you're using, what other apps you have, and even tracks whether the app is open or closed on your phone.
- **Tracks you across Meta apps and devices.** Meta uses your Threads data to show you ads and recommendations across Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, and even other apps that use Meta's ad network. They track you across all your devices.
- **Your content trains Meta's AI.** The posts, comments, photos, and messages you create on Threads are used to develop and improve Meta's artificial intelligence systems, including AI that may be used by third parties.
- **Your profile and posts are public by default.** Unless you change your settings, anyone on Threads — and anyone on the internet — can see your profile and posts. Other people can also share or reshare your content without your direct approval.
- **Data shared with advertisers and partners.** Meta shares your information with advertisers, measurement companies, marketing vendors, and integrated partners so they can measure how well their ads work and target you better.
- **Humans may review your private content.** Meta says their systems mostly process your data automatically, but in some cases real people at Meta manually review your messages, posts, and photos for safety or policy enforcement.

### Green Flags
- **Meta says they don't sell your data.** Meta explicitly states they don't sell your information to anyone, though they do share it broadly with partners and advertisers.
- **Notified before major policy changes.** Meta promises to tell you before they make important changes to the privacy policy, and gives you a chance to review the new terms before continuing to use the app.
- **Account and data deletion tools exist.** You can download a copy of your data, delete your account, or manage what information Meta collects about you through the app's settings.
- **Some ad personalization controls.** You can manage some ad preferences and turn off certain types of tracking, though this doesn't stop all data collection.

### Verdict
Threads inherits Meta's data-heavy privacy model. It collects your posts, messages, camera roll, device info, location, and app usage, then uses it to personalize ads, train AI, and share with a vast network of advertisers, partners, and service providers. Your profile is public by default, and your data flows across all Meta products including Instagram and Facebook. While Meta promises not to sell your data, the sheer scale of collection and sharing is significant.
