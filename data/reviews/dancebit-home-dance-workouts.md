## Summary Card

**App:** Dancebit: Home Dance Workouts  
**Overall Risk:** Caution  
**Score:** 58 / 100 (Grade C)

### Red Flags
- **Data shared with a vast network of advertisers.** Dancebit shares your information with advertising platforms like Meta, Google, TikTok, Pinterest, X, Snapchat, and AppLovin, plus analytics companies like Amplitude, AppsFlyer, and Facebook. They explicitly admit this may count as selling your data under California privacy law.
- **Sensitive health and body data collected.** The app collects your physical attributes, exercise routines, weight, height, and can import fitness data from Apple HealthKit or Google Health Connect. It also uses AI to process your body movements and activity for personalized dance routines.
- **AI processes your body movements and preferences.** If you use the AI-powered dance features, the app processes data about your activity, body movements, and preferences to generate personalized routines. This data may be retained for up to one month.
- **Advertising IDs and device tracking used.** The app collects your device's advertising identifier (Apple IDFA or Google GAID), IP address, and other device details. These are shared with advertising partners to track you across apps and show you targeted ads.
- **Customer support chats processed by OpenAI.** When you contact customer support, your inquiries and subscription details may be processed by an AI Assistant powered by OpenAI's GPT-4 to generate responses and analyze your tone.

### Green Flags
- **Strong data protection rights available.** You can access, correct, delete, or export your data. You can also object to processing and withdraw consent. The app responds to requests within 30 days and provides detailed region-specific rights for EU, UK, US states, Canada, Brazil, Australia, Japan, and Mexico.
- **You can opt out of targeted advertising.** You can opt out of data sharing for targeted ads by contacting support, adjusting cookie preferences, using device settings, or using industry opt-out tools like the Digital Advertising Alliance.
- **Claims not to sell data for money.** The company states it does not sell or rent your personal data for direct financial gain. They clarify that advertising partnerships may legally count as selling under some laws, but no money is exchanged for your data.
- **Easy account deletion available in the app.** You can delete your account directly within the app on both iOS and Android. After deletion, data is retained for no longer than two months unless legally required.

### Verdict
Dancebit collects detailed health and wellness data including physical attributes, exercise routines, weight, and height, and can import data from Apple HealthKit or Google Health Connect. It uses AI to analyze your activity and body movements for personalized dance routines. While the app offers strong privacy rights and transparent disclosures, it shares your information with an extensive list of third parties—including Meta, Google, TikTok, and over a dozen analytics and advertising platforms—for targeted advertising and user tracking.
