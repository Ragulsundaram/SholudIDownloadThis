# Zoom Workplace Privacy Review

**Date:** 2026-05-17  
**Analyst:** claude-kimi-k2.5  
**Policy:** https://www.zoom.com/en/trust/privacy/privacy-statement/  
**Policy Last Updated:** 2026-02-02

## Methodology
Re-analysis performed with strict legal-safe rules: every claim directly quoted, no strong verbs from weak evidence, no invented purposes, high-severity flags require explicit unambiguous backing.

## Key Findings

### Verdict
- **Score:** 54 / 100
- **Grade:** D
- **Risk:** Risky
- **Recommendation:** Think twice

### Red Flags (6)
1. **Your employer can access everything you say and share in meetings** — The policy explicitly states that account owners can access recordings, transcripts, chat content, participant lists, and direct messages (unless Advanced Chat Encryption is enabled). (Severity: high)
2. **Zoom collects detailed information about your device** — The policy lists MAC address, hard disk ID, PC name, OS version, battery level, WiFi info, and Bluetooth signals as collected device information. (Severity: high)
3. **Your mouse movements, clicks, and keystrokes are collected** — The policy explicitly collects mouse movements, clicks, keystrokes, and actions like mute/unmute or video on/off to understand feature usage and improve product design. (Severity: high)
4. **Your facial geometry and voiceprint may be processed on servers** — With consent, Zoom may process facial geometry and/or voiceprint on its servers, retained for up to two years. (Severity: medium)
5. **Data shared with resellers, vendors, and marketing partners** — The policy discloses sharing with resellers, vendors, marketing and analytics partners, corporate affiliates, and third-party developers. (Severity: medium)
6. **Targeted advertising and analytics through cookies** — Third-party cookies and trackers are used for targeted advertising and website analytics. (Severity: medium)

### Removed Flag
- **Meeting content is not end-to-end encrypted by default** — Removed because the privacy policy does not contain an explicit statement about end-to-end encryption for regular meetings. This claim could not be supported with a direct quote.

### Green Flags (5)
1. **Zoom does not use your meeting content to train AI models** — Explicitly promised in the policy.
2. **Basic video effects are processed on your device only** — Filters, avatars, and gestures are processed locally and not retained.
3. **Zoom explicitly says it does not sell your personal data** — The policy states it does not sell personal data "in the conventional sense" and has no knowledge of selling data from minors under 16.
4. **You are notified before material privacy policy changes take effect** — Material changes trigger notification and an opportunity to review.
5. **Strong privacy rights for EEA and U.S. state residents** — Access, deletion, correction, and portability rights are described.

## Notable Corrections
- Rephrased device fingerprinting flag to remove implied purpose ("to identify and track") that was not explicitly stated for the full list of device attributes.
- Rephrased keystroke/mouse flag to use "collected" instead of "logged" to align with strict verb rules.
- Updated no-sale language to reflect the exact hedged wording in the policy ("in the conventional sense").
- Score unchanged at 54 (D). Total deductions remain 93.75.
