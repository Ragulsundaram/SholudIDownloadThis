## Summary Card

- **Overall risk:** Risky
- **Score:** 54 / 100
- **Grade:** D

### Red flags
- **Your employer can access everything you say and share in meetings** — If you use Zoom through a work account, your employer (the account owner) can view recordings, transcripts, chat messages, participant lists, and even direct messages sent during meetings. (High)
- **Zoom builds an extremely detailed fingerprint of your device** — Zoom collects your MAC address, hard disk ID, PC name, device model, operating system, battery level, WiFi info, and Bluetooth signals to identify and track your device. (High)
- **Your mouse movements, clicks, and keystrokes are logged** — Zoom tracks your mouse movements, clicks, keystrokes, and actions like mute/unmute or video on/off to understand how you use the app and improve product design. (High)
- **Your facial geometry and voiceprint may be processed on servers** — If you enable enhanced video features and give consent, Zoom processes your facial geometry and voiceprint on its servers. This data is kept for up to 2 years. (Medium)
- **Data shared with resellers, vendors, and marketing partners** — Zoom shares your data with resellers, third-party service providers, marketing and advertising partners, corporate affiliates, and third-party app developers in the Zoom App Marketplace. (Medium)
- **Targeted advertising and analytics through cookies** — Zoom uses third-party cookies and trackers on its website and app to deliver targeted advertising and analyze your behavior. You can opt out through the cookie management tool. (Medium)
- **Meeting content is not end-to-end encrypted by default** — Your video, audio, and chat in meetings are encrypted in transit but not end-to-end encrypted by default. Zoom has the technical ability to access meeting content if required for legal or safety reasons. (Medium)

### Green flags
- **Zoom does not use your meeting content to train AI models** — Zoom explicitly promises it does not use your audio, video, chat, screen sharing, or other meeting content to train its own or third-party artificial intelligence models.
- **Basic video effects are processed on your device only** — Filters, studio effects, avatars, and gestures are processed entirely on your device. This data does not leave your device and cannot be used to identify you.
- **Zoom explicitly says it does not sell your personal data** — Under California privacy law, Zoom states it does not sell your personal information and has no knowledge of selling data from minors under 16.
- **You are notified before material privacy policy changes take effect** — If Zoom makes material changes to its privacy policy, it will notify you and give you an opportunity to review before you choose to continue using the service.
- **Strong privacy rights for EEA and U.S. state residents** — Depending on where you live, you have rights to access, delete, correct, or port your data, and to object to processing or marketing communications.

### Verdict
Zoom Workplace is an essential tool for video meetings and team collaboration, but the privacy trade-offs are significant. Your employer or meeting host may have full access to recordings, transcripts, and messages. Zoom also tracks your device extensively, logs your keystrokes and mouse movements, and shares data with numerous third parties. On the positive side, Zoom does not use meeting content for AI training, processes basic video effects on-device, and provides strong regional privacy rights. Users should be aware of what their account owner can access and consider using personal accounts for sensitive conversations.

## Audit Trail — Category-by-Category Analysis

### Camera & Microphone
- **Risk:** Caution
- **Reasoning:** Camera and microphone are required for core video meeting functionality. Basic video features (filters, avatars, gestures) are processed entirely on-device and do not leave the device. Enhanced features requiring consent process facial geometry and voiceprint on servers.
- **Policy excerpt:** "If you elect to enable certain video features, if available in your area, such as filters, studio effects, avatars, and gestures, information about your movements or the positioning of your face or hands may be processed on your device to apply the selected features. Such data does not leave your device, is not retained, cannot be used to identify you, and is only used to generate the selected effects."

### Location & GPS
- **Risk:** Safe
- **Reasoning:** No explicit GPS or precise location access mentioned. IP address may infer general location at city or country level.
- **Policy excerpt:** "IP address (which may be used to infer general location at a city or country level)"

### Contacts & Phonebook
- **Risk:** Caution
- **Reasoning:** Users can add contacts manually or integrate from third-party apps. Not automatically uploaded.
- **Policy excerpt:** "Contact Information: Contact information added by accounts and/or their licensed end users to create contact lists on Zoom products and services, which may include contact information a user integrates from a third-party app."

### Storage & File Access
- **Risk:** Caution
- **Reasoning:** Files can be uploaded and shared through Zoom Chat. Zoom automatically scans files for security.
- **Policy excerpt:** "Zoom uses advanced tools to automatically scan certain types of content such as... files uploaded or exchanged through chat, for the purpose detecting and preventing violations of our terms or policies and illegal or other harmful activity."

### Data Sharing with 3rd Parties
- **Risk:** Risky
- **Reasoning:** Extensive sharing with resellers, vendors, marketing/advertising/analytics partners, corporate affiliates, third-party developers, for legal purposes, and in corporate transactions.
- **Policy excerpt:** "Zoom provides personal data to third parties only with consent or in one of the following circumstances... Resellers... Vendors... Marketing, Advertising, and Analytics Partners... Corporate Affiliates... Change of Control... Third-Party Developers... For Legal Purposes"

### Account & Identity Data
- **Risk:** Risky
- **Reasoning:** Comprehensive identity data collected. Account owners (employers) have broad access to all user profile data on their accounts.
- **Policy excerpt:** "Profile and Participant Information: Information associated with the Zoom profile of a user... which may include name, display name, picture, email address, phone number, job information, stated locale, user ID, or other information provided by the user and/or their account owner."

### Behavioural & Ad Tracking
- **Risk:** Risky
- **Reasoning:** Third-party marketing, advertising, and analytics providers used for targeted advertising and website/app usage analysis.
- **Policy excerpt:** "Zoom uses third-party marketing, advertising, and analytics providers: to provide statistics and analysis about how people are using Zoom products and services, including our website; and to provide advertising and marketing for Zoom products and services, including targeted advertising based on your use of our website."

### Children's Data Handling
- **Risk:** Caution
- **Reasoning:** Not for under 16. Separate Children's Educational Privacy Statement for schools.
- **Policy excerpt:** "Zoom does not allow children under the age of 16 to sign up for a Zoom account."

### Network & Internet Activity
- **Risk:** Risky
- **Reasoning:** Extremely comprehensive logging including mouse movements, clicks, keystrokes, feature usage, WiFi, Bluetooth, and IP address.
- **Policy excerpt:** "Usage Information Regarding Meetings, Webinars, Messaging, Collaborative Features and the Website: Information about how people and their devices interact with Zoom products and services, such as: when participants join and leave a meeting; whether participants sent messages and who they message with; performance data; mouse movements, clicks, keystrokes or actions... features used (such as screen sharing, emojis, or filters)"

### Device Info & Fingerprinting
- **Risk:** Risky
- **Reasoning:** Very comprehensive device fingerprinting including MAC address, hard disk ID, PC name, Bluetooth signals, and battery level.
- **Policy excerpt:** "Device Information: Information about the computers, phones, and other devices used when interacting with Zoom products and services, which may include information about the speakers, microphone, camera, OS version, hard disk ID, PC name, MAC address, IP address... device attributes (like operating system version and battery level), WiFi information, and other device information (like Bluetooth signals)."

### Health & Biometric Data
- **Risk:** Risky
- **Reasoning:** Facial geometry and voiceprint processed on servers for enhanced features with consent. Retained up to 2 years.
- **Policy excerpt:** "If you elect to enable certain features and you provide the requisite consent, Zoom may process data about your unique physical characteristics, including your facial geometry and /or voiceprint, for the purpose of offering the features. This data will be deleted when no longer necessary, adequate, or relevant to offering the features or when two years have passed since your last interaction with Zoom, whichever occurs first."

### Financial & Payment Data
- **Risk:** Caution
- **Reasoning:** Billing and transaction information collected. Payment processing handled by third parties.
- **Policy excerpt:** "Account Information: Information associated with an account that licenses Zoom products and services, which may include... billing and transaction information, and account plan information."

### Data Deletion & User Rights
- **Risk:** Caution
- **Reasoning:** Users can request access, correction, or deletion. EEA and U.S. state residents have additional rights. Some data retained for legal obligations.
- **Policy excerpt:** "At your request, and as required by applicable law, we will: Inform you of what personal data we have about you that is under our control; Amend or correct such personal data... and/or Delete such personal data or direct you to applicable tools."

### Policy Change Notification
- **Risk:** Caution
- **Reasoning:** Material changes notified with opportunity to review. No specific advance period stated.
- **Policy excerpt:** "If we make material changes to this Privacy Statement, we will notify you and provide you an opportunity to review before you choose to continue using our products and services."

## Notes

- **Terms URL found:** https://www.zoom.com/en/trust/terms/ — this was successfully extracted from the App Store page.
- The Zoom privacy policy is exceptionally detailed and transparent, covering multiple jurisdictions (EEA, UK, Switzerland, California, Virginia, Nevada, and other U.S. states).
- The policy distinguishes between "Customer Content" (meeting content) and other data types. Customer Content is treated more carefully — Zoom employees do not access it without authorization, and it's not used for AI training or marketing.
- The account owner control structure is a double-edged sword: employers get powerful administrative tools but users lose privacy within their organization's account.
- The "Enhanced Features" requiring consent for biometric processing is a good practice, but the 2-year retention is longer than ideal.
- Zoom's Data Privacy Framework (DPF) certification is a positive signal for cross-border data transfers.
- The policy mentions that Zoom Email is end-to-end encrypted by default between Zoom Email users, which is a positive feature not commonly known.
