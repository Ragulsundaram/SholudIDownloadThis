## Summary Card

### Mimi Hearing Test
- **Overall Risk:** Caution  
- **Score:** 79 / 100 (Grade B)  
- **Recommendation:** Recommended

**Red Flags**
- **Privacy policy ignores hearing test data** — The app collects your hearing test results, which are sensitive health data. But the privacy policy does not explain how this data is stored, who can see it, or how long it is kept.
- **Hearing profiles added to company database** — The app says it has contributed to the world's largest database of digital hearing profiles. This means your hearing data is likely being collected and stored by the company, but the policy does not explain how it is used or anonymized.
- **App is 12+ but policy says 18+** — The app is rated 12+ on the App Store, but the privacy policy says it is not for anyone under 18. This is a direct contradiction.
- **Multiple third-party trackers** — The app and website use Google Analytics and Mixpanel to track how you use them. These companies collect data about your device and activity.

**Green Flags**
- **GDPR data rights available** — If you live in Europe, you have legal rights to see, correct, delete, or move your data. The company has a Data Protection Officer in Germany.
- **Apple Health integration** — Your hearing test results can be synced to the Apple Health app on your phone, giving you a local copy of your data.
- **Core test works offline** — The hearing test itself can be done without an internet connection, though syncing and exporting require connectivity.

**Verdict**  
Mimi Hearing Test is a popular and well-designed tool for assessing your hearing across a wide range of frequencies. It integrates with Apple Health and has been used by millions of people. However, the only privacy documentation available is a generic website notice focused on B2B activities, with no explanation of how your sensitive hearing test data is handled, stored, or protected. Users should be aware that their hearing profiles are likely being added to a company database with unclear safeguards.

## Full Analysis

### What it does
Mimi Hearing Test is a hearing assessment app that tests a wide range of frequencies in about 5 minutes. It delivers instant results, tracks hearing changes over time, syncs with Apple Health, and allows exporting results as PDFs (in certain regions). The app supports specific headphone models for accurate testing.

### Data collected
- **Hearing test data:** Frequency-specific hearing thresholds, hearing profiles
- **Device information:** Headphone model used, device OS, browser type, country
- **Usage data:** Website visits, app interactions, clicks, satisfaction rates
- **Analytics data:** Google Analytics cookies (_ga, _gid, _gat), Mixpanel behavioral data

### Who it is shared with
- **Google Analytics** — Website and app usage tracking, visitor behavior, traffic sources
- **Mixpanel** — Chrome extension behavioral analytics, feature clicks, geo data
- **YouTube/Google** — When viewing videos on the website, YouTube servers are contacted
- **Mimi's hearing profile database** — Hearing test results contribute to the "world's largest database of digital hearing profiles"

### Health Data
The app's core function is collecting hearing test data, which is sensitive health information. The app:
- Creates a digital hearing profile based on your test results
- Syncs results with Apple Health
- Contributes to a company database of hearing profiles
- Exports results as PDFs for sharing with healthcare professionals

However, the privacy policy (which is website-focused) makes **no mention** of hearing data, health data protection, HIPAA compliance, or how hearing profiles are anonymized, stored, or shared.

### Children's Data
The App Store content advisory is 12+ (Infrequent/Mild Medical/Treatment Information). However, the privacy notice states it does not intend to collect data from individuals under 18, with parental consent required for minors. This 18+ threshold is unusually high and contradicts the 12+ rating.

### Data Transfer
The company's website and services are hosted in the United States. For EU users, the company offers a Data Processing Addendum with Standard Contractual Clauses for data transferred to US servers.
