## Summary Card

**App:** Headspace: Sleep & Meditation  
**Overall Risk:** Caution  
**Score:** 61 / 100 (Grade C)

### Red Flags
- **Sensitive health data collected and used for advertising.** Headspace collects your mental health status, therapy discussions, sleep data, and wellness goals. While they don't use your actual therapy content for ads, they do use other personal information to show you targeted ads on other websites and apps through third-party advertising platforms.
- **Your employer may see your usage data.** If you access Headspace through your employer or health plan, they may receive your name, email, registration date, and last use date. In some cases, they may also see details about your in-app activity or therapy use.
- **AI features may process your reflections.** Headspace uses artificial intelligence for content recommendations, security monitoring, and quality assurance. Some features let you directly interact with AI (like "Ebb" for reflections). You get a choice before using these features, but your interactions may still be processed.
- **Data is stored and processed in the United States.** Headspace is a US company and your personal information, including sensitive health data, is primarily processed and stored in the United States. This means it is subject to US laws, which may have weaker privacy protections than laws in your country.
- **Third-party platforms track you for ads.** Headspace works with third-party advertising platforms to show you personalized ads. Under California privacy law, this counts as "selling" or "sharing" your personal information. You can opt out, but it is enabled by default.

### Green Flags
- **Health information protected under HIPAA.** Headspace treats your health and wellness information as protected health information under HIPAA, the US medical privacy law. This means your therapy and health data gets extra legal protections.
- **You can access, correct, or delete your data.** You have the right to request a copy of your data, correct inaccurate information, export your data, and request deletion. Headspace says they will honor these requests regardless of where you live.
- **Not designed for children under 13.** Headspace explicitly states it is not for children under 13 and does not intentionally collect data from them. They will delete a child's data if discovered.
- **Detailed and transparent privacy policy.** Headspace provides a comprehensive privacy policy with clear explanations, a table of contents, and supplemental notices for specific regions like the EU, UK, and California. They also disclose their AI use practices.

### Verdict
Headspace is a popular mental health and meditation app with strong privacy protections on paper—HIPAA compliance, detailed disclosures, and good user rights. However, for a mental health app, the sharing of personal information with advertising platforms for targeted ads and the potential for employer access to usage data are significant concerns. If you are using Headspace for therapy or sensitive mental health support, be aware that some of your data may be used to show you ads elsewhere on the internet. Consider whether you are comfortable with this trade-off before downloading.
