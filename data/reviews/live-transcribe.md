## Summary Card

### Live Transcribe
- **Overall Risk:** Caution  
- **Score:** 75 / 100 (Grade B)  
- **Recommendation:** Recommended

**Red Flags**
- **Third-party ads and trackers** — The app uses outside advertising companies to show ads. These companies can collect data about your app usage and combine it with data from other sites.
- **Facebook Custom Audience tool** — If you provide your email, it can be converted into a code Facebook uses to match you with personalized ads.
- **Data shared with vendors** — Your information is shared with outside companies for data analysis, email delivery, hosting, customer service, and marketing. Some vendors use tracking technology.

**Green Flags**
- **Microphone use is clear and limited** — The app only uses your microphone when you actively start a transcription, which is the whole point of the app. It does not record in the background or share recordings.
- **No contact list access** — The app never asks for your contacts.
- **No GPS or precise location** — The app does not track your exact location.
- **Users can request data deletion** — You can email the company to ask for your data to be deleted, and they promise to respond within 30 days.
- **Email is optional** — You can use the app fully without sharing your email address.

**Verdict**  
Live Transcribe is a genuinely helpful accessibility app that handles its core microphone access responsibly. However, it unnecessarily brings in third-party advertisers and tracking tools that collect data about how you use the app. If you need live transcription and are okay with some ad tracking, it is a solid choice. If you want to avoid ad tracking entirely, look for a paid alternative with no ads.

## Full Analysis

### What it does
Live Transcribe converts speech to text in real time for d/Deaf and hard-of-hearing users. It supports 145+ languages and works offline for 9 languages. The app is free and funded by ads.

### Data collected
- **Device info:** IP address, device ID, model, manufacturer, operating system, browser characteristics, language preferences, referring URLs, device name, country
- **Optional:** Email address (only if you choose to share it)
- **Usage data:** How and when you use the app, collected for internal analytics and reporting

### Who it is shared with
- Third-party vendors for payment processing, data analysis, email delivery, hosting, customer service, and marketing
- Third-party advertising companies that serve ads and use tracking cookies
- Facebook (via Custom Audience Tool) if you provide an email address
- Potential acquirers in a business transfer (merger, sale, financing)

### Retention
The company keeps personal information for no longer than 1 year unless legally required. When no longer needed, data is deleted, anonymized, or securely isolated.

### User rights
- You can request to review, update, or delete your data by emailing support@mightyfine.app
- The company promises a response within 30 days
- Users in the European Economic Area have rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, and data portability
- California residents can request information about personal data shared for direct marketing

### Policy history
- **Last updated:** August 1, 2021
- The policy is over 4 years old, which is concerning. The company says it will update as necessary to stay compliant with laws.

### Notes for reviewer
- The privacy policy is a generic template that mentions "orders, payments, returns, and exchanges" which do not appear to be core features of the free transcription app. This suggests the policy may be a copy-paste template shared across multiple apps by the same developer.
- The policy explicitly states it does not share information with third parties for their promotional purposes, but it does use third-party advertising companies and Facebook Custom Audience, which is a form of promotional data sharing.
- No HealthKit or biometric data collection.
- No location/GPS tracking beyond country-level IP inference.
- Transcripts can be manually exported to other apps by the user.
