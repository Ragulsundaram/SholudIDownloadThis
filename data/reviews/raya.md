## Summary Card

**App:** Raya  
**Developer:** Raya App Inc  
**Overall Risk:** Caution  
**Score:** 57 / 100 (Grade C)

### Red Flags
- **Privacy policy is 6 years old.** Last updated in May 2020, yet the app is actively maintained (v5.30.0 as of May 2026). Current data practices may differ significantly.
- **Harvests your entire Instagram profile during application.** Raya collects your Instagram username, profile picture, biography, website, full followers list, full following list, and photo count to evaluate your application.
- **Uploads your phone contacts.** As part of the application, Raya accesses your phone contacts to check if you know existing members. Other users may also upload your contact info if you are in their phone.
- **Profile visible to all accepted members.** Once accepted, your profile including Instagram photos, first name, age, city, hometown, and biography is viewable by every other member. You cannot fully hide from the member pool.
- **Tracks location via IP even if GPS is denied.** Even if you refuse exact location sharing, Raya infers your city, state, and general area from your internet connection address.
- **Uses Google Analytics.** Raya shares usage and behavioral data with Google Analytics to analyze trends and improve the service.
- **Explicitly disclaims liability for data breaches.** The policy states the company has no legal responsibility to you if your personal information is lost, misused, or altered in a security breach.
- **Data could be transferred in a company sale.** If Raya is sold or its assets are transferred, your personal information may be included.

### Green Flags
- **Explicitly promises never to sell your data.** Raya states clearly it will never sell personal information to any third party and will not disclose it without permission.
- **Strict 18+ age requirement.** Raya restricts membership to adults 18 and older and immediately removes underage users if discovered.
- **Never handles your payment details.** All payments go through Apple or Stripe; Raya never sees or stores credit card information.
- **Deletes inactive accounts after 2 years.** If you stop using the app for 2 years, your data is deleted or anonymized. After account deletion, a 3-month safety window applies before removal.
- **Offers GDPR-style data rights.** You can request access, correction, deletion, objection, and portability by contacting Raya directly.

### Verdict
Raya is an exclusive, application-only private community that makes some genuine privacy commitments, especially its no-selling promise and strict age gate. However, the 6-year-old privacy policy raises serious questions about whether current practices are accurately disclosed. The app collects extensive personal data during the application process, including your full Instagram social graph and phone contacts, and makes your profile visible to all accepted members. If you are comfortable with this trade-off for access to an exclusive community, it is a reasonable choice, but privacy-conscious users should proceed with caution.

## Review Notes

- The privacy policy was last updated on **May 6, 2020** — more than 6 years ago. The app has released numerous updates since then (current version 5.30.0 as of May 2026), making it highly likely that current data practices are not accurately reflected.
- **Instagram data collection** is unusually extensive for a dating app: followers list, following list, photo count, biography, and website are all harvested during the application process. This creates a detailed social graph that may be used for evaluation and matching.
- **Phone contacts access** is mandatory-feeling during the application, though technically the user can presumably decline. The fact that "other users may provide information about you as they use the Service if... you are a contact in their phone" creates a network effect where your data can enter Raya's systems without your direct consent.
- The **explicit liability disclaimer for security breaches** is rare and concerning: "We have no liability to you or to any third party arising out of any loss, misuse or alteration of your personal information." This directly contradicts the trust-oriented brand positioning.
- **Device fingerprinting persistence** is notable: the unique device identifier survives uninstallation and is re-associated if the app is reinstalled on the same device.
- **Cross-border data transfers** to the United States and other jurisdictions are explicitly acknowledged, with standard contractual clauses mentioned as safeguards.
- The **2-year inactivity deletion** and **3-month post-deletion safety window** are reasonable retention practices, though broad exceptions apply.
- The **18+ age gate** is genuine and well-enforced according to the policy, though the App Store rates it 17+.
