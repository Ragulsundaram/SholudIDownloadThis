## Summary Card

**App:** LinkedIn: Community & Network
**Overall Risk:** Risky (Grade D)
**Score:** 47/100

### Red Flags
- **AI model training** - Your personal data is used to develop and train artificial intelligence models, and your public posts may be shared with Microsoft for their AI development
- **Microsoft data sharing** - Your data is shared with Microsoft and other affiliates for advertising services and AI development
- **Extensive cross-device tracking** - LinkedIn uses cookies, pixels, and device IDs to recognize you across different services and devices, even when you haven't engaged with them
- **Public content spreads** - Public posts can be viewed by everyone, re-shared anywhere, and may appear in search engine results even after deletion
- **Data sharing in corporate transactions** - Your data may be shared as part of a sale, merger, or change in control

### Green Flags
- **Job search privacy** - LinkedIn explicitly does not share your job hunting activities or personal messages with your employer
- **Account deletion available** - Profile stops being visible within 24 hours; account data generally deleted within 30 days
- **Ad personalization controls** - Settings available to control personalized ads

### Verdict
LinkedIn is a professional networking platform with significant privacy trade-offs. While your job searches are protected from employers and account deletion is available, the platform extensively tracks your behavior across devices, shares your data with Microsoft for advertising and AI training, and uses your content to train AI models. Consider using the platform with minimal personal information and reviewing your privacy settings regularly.

## Analysis Notes

### Key Findings

**1. AI Model Training (Major Concern)**
LinkedIn explicitly states it uses your personal data to "develop and train artificial intelligence (AI) models." Your publicly-shared content may also be shared with Microsoft to "provide or develop their services."

**2. Microsoft Data Sharing (Dangerous)**
LinkedIn shares personal data with Microsoft and other affiliates for:
- Improving, providing, or developing advertising services
- Providing or developing their services
- Security and fraud prevention

**3. Extensive Cross-Device Tracking**
LinkedIn uses cookies, pixels, ad tags, and device IDs to:
- Recognize you and your devices on, off, and across different services
- Track you even when you haven't engaged with LinkedIn (outside Designated Countries)
- Collect device information including IP address, operating system, browser details

**4. Public Content Persistence**
Content shared publicly:
- Can be viewed by everyone and re-shared anywhere
- May appear in search engine results
- May remain accessible even after LinkedIn fulfills a deletion request
- Content shared with others may remain visible to them after you delete it

**5. Data Retention**
Personal data is retained:
- As long as your account is open
- Even after account closure for legal obligations, regulatory requirements, dispute resolution, security, and fraud prevention
- Information you share with others remains visible even after account closure

**6. Job Search Privacy Protection**
LinkedIn explicitly states it does not share:
- Your job hunting activities with your employer
- Personal messages with your employer
- Unless you choose to share them through the Services

### User Rights
- **Access, correction, deletion** - Available for personal data
- **Data portability** - Can request data in machine-readable format
- **Object to processing** - Can object to data use where lawful basis is legitimate interests
- **Withdraw consent** - Can withdraw consent at any time through settings
- **Account closure** - Profile visibility stops within 24 hours; data generally deleted within 30 days

### Privacy Controls
- Ad personalization settings available for Members
- Communication preferences can be adjusted
- Profile visibility settings control who can see your information
- Cookie controls (though disabling may limit functionality)
- "Do Not Track" signal response (limited)

## Verbatim Policy Excerpts

**On AI Training:**
> "We may use your personal data to improve, develop, and provide products and Services, develop and train artificial intelligence (AI) models, develop, provide, and personalize our Services, and gain insights with the help of AI"

**On Microsoft Sharing:**
> "We may also share with our Affiliates, including Microsoft, your (1) publicly-shared content (such as your public LinkedIn posts) to provide or develop their services and (2) personal data to improve, provide or develop their advertising services."

**On Cross-Device Tracking:**
> "We collect data through cookies and similar technologies (e.g., pixels and ad tags) to collect data (e.g., device IDs) to recognize you and your device(s) on, off and across different services and devices where you have engaged with our Services."

**On Public Content:**
> "When you share an article or a post (e.g., an update, image, video or article) publicly it can be viewed by everyone and re-shared anywhere"

**On Data Retention:**
> "We generally retain your personal data as long as you keep your account open or as needed to provide you Services."

**On Job Search Privacy:**
> "We understand that certain activities such as job hunting and personal messages are sensitive, and so we do not share those with your employer unless you choose to share it with them"

**On Data Sharing in Corporate Transactions:**
> "We can also share your personal data as part of a sale, merger or change in control, or in preparation for any of these events. Any other entity which buys us or part of our business will have the right to continue to use your data"
