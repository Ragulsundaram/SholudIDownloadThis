# F1 TV — Privacy Review

**Reviewed:** 2026-05-16
**Policy:** https://account.formula1.com/#/en/privacy-policy
**Platform:** iOS
**Score:** 69/100 (Grade C, Caution)

---

## Summary

F1 TV's privacy practices are typical for a subscription streaming service. It collects viewing and payment data and may share it across the Formula 1 group and with advertising partners. It also gives users solid GDPR rights and ways to opt out of marketing.

Key concern addressed in this review: the previous analysis incorrectly claimed that F1 TV "shares your personal data with third-party sponsors" and "promoters who organise race events." The privacy policy only lists sponsors and promoters as *sources* of data, not as *recipients*. The sharing section explicitly names Formula 1 group companies, third-party service providers, third-party partners (independent data controllers), and social media/advertising/analytics partners (for US residents).

---

## Methodology

This review was conducted by analyzing the F1 TV Fan Privacy Policy, accessed via jina.ai text extraction on 2026-05-16. The privacy policy was last updated April 2025. All claims are backed by direct verbatim quotes. Cautious language ("may," "states that") is used where the policy leaves room for interpretation.

### Data Collection Observed

- **Identity:** First name, last name, username, marital status, title, date of birth, age, gender
- **Contact:** Billing address, email, telephone numbers
- **Financial:** Bank account and payment card details
- **Technical:** IP address, login data, screen resolution, browser type/version, time zone, location, mobile and network info, OS, platform
- **Usage:** Full URL clickstream, pages viewed/searched, page response times, download errors, visit length, page interactions (scrolling, clicks, mouse-overs), customer service call numbers
- **Marketing:** Communication preferences, survey responses, interests, feedback

### Notable Excerpts

> "To present you with targeted and personalised adverts on third-party websites and applications, including social media sites."

> "We may share your personal data with the following third parties for the purposes set out above: Our Formula 1 group companies. Our third party service providers... Our third party partners who act as independent data controllers..."

> "To analyse data collected about you, such as your purchase history, by us and our Third Party Licensees (where we have a legal basis to receive such data from them) for the purpose of profiling and personalisation of the direct marketing we send you."

> "We and our service providers and third party partners may monitor some of your uses of, or activity on, our Digital Products... and we may capture and retain records reflecting such activity on our Digital Products, for our business purposes."

> "We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 16 and our Digital Products are not directed at children under the age of 16."

> "We may, from time to time, update this Privacy Policy. Any such changes will be posted on this page, and we will notify you of any significant changes, such as change to our processing activities."
