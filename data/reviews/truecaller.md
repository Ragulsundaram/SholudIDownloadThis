## Overall Risk: Risky | **Score:** 47/100 | **Grade:** D

## Red Flags

- **Truecaller collects a wide range of device and usage data:** The privacy policy explicitly lists extensive data collection including geo-location, IP address, device ID, installed apps, advertising ID, call and message metadata, and paired devices.
- **Your data is shared with third parties for targeted ads:** Truecaller shares personal information with third-party clients, agencies, and networks who may use it for analytical and marketing purposes, including targeted advertising.
- **Your location is used for interest-based advertising:** Truecaller states it may use your location and other collected information to provide location and interest-based advertising.
- **Call and message metadata is collected:** Truecaller collects logs, keywords, and metadata of your incoming and outgoing calls and messages.
- **Truecaller checks what apps are installed on your device:** The policy explicitly states that Truecaller collects information about applications installed on your device.

## Green Flags

- **You can permanently unlist your number:** Truecaller states users can permanently unlist their number from the database.
- **You can download a copy of your data:** Users can download an entire copy of the data Truecaller holds about them.
- **You can opt out of ad targeting:** Users can limit or opt out of the collection and use of their information for ad targeting through device settings.
- **iOS app does not share your contacts with others:** For apps from the Apple App Store, Truecaller explicitly states it does not share user contact information.

## Verdict

Truecaller is a caller ID and spam blocking app, but its privacy practices are extensive. It collects your location, device identifiers, installed apps, call and message metadata, and advertising ID. This data is shared with third-party advertising and analytics partners for targeted ads. While the iOS app does not share your contacts with other users, it does collect them. Users have some controls: they can delist their number, download their data, and opt out of ad targeting. The policy does not mention camera, microphone, or health data, so those categories could not be fully assessed.
