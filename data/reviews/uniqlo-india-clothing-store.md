## Summary

UNIQLO India's privacy policy is a standard retail privacy policy. It collects personal information for order processing, marketing, and fraud prevention. It uses cookies and device identifiers for personalized ads and builds individual profiles for product recommendations. Data is shared with parent company Fast Retailing, affiliates, and service providers. Notably, the app does not sell personal information and promises not to materially weaken privacy protections without consent. The policy is silent on camera, contacts, storage, and health data.

Key concerns: third-party cookies for marketing, individual profile building for recommendations, GPS location collection, unique device identifier collection.

Key positives: explicitly does not sell data, material changes require consent, uses SSL encryption, marketing emails can be unsubscribed.

## Summary Card

### UNIQLO India: Clothing Store
- **Overall Risk:** Caution
- **Score:** 65/100 (Grade C)

**Red Flags:**
- **Third-party cookies for marketing.** UNIQLO uses cookies from third-party partners for marketing and analytical purposes, which may track you across websites.
- **Builds individual profile for recommendations.** UNIQLO creates an individual profile for you to understand your preferences and offer products of your interest, using your browsing and purchase data.
- **Collects precise GPS location.** With your consent, UNIQLO collects your precise GPS location to show nearby stores and provide location-based services.
- **Collects unique device identifier.** UNIQLO collects a unique identifier for your device to recognize your browser and provide personalized features.

**Green Flags:**
- **Explicitly does not sell data.** UNIQLO explicitly states it is not in the business of selling, renting, sharing, or otherwise disclosing personal information to third parties except as set out in the privacy policy.
- **Material changes require consent.** UNIQLO promises it will never materially change its policies to make them less protective of customer information collected in the past without the consent of affected customers.
- **Uses SSL encryption.** UNIQLO uses Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) software to encrypt information you input during transmission.
- **Marketing emails can be unsubscribed.** You can unsubscribe from UNIQLO's marketing emails by clicking the unsubscribe link at the bottom of their emails.

**Verdict:** UNIQLO India collects standard shopping data and uses cookies for personalized ads, but explicitly states it does not sell your personal information and limits sharing to affiliates and service providers. If you are concerned about cookie-based tracking, personalized ads, and data sharing with a Japanese parent company, consider the trade-off before installing.
