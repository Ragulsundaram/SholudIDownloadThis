## Summary

Badoo is a global dating app operated by Badoo Software Ltd, a subsidiary of Bumble Inc. Its privacy policy is comprehensive and transparent, revealing extensive data collection and sharing practices typical of a major dating platform.

Badoo collects extensive personal information including your name, email, phone number, gender identity, sexual preference, photos, precise GPS location, WiFi access points, device information, and even biometric data for verification. This data is shared with numerous third parties including Facebook (as a joint data controller for marketing), OpenAI (for AI icebreaker messages), advertising partners, marketing service providers (for "lookalike" audiences), and various service providers for verification, moderation, and analytics.

Key concerns: facial biometric data collected for photo verification; government ID photos may be collected; photo interaction data is analyzed and you cannot opt out; extensive third-party sharing including with Facebook and marketing providers; data retained up to 15 years for blocked accounts; student targeting based on campus location for US/Canada users.

Key positives: GDPR compliant with DPO contact; detailed transparent policy; AI safety tools (Private Detector, Deception Detector); account deletion with 28-day restore window; clear data retention periods.

## Methodology

This analysis was conducted with strict legal-safe rules: every claim is backed by a direct verbatim quote from the Badoo Privacy Policy (last updated March 13, 2026), cautious language is used for uncertain areas.

### Data Collection Observed

- **Identity:** Name, username, email, phone number, gender identity, date/place of birth, sexual preference
- **Photos:** Profile photos, verification photos, ID photos
- **Biometric:** Facial recognition data for photo verification; government ID scans in some regions
- **Location:** GPS coordinates, WiFi access points, longitude/latitude
- **Device:** Unique identifier, model, OS, log/usage data, apps installed (Android only)
- **Financial:** Credit/debit card number, expiration, CVV, billing address
- **Behavioral:** Swiping behavior, photo interaction data (views, yes/no votes), profile visits
- **Messages:** Message content, video/audio call usage data
- **Third-party:** Facebook/Spotify login data

## Summary Card

**App:** Badoo Dating: Meet New People
**Overall Risk:** Risky — 52/100 (Grade D)

### Red Flags
- **Collects biometric data and government ID for verification.** Badoo collects your facial biometric data and may ask for government-issued ID photos to verify your identity. While manual review is an option, facial recognition technology is used by default for photo verification.
- **Shares data with numerous third parties including OpenAI and Facebook.** Badoo shares your data with many third parties including OpenAI for AI icebreaker messages, Facebook for advertising, and various marketing service providers who build "lookalike" audiences from your behavior.
- **Analyzes your photos and you can't opt out.** Badoo tracks how long people look at your photos, which photos get "yes" votes, and lighting quality to reorder your photos for better performance. You cannot opt out of this feature.
- **Keeps blocked users' data for up to 15 years.** If your account is blocked for serious violations, Badoo may keep your information for up to 15 years to prevent you from creating a new account.
- **Targets students based on campus location checks.** For US and Canadian students aged 18-24, Badoo checks if your location matches a college campus area to send targeted offers and features.

### Green Flags
- **GDPR compliant with clear rights and DPO contact.** Badoo has a Data Protection Officer, provides EU model clauses for data transfers, and clearly outlines your GDPR rights including access, deletion, and portability.
- **Very detailed and transparent privacy policy.** The privacy policy is comprehensive, well-organized, and explains what data is collected, why it's used, how long it's kept, and who it's shared with in clear tables.
- **Uses AI safety tools like Private Detector and Deception Detector.** Badoo uses AI to automatically blur potential nude images in chats and to detect and block fake, scam, and spam accounts before they can harm users.
- **Account deletion available with 28-day restore window.** You can delete your account, and for 28 days you can restore it if you change your mind. Most profile data is deleted 28 days after account deletion.

### Verdict
Badoo collects very personal data including sexual preference and biometric information, then shares aggregated behavioral data with advertisers for targeted ads. While the privacy policy is unusually transparent and detailed, the sheer volume of data collection — facial recognition, government ID scans, photo interaction analysis, swipe behavior tracking, and extensive third-party sharing — makes this a high-privacy-risk dating app. Consider whether you're comfortable with this level of data exposure before signing up.
