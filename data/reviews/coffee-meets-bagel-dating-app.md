## Summary Card

**App:** Coffee Meets Bagel Dating App  
**Developer:** COFFEE MEETS BAGEL PTE. LTD.  
**Overall Risk:** Risky (Score: 54/100, Grade: D)

### Red Flags
- **Facial geometry sent to third-party for verification:** If you complete identity verification, the app sends your facial geometry — a measurement of your face — to a company called Persona. This is sensitive biometric data that can be used to identify you. Persona must delete it within three years, but during that time your face data is held by an outside company.
- **External advertisers track you across devices:** While the app says it does not sell your personal data, it allows outside advertising companies to collect information about you to show you targeted ads on different devices and apps you use.
- **Collects sensitive personal attributes:** The app asks for information about your ethnicity and sexual orientation as part of your profile. This is highly personal data that could be used to discriminate against you if exposed.
- **Imports data from Facebook and other outside sources:** If you sign in with Facebook or link your account, Coffee Meets Bagel pulls in information about you from Facebook and other outside companies.
- **Do-Not-Track signals are ignored:** If you turn on the Do-Not-Track setting, Coffee Meets Bagel explicitly says it does not act on it. They will keep tracking you anyway.

### Green Flags
- **Biometric data must be deleted within three years:** The company that handles identity verification, Persona, is required to destroy your facial geometry data within three years of your last use of the service.
- **You can delete your account and request data removal:** You can edit or deactivate your profile and request permanent deletion. You also have the right to access, correct, transfer, restrict, or delete the records the app holds about you.
- **Can opt out of marketing emails and texts:** You can stop receiving promotional emails and text messages by using the unsubscribe links provided.
- **Claims not to sell personal information:** The app states it does not sell your personal information to third parties, and does not sell or share data of users under 16.

### Verdict
Coffee Meets Bagel is a relationship-focused dating app that collects extensive personal information including your profile details, photos, chat records, billing information, and sensitive attributes such as ethnicity and sexual orientation. The app sends your facial geometry to Persona, a third-party identity verification provider, for verification checks. It also imports data from Facebook and other external sources, tracks your activity via cookies and pixel tags, and allows external advertisers to collect data for behavioural ad campaigns across devices. Your profile is shown to potential matches, and your data may be shared with service vendors, authorities, and successors in a business sale. On the positive side, Persona must delete biometric records within three years, you can request account deletion and exercise data subject rights, and marketing communications can be opted out of. However, the combination of biometric sharing, extensive ad tracking, and sensitive data collection makes this a high-risk app from a privacy perspective.
