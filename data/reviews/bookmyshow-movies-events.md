## Summary Card

### Red Flags
- **Privacy policy has not been updated since October 2020** — BookMyShow's privacy policy was last updated in October 2020, nearly 6 years ago. This means it may not accurately reflect how your data is currently being used.
- **Your data is shared with advertising networks and social media platforms** — BookMyShow shares your personal data with advertising networks and social media platforms so they can show you targeted ads. They also share with search engines and analytics providers.
- **App extensively tracks what you view, click, and search for** — BookMyShow logs every page you view, how long you spend, what you search for, what you click on, and the services you use. This information is used to profile you and personalize content.
- **App collects detailed device and hardware information** — BookMyShow collects your browser type, IP address, operating system, device ID, cookies, hardware information, software information, and demographic data to build a profile of your device.
- **Users under 18 are technically not allowed but no verification exists** — BookMyShow says you must be over 18 to use the service, but there is no age verification system. By using the app, you automatically agree that your legal guardian has consented.

### Green Flags
- **Payment data is protected by PCI DSS certification** — BookMyShow is PCI DSS certified, which means your payment data is secured according to globally accepted data security standards.
- **You can opt out of marketing emails at any time** — You can unsubscribe from marketing and promotional materials using the unsubscribe function in emails or by contacting customer support.
- **You can request updates to your personal data** — You can contact BookMyShow to update your personal information, and they will try to process your request.
- **Dedicated customer grievance and data protection officer** — BookMyShow has a dedicated Customer Grievance Officer who also serves as the Data Protection Officer for GDPR purposes.

### Verdict
BookMyShow makes booking tickets easy, but it tracks your behavior extensively and shares your data with advertising networks and social media platforms. While it has strong payment security (PCI DSS certified), its privacy policy is severely outdated and lacks modern transparency. If you only need to browse events without creating an account, you expose yourself to less tracking.
