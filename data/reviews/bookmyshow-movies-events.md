### Red Flags
- **Privacy policy has not been updated since October 2020** — The policy states it was last updated in October 2020. It may not reflect current practices.
- **Shares data with advertising networks and social media platforms** — The policy states that BookMyShow may share your data with advertising networks and social media platforms for selecting and serving relevant adverts, and with search engine and analytics providers.
- **Tracks what you view, click, and search for** — The policy states that BookMyShow collects the pages and videos you view, how long you spend on them, what you search for, what you click on, and the services you use.
- **Vague data retention policy** — The policy states that BookMyShow retains personal data for "a variety of legitimate legal or business purposes" but does not clearly explain exactly how long or what those purposes are.

### Green Flags
- **Payment data is protected by PCI DSS certification** — The policy states that BookMyShow is PCI DSS certified, which means the data you submit is secured according to globally accepted data security standards.
- **You can opt out of marketing emails** — The policy states that you can unsubscribe from receiving marketing information at any time by using the unsubscribe function within the electronic marketing material.
- **You can request updates to your personal data** — The policy states that you may update your personal data by contacting helpdesk@bookmyshow.com.
- **Dedicated customer grievance and data protection officer** — The policy states that BookMyShow has a Customer Grievance Officer who also serves as the Data Protection Officer for GDPR purposes.

### Verdict
BookMyShow's privacy policy has not been updated since 2020 and states that your data may be shared with advertising networks and social media platforms. The policy is also silent on several data categories including camera, contacts, storage, and health data. Consider the privacy trade-off before creating an account.
