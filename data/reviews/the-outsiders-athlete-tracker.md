**Score:** 95 / 100 (Grade A)

### Red Flags
- **Workout photo times saved to your iCloud.** The app saves just the creation time and date of your photos (not the photos themselves) to your private iCloud so it can match them to workouts.
- **Email deletion requires contacting support.** If you sign up for the newsletter with your email, removing it later requires sending a manual request to the company's support team.

### Green Flags
- **All health data stays on your device.** The app reads your workouts, heart rate, and other health data from Apple Health, but it never stores, caches, or sends any of it to a server.
- **Health data never shared with external AI.** The developer explicitly promises that your health and workout data will not be processed or shared with any outside AI companies.
- **Health data never used for ads or marketing.** The app promises it will never use your health data to show you advertisements or profile you for commercial purposes.
- **Workout photo matching happens only on your device.** When the app links photos to your workouts, it does all the work locally without uploading anything.
- **Developer promises no surprise privacy changes.** The privacy policy says the company will not change the rules to allow new data collection without first getting your consent.

### Verdict
The Outsiders is one of the most privacy-respecting fitness apps we've analyzed. It reads extensive Apple HealthKit data to calculate training readiness, load ratios, and workout zones, but keeps every byte of it on your device. The developer makes explicit, unambiguous promises: no server storage, no external AI processing, no marketing use, no third-party sharing. The only data the company ever receives is an optional email for their newsletter. For privacy-conscious athletes, this is an easy recommendation.

## Review Notes

- The privacy policy is exceptionally clear and concise. It focuses almost entirely on the app's local-only data architecture.
- The developer makes **three strong explicit promises** that are rare in fitness apps: (1) health data is never stored or cached by the app, (2) health data is never sent to any server, and (3) health data is never processed or shared with external AI providers.
- The app is **free** with no in-app purchases or subscriptions mentioned.
- The developer, Gentler Stories LLC, is the same team behind **Gentler Streak** (Apple Watch App of the Year & Apple Design Award winner), suggesting an established track record.
- The only personal data the developer collects is an **optional email address** for the app launch newsletter. GDPR compliance is mentioned for EU users.
- The **17+ App Store rating** is appropriate for a performance-focused athletic training app, and no child-specific concerns arise.
- The app's reliance on Apple HealthKit means it requires >90 days of workout history to provide meaningful insights, as noted in the App Store description.
- Minor deductions applied for: Location GPS (reads workout routes via HealthKit, though local-only), Storage File Access (reads photo library timestamps, though local-only), and Data Deletion (email removal requires manual support contact).
