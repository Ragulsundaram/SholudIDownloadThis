## Summary Card

**App:** Bumble
**Overall Risk:** Risky

**Grade:** D

### Red Flags
- **Collects facial biometrics if you opt in** — The policy states that if you choose photo or ID verification, Bumble scans your photo using facial recognition technology and keeps verification scans for up to 3 years.
- **Reviews message content for topics and sentiments** — The policy states that Bumble reviews the content of messages to identify topics, sentiments, and trends, but claims to remove personally identifying information before reviewing.
- **Shares data with many vendors** — The policy states that Bumble shares your data with numerous companies including Veriff, Twilio, Zendesk, Google Cloud, OpenAI, and advertising partners.
- **Keeps some data for years after deletion** — The policy states that even after you delete your account, purchase records may be kept for 10 years, blocked account evidence for up to 15 years, and help chats for 6 years.
- **Shares data with advertisers** — The policy states that Bumble shares your device information, age, gender, and behavior with advertising partners and marketing service providers to show you targeted ads.

### Green Flags
- **Biometric verification is optional** — The policy states that photo verification and ID verification are optional. You only go through them if you choose to.
- **User data rights available** — The policy states that you can request to access, correct, delete, or transfer your data. European and some U.S. users have additional legal protections.
- **Email notice for material policy changes** — The policy states that Bumble will let you know by email or notice of any material changes to the privacy policy.
- **You can opt out of marketing and targeted ads** — The policy states that you can withdraw consent for marketing and opt out of targeted advertising through app settings or device privacy preferences.

### Verdict
Bumble collects extensive personal data including optional biometrics, reviews message content, and shares data with advertisers and numerous vendors. Some data may be retained for up to 15 years in severe cases. If you use the app, be aware of what you share in messages and consider whether the privacy trade-off is acceptable. Avoid the optional face verification if privacy is a concern.
