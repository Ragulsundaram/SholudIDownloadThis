## Summary Card

**Overall Risk:** Caution  
**Score:** 60 / 100 (Grade C)

### Red Flags
- **Sensitive health and biometric data collected:** KIC collects information about your physical and mental health, fitness assessments, medical clearances, and biometric data like fingerprints for studio access. Your image and voice may also be captured during classes for commercial use.
- **Location tracked even when app is closed:** The KIC app can access your location while it runs in the background on your device, which can drain your battery and continuously track your movements.
- **Purchases and usage data used to track you across apps:** KIC's App Store label shows that your purchases, device identifiers, usage data, and diagnostics may be used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies.
- **Your image and voice may be used for commercial content:** If you attend studio classes, your image, voice, and likeness may be filmed or photographed for commercial purposes, such as promotional videos or social media content.
- **Studio CCTV captures your image for security:** KIC uses CCTV systems at studio premises that record images and footage for security and safety purposes.
- **Data stored and processed overseas:** Your personal data may be transferred to and stored in Australia, the United States, New Zealand, and the EU, which means different privacy laws may apply.

### Green Flags
- **Explicit no-sale commitment:** KIC explicitly states that it does not sell personal information in any manner.
- **AI inputs not used to train models:** KIC states it does not use data or inputs submitted through AI Features to train its own AI models, and configures AI features so third-party providers do not use them to train underlying models either.
- **Deletion rights available on request:** You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing hello@kicwellness.com.
- **GDPR-aligned disclosures for EEA/UK residents:** KIC provides detailed Schedule 1 disclosures for EEA and UK residents, including lawful bases for processing, data subject rights, and information about Standard Contractual Clauses for international transfers.

### Verdict
KIC Wellness's privacy policy reveals extensive data collection including sensitive health and fitness information, biometric data for studio access, and your image/voice captured during classes for commercial purposes. The app may track your location in the background, uses third-party cookies from Facebook and Google for targeted ads, and shares data with AI infrastructure and cloud providers across multiple countries including Australia, the US, and the EU. On the positive side, KIC explicitly states it does not sell personal data, does not use AI inputs to train its own models, and allows you to request deletion of your data.
