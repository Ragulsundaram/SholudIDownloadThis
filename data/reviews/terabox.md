## Summary Card

**App:** TeraBox: 1TB Cloud & AI Space
**Overall Risk:** Risky (Grade D)
**Score:** 52/100

### Red Flags
- **Builds behavioral profiles for automated marketing** - Creates "label information" about your operation habits, interests, or credit for automated decision-making
- **Uses AI to categorize your uploaded photos** - Automated clustering of photos based on people, scenes, and geographic locations
- **Sends your audio to Google for transcription** - Audio content transmitted to Google for processing when using transcription features
- **May read your clipboard** - Reads clipboard for invitation codes, file links, passwords, and redemption codes
- **Stores your contacts on servers if allowed** - Contact information including social media contacts stored on TeraBox servers

### Green Flags
- **Encrypts files at rest** - Uses encryption as part of security measures
- **Account deletion available in app** - Can delete account directly in app settings
- **Can opt out of marketing emails** - Unsubscribe link available in emails
- **Can withdraw permissions in device settings** - Direct control over contacts, photos, camera access

### Verdict
TeraBox offers free cloud storage but at a significant privacy cost. Your files are scanned by AI, audio is sent to Google, and your behavior may be profiled for marketing. Consider alternatives with stronger privacy commitments like encrypted cloud storage services.

## Analysis Notes

### Key Findings

**1. Behavioral Profiling for Marketing**
TeraBox explicitly states it may process personal information to create profiles about your:
- Operation habits
- Interests
- Credit (creditworthiness)

These profiles are used for "automated decision-making" to send personalized marketing information. Users can refuse via contact or app settings.

**2. AI Photo Categorization**
TeraBox provides "smart classification services" including:
- Automated clustering of photos based on people
- Scene recognition
- Geographic location identification

This means all your uploaded photos are analyzed by AI.

**3. Audio Processing with Google**
When using transcription or note-taking features:
- Audio content is transmitted to TeraBox servers
- Then sent to Google for processing
- Google is described as their "third-party AI service provider"

**4. Clipboard Access**
TeraBox reads clipboard information for:
- Registration using invitation codes
- Accessing file links shared by other users
- Adding friends through passwords
- Obtaining membership services through redemption codes

**5. Contact Data Storage**
If you grant contact access:
- Phone contacts stored on TeraBox servers
- Email addresses stored
- Social media contacts (Facebook, Twitter) stored
- Used for sharing and collaboration features

**6. Facial Recognition Contradiction**
The policy contains a direct contradiction:
- States you "must provide your real name, identity document number, and facial recognition information for account authentication"
- Immediately claims "We do not collect or store any users' facial biometric information"

**7. Data Sharing**
Data shared with:
- Google (for audio transcription)
- Flextech affiliates (infrastructure, systems, technology)
- Third-party service providers (customer support, IT)
- Other users (via Teamwork/Share functions)
- Cross-border transfers to Japan

**8. Security Measures**
- Encryption of files at rest
- Two-factor authentication
- Alerts when new devices link to accounts
- Automated technology to detect abusive behavior
- Security background checks on personnel

**9. Data Retention**
- Data retained as long as account exists
- Account deletion takes up to 30 days
- May be delayed for:
  - Identity verification
  - Backup deletion
  - Legal obligations
  - Dispute resolution

**10. User Rights**
- Right of access
- Right of rectification and supplementation
- Right of erasure (account deletion)
- Right of withdrawal of consent
- Right of account deletion
- Right of copy and data portability

**11. Age Requirements**
- Users must be at least 13 years old
- No described age verification mechanism

### Policy Change Notification
- Revisions posted on TeraBox website
- Notification only if revision "materially affects your rights"
- No explicit opt-out mechanism for changes described

## Verbatim Policy Excerpts

**On Behavioral Profiling:**
> "You agree that we can aggregate, integrate, identify, analyze, portray, or further process the personal information actively or passively provided by you in the Services through the technology of us or our trustees to form a variety of label information with different degrees of granularity that reflect your operation habits, interests, or credit... We will use label information for automated decision-making in specific scenarios and applications, so as to send you personalized information and/or marketing information."

**On AI Photo Categorization:**
> "we provide smart classification services based on User Data (including automated clustering of photos based on people, scenes, and geographic locations)"

**On Audio Processing:**
> "To provide transcription and note generation services, your audio content is transmitted to Google, our third-party AI service provider, for processing."

**On Clipboard Access:**
> "When you register a Terabox account using someone's invitation code, access file links shared by other users, add friends through passwords, or obtain membership services through redemption codes, we will read your clipboard information."

**On Contacts:**
> "You can choose to allow TeraBox to access your contact information to facilitate certain operations... We store this information on our server for you to use."

**On Facial Recognition (Contradiction):**
> "you must provide your real name, identity document number, and facial recognition information for account authentication... We do not collect or store any users' facial biometric information."

**On Encryption:**
> "In addition to two-factor authentication, encryption of files at rest, and alerts when new devices and apps link to your accounts, we continue to work hard to ensure the security of your information."

**On Account Deletion:**
> "You may delete the account that you have created. You can delete your account in TeraBox at: [Account Avatar] - [Menu] - [Settings] - [Security Center] - [Account Deletion]."
