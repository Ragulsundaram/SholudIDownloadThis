# TeraBox Review

**App:** TeraBox: 1TB Cloud & AI Space
**Analyzed:** 2026-05-16
**Policy URL:** https://www.terabox.com/terms/privacy?lang=en
**Terms URL:** https://www.terabox.com/terms/duty?lang=en

---

## Summary Card

- **Overall risk:** Risky (Score: 52, Grade: D)

### Red Flags
- **Builds behavioral profiles for automated marketing.** TeraBox explicitly states that it may process your personal information to create profiles about your operation habits, interests, or credit, and uses these for automated decision-making to send you personalized or marketing information.
- **Uses AI to categorize your uploaded photos.** TeraBox states that it provides smart classification services that automatically cluster your uploaded photos based on people, scenes, and geographic locations.
- **Sends your audio to Google for transcription.** When you use transcription or note-taking features, TeraBox transmits your audio content to Google, its third-party AI service provider, for processing.
- **May read your clipboard for specific features.** TeraBox states that it will read your clipboard when you use invitation codes, file links, passwords, or redemption codes.
- **Stores your contacts on its servers if you allow access.** If you grant contact access, TeraBox stores this information on its server.

### Green Flags
- **Encrypts files at rest.** TeraBox states that it uses encryption of files at rest as part of its security measures.
- **Account deletion available in app.** You can delete your account directly within the app settings.
- **Can opt out of marketing emails.** You can unsubscribe from marketing material by clicking the unsubscribe link in emails.

### Verdict
TeraBox offers generous free cloud storage, but its privacy policy reveals significant trade-offs. The app explicitly builds behavioral profiles for marketing, scans your photos with AI, and sends audio to Google. While it provides encryption and deletion options, the breadth of data collection and sharing is concerning. Consider whether the free storage is worth the privacy cost.
