## Summary Card

- **App:** Cleartrip Flights, Hotels, Bus
- **Overall Risk:** Risky
- **Score:** 46 / 100
- **Grade:** D

### Red Flags

- **Uses your data to train AI models:** If you use the app's AI chatbot or other AI features, your conversations and behavior may be used to train artificial intelligence models. The policy warns that AI can produce incorrect or misleading results.
- **Reads your text messages:** With your permission, the app can access SMS messages on your phone for things like verification codes, booking updates, and checking if you qualify for certain services.
- **Collects passport and government ID:** The app collects your passport number and other government-issued identification to verify your identity for bookings. This sensitive data is stored and processed on their servers.
- **Shares your data for targeted ads:** The app shares your personal data with advertising companies to show you targeted ads. These partners may also collect your device ID and advertising ID directly from your phone to track you across other apps and websites.
- **Collects your payment card details:** The app collects your payment card details and billing address as part of the booking process. While payment gateways process the actual transaction, Cleartrip still handles this sensitive financial information.

### Green Flags

- **You can delete your account:** You can delete certain information and your user account directly through the website by visiting the profile and settings sections.
- **Marketing emails can be opted out:** You can unsubscribe from promotional and marketing communications by clicking the unsubscribe link in emails or writing to the data protection officer.
- **Does not knowingly collect data from children:** The app states it is only for adults who can legally contract, and it does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 years old.

### Verdict

Cleartrip is a popular and functional travel booking app, but its privacy practices are concerning. The combination of AI training on user data, SMS reading, passport collection, behavioral advertising, and broad data sharing with partners makes it a risky choice for privacy-conscious travelers. While account deletion and marketing opt-outs are available, the extensive data collection and sharing practices outweigh these limited protections.

## Detailed Category Analysis

### Data Sharing with 3rd Parties — Risky (19.5 pts)

Data is shared internally with group companies and externally with third-party service providers (airlines, hotels, cloud providers, payment gateways, customer service), advertising companies for behavioral advertising, professional advisors, government agencies, and potential acquirers. Affiliates may market to users unless explicitly opted out. Partners may collect device ID, advertising ID, and IP address directly from the user's device.

### Behavioural & Ad Tracking — Risky (16.25 pts)

Google Analytics, Microsoft Advertising, and third-party advertising companies are used. Cookies track page views, clicks, and search terms for ad personalization. Third-party advertising companies may use information about visits to this and other websites for targeted ads. Partners collect device ID, advertising ID, and browser/OS information.

### Contacts & Phonebook — Risky (13 pts)

Contacts of people added to reservations are collected. SMS messages are accessed on the device with explicit permission for registration, verification, security alerts, OTPs, booking status updates, and eligibility assessment. While permission-based, the SMS access grants broad message visibility.

### Account & Identity Data — Risky (9.75 pts)

Extensive identity data is collected including name, age, birthday, gender, contact info, passport number, country of citizenship, visa details, login credentials, and government-issued identification. Social media data may also be collected if accounts are linked.

### Device Info & Fingerprinting — Risky (9.75 pts)

Partners collect device ID, advertising ID, IP address, and browser/operating system information directly from the user's device. Cookies and similar technologies are used for tracking. Microsoft Advertising and Google Analytics are explicitly named.

### Health & Biometric Data — Risky (9.75 pts)

Sensitive health data (e.g., vaccination status) is explicitly mentioned as collected when required for travel. There is limited clarity on how this data is protected or shared.

### Financial & Payment Data — Risky (9.75 pts)

Payment card details and billing address are explicitly listed as collected data. While payment gateways are mentioned as third-party processors, the policy does not state that Cleartrip never sees or stores raw card data.

### Storage & File Access — Risky (6.5 pts)

SMS messages and Photo Gallery are accessed on the device with explicit permission. This constitutes broad file access beyond simple user-initiated selections.

### Children's Data Handling — Caution (3 pts)

The platform is stated to be available only to adults who can form legally binding contracts. The app says it does not knowingly collect data from children under 18. However, there is no technical enforcement mechanism.

### Data Deletion & User Rights — Caution (3 pts)

Users can access, correct, and update personal data through website functionalities. Non-mandatory information and user accounts can be deleted via profile and settings sections. The data protection officer (dpo@cleartrip.com) can assist with requests. Consent can be withdrawn by email.

### Location & GPS — Caution (4.5 pts)

Location is accessed with explicit permission for location-based services like finding nearby hotels or airports. No mention of background or continuous location tracking.

### Camera & Microphone — Caution (4.5 pts)

Camera and Photo Gallery are accessed with explicit permission, mainly for uploading photos like profile pictures or travel documents. No microphone access is mentioned.

### Network & Internet Activity — Caution (1.5 pts)

Cookies analyze web page flow and measure promotional effectiveness. Page views, clicks, and search terms are stored for ad personalization. Partners collect IP address and device information.

### Policy Change Notification — Caution (1.5 pts)

Changes are posted on the platform with the last updated date. Significant changes are alerted by posting the date, placing a notice, or sending an email when required by law. Continued access or use is deemed acceptance.
