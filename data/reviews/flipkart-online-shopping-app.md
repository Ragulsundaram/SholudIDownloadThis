## Summary

Flipkart's privacy policy reveals extensive data collection and broad sharing practices. The app gathers everything from browsing behavior and device fingerprints to government IDs and health prescriptions, then shares insights across a wide network of affiliates, advertisers, and lending partners.

Key concerns: broad data sharing with group companies and third-party advertisers, collection of PAN and KYC documents for credit profiling, behavioral tracking across websites, health data shared with teleconsultants, and financial data used to qualify users for loans.

Key positives: users can opt out of personalized ads via device settings, a dedicated grievance officer is listed with contact details, and sensitive permissions like camera and contacts require explicit consent.

## Summary Card

### Flipkart - Online Shopping App
- **Overall Risk:** Risky
- **Score:** 46/100 (Grade D)

**Red Flags:**
- **Shares data widely with affiliates and advertisers.** Flipkart may share your personal data with group companies, affiliates, business partners, and third-party advertisers. These partners can use your data for their own marketing, product development, and to personalize your experience on their platforms too.
- **Collects government IDs and KYC details.** The app asks for sensitive documents like your PAN card, GST number, and government-issued ID for credit checks and KYC verification. This creates a detailed identity profile tied to your shopping activity.
- **Tracks your behavior across websites.** Flipkart records the websites you visit before and after using the app, your IP address, and your browsing patterns. They also use third-party advertising companies that may track you across other apps and sites to show personalized ads.
- **Shares health data with third-party doctors.** If you buy medicines, Flipkart may share your prescription and health information with third-party teleconsultants and medical practitioners to validate or reissue your prescription.
- **Uses payment data for credit profiling.** Your payment details and transaction history may be used to check your eligibility for loans, insurance, and credit products offered by Flipkart's lending partners and affiliates.

**Green Flags:**
- **You can opt out of personalized ads.** You can opt out of personalized ad tracking through your device's settings, which limits how third-party advertising companies use your data for targeting.
- **Has a dedicated privacy grievance officer.** Flipkart lists a specific grievance officer with a real email address and physical office location, making it easier to raise privacy complaints.
- **Sensitive permissions require your consent.** Access to your camera, contacts, location, and photo gallery is only granted if you explicitly give permission, rather than being forced on you automatically.

**Verdict:** Flipkart collects an unusually broad range of personal data for a shopping app, including government IDs and health information, and shares it widely across its corporate family and with advertisers. While you can opt out of some ad tracking and sensitive features require permission, the overall privacy risk is high. Think twice before installing if you value keeping your shopping and identity data tightly controlled.
