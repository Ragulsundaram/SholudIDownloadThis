## Summary

Decathlon India's privacy policy reveals broad data sharing practices and aggressive location tracking. The app collects your personal details for orders, tracks your device location in the background for personalized offers, and uses Google and Facebook remarketing to follow you across the web. Data is shared with corporate affiliates and may be transferred to a new owner if Decathlon is acquired.

Key concerns: background location tracking when app is closed, Google and Facebook remarketing across third-party websites, data shared with affiliates and may be transferred in merger/acquisition, Google Analytics with advertising demographics enabled.

Key positives: explicitly does not ask for OTP or CVV, self-service account deletion available via Account -> My Profile, users can opt out of marketing communications, claims physical and technological security measures.

This analysis follows the ShouldIDownloadThis parser rules: every claim is backed by a direct verbatim quote, cautious language is used, and categories are rated based on the explicit evidence in the policy.

### Data Collection Observed

- **Personal:** Mobile number, email, first name, last name, address, postal code, opt-in preferences (SMS/Email/WhatsApp)
- **Location:** Device location, including background access when app is closed for personalized offers
- **Device:** Device information, cookies placed on device
- **Behavioral:** Google AdWords remarketing, Facebook remarketing, Google Analytics Demographics and Interest Reporting, cookies for web page flow analysis and promotional effectiveness
- **Financial:** Payment data processed; explicitly does not collect OTP, CVV, banking passwords, or UPI IDs
- **Other:** Web page flow, browsing activity, user demographics and profile data

## Summary Card

### Decathlon Online Shopping App
- **Overall Risk:** Risky
- **Score:** 47/100 (Grade D)

**Red Flags:**
- **Tracks location when app is closed.** Decathlon may access your device location even when the app is closed to send personalized offers and enhance your shopping experience.
- **Google and Facebook remarketing.** Decathlon uses Google AdWords and Facebook remarketing to advertise to you on third-party websites based on your past visits to their site.
- **Shares data with affiliates and may transfer in acquisition.** Decathlon shares your personal information with its corporate entities and affiliates, and may transfer all of it to another business if Decathlon is ever merged or acquired.
- **Google Analytics with advertising demographics.** Decathlon uses Google Analytics with Demographics and Interest Reporting, which combines your browsing data with Google's advertising profile about you.

**Green Flags:**
- **Does not ask for OTP or CVV.** Decathlon explicitly states it does not ask for your net banking OTP, debit/credit card CVV, banking passwords, or UPI IDs.
- **Self-service account deletion available.** You can request deletion of your personal data and account through the Account -> My Profile section in the app or website.
- **Can opt out of marketing communications.** You can opt out of receiving marketing and promotional messages from Decathlon and its affiliates at any time.
- **Claims physical and technological security.** Decathlon states it stores and processes data on computers protected by physical and reasonable technological security measures per the Indian IT Act 2000.

**Verdict:** Decathlon India collects standard shopping data but tracks your location even when the app is closed, shares data with affiliates, and may transfer it in a merger or acquisition. If you are concerned about background location tracking, broad data sharing with affiliates, and the possibility of your data being transferred in a corporate sale, consider whether the sports gear convenience is worth the privacy trade-off.
