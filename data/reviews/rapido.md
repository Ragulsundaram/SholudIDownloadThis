## Summary Card

**App:** Rapido: Bike-Taxi, Auto & Cabs
**Overall Risk:** Caution (Grade C)
**Score:** 56/100

### Red Flags
- **Captains' location is collected when the app is closed** - Background location collected when On-Duty is enabled
- **Government IDs and selfies may be required for Captains** - Aadhaar, PAN, license, and real-time selfies collected
- **May collect health information from Captains** - Body temperature, symptoms, vaccination status
- **Your data may be shared for market research and other purposes** - Broad third-party sharing clause
- **Calls with Rapido are recorded for Captains** - Calls with Vendor Partners and Captains are recorded

### Green Flags
- **No targeted advertising** - Rapido explicitly states it does not use information for targeted advertising
- **Data deleted after 180 days** - After account termination, data retained for only 180 days before deletion or anonymization
- **Complies with Indian IT Act** - States compliance with Information Technology Act, 2000

### Verdict
Rapido collects extensive data, especially for Captains including government IDs, background location, and health information. However, it explicitly avoids targeted advertising and limits data retention to 180 days after account closure.

## Analysis Notes

### Key Findings

**1. Extensive Data Collection for Captains**
Captains (drivers) must provide:
- Government IDs: Aadhaar, PAN, driving license
- Vehicle documents: Registration, permit, fitness certificate, insurance, pollution certificate
- Real-time selfies for identity verification
- Background location when On-Duty (even when app closed)
- Health declarations: body temperature, symptoms, vaccination status
- Call recordings with Rapido

**2. Customer Data Collection**
Customers provide less data but still:
- Location during active trips (foreground only)
- Trip history linked to account
- Contact details for referrals
- Device information and usage patterns

**3. No Targeted Advertising**
Rapido explicitly states: "Please note, we do not use the information collected from you for targeted advertising." This is a significant positive.

**4. Data Sharing**
Data shared with:
- Third-party service providers (TPSPs)
- Business partners and analytics providers
- Government and law enforcement when required
- Co-branded service partners
- For market research and data analysis

**5. Data Retention**
- Active accounts: Data retained as long as account is active
- Terminated accounts: 180 days retention before deletion or anonymization
- Purpose: Resolve disputes, fraud investigation, legal compliance

**6. Access to Other Apps**
For Captains and Vendor Partners, Rapido may access other applications on the device to prevent and detect fraud.

**7. Policy Change Notification**
Changes effective immediately upon posting. Material changes notified by email or app notice. Continued use equals acceptance.

### User Rights
- **Access/Correction** - Can update information through account or contact Grievance Officer
- **Deletion** - Can cancel account and request deletion
- **Communication Preferences** - Can modify communications via account settings

### Privacy Controls
- Location permissions can be managed in device settings
- Communication preferences adjustable in account
- Account deletion available

## Verbatim Policy Excerpts

**On No Targeted Advertising:**
> "Please note, we do not use the information collected from you for targeted advertising."

**On Background Location for Captains:**
> "Rapido collects location data when the Rapido App is running in the foreground (app is open and on-screen) or background (app is not in use) in your mobile device. For Captains, Rapido collects location data only when the Captains have enabled the icon 'On-Duty' on the Rapido App."

**On Government ID Collection:**
> "Verification Information: If you are a Captain, we may collect... copies of government issued identification documents such as Aadhaar, Permanent Account Number, etc., license details, and other details (KYC)... If you are a Captain we may also require you to capture your real time self-clicked images (selfies)"

**On Health Data:**
> "If you are a Captain, we may collect certain health related Information and declarations such as body temperature, symptoms, vaccination status and other health conditions."

**On Data Retention:**
> "We will retain your Information including Personal Information and Usage Information (including geo-location, trip history, and transaction history) for a period of 180 days, to resolve disputes, conclude any activities related to cancellation of an account, investigate, or prevent fraud and other inappropriate activity related to your account"

**On Call Recording:**
> "If you are a Vendor Partner or a Captain, we will, additionally, record your calls with us made from the Device used to provide Services and related call details."

**On Access to Other Apps:**
> "In case you sign up as a Captain or a Vendor Partner, enable features that require Rapido's access to other applications on your Device aiming to prevent and detect fraud towards the Customers."

**On Policy Changes:**
> "Any changes will be effective immediately upon the posting of the revised Policy on the Rapido Platform. If we make any material changes, we will notify you by email (sent to the e-mail address specified in your account) or by means of a notice on the Rapido App prior to the change becoming effective."
