## Summary Card

**App:** Canva
**Overall Risk:** Risky
**Score:** 47 / 100
**Grade:** D

### Red Flags
- **Uses your content for AI training** — The policy states that Canva may analyze your activity, content, and media uploads to train its algorithms, models, and AI products, unless you are using Canva Education.
- **May obtain data from third-party sources** — The policy states that Canva may obtain information about you from third-party data providers and public sources, including your company, job title, and industry.
- **Shares data with ad platforms** — The policy states that Canva shares your information with external advertising partners and social media platforms to show you personalized ads on Canva and other sites.
- **Employers may gain account control** — The policy states that if you sign up with a work email, your employer or organization may be given control of your account and access to your content and designs.
- **Vague data retention policy** — The policy states that Canva keeps your data for a "commercially reasonable time" after you close your account, which is vague and does not clearly explain how long.

### Green Flags
- **Education accounts protected** — The policy states that if you are a student using Canva Education, your content will not be used for AI training and you will not see ads.
- **Does not sell direct data to ad networks** — The policy states that Canva does not sell or rent the personal information it collects directly from you to third-party ad servers or ad networks for those parties' own marketing purposes.
- **You can opt out of AI training** — The policy states that you can manage whether Canva uses your data for training AI in your privacy settings.
- **Email notice for material policy changes** — The policy states that Canva will notify you by email or on the website if there are material changes to how your personal information is collected, used, stored, or shared.

### Verdict
Canva collects extensive personal data including your designs and location, may use your content for AI training, and shares data with advertisers and vendors. The vague retention policy and possibility of employer account control are concerning. The policy is also silent on camera, contacts, and health data. If you use Canva, consider opting out of AI training and avoid signing up with a work email.
