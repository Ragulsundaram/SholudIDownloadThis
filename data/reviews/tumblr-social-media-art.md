## Summary Card

- **App:** Tumblr: Social Media & Art
- **Overall Risk:** Caution
- **Score:** 58 / 100
- **Grade:** C

### Red Flags
- **Your posts are public by default.** Everything you post on Tumblr can be seen by anyone on the internet, including search engines. Even if you delete your account, reblogs of your posts may live forever on other people's blogs.
- **Extensive activity and device tracking.** Tumblr tracks what you view, like, reblog, and reply to. They also collect your device type, browser info, IP address, and may assign your phone a unique tracking identifier.
- **Data shared for targeted advertising.** Tumblr shares your online identifiers, device activity, IP address, and approximate location with advertising partners to show you personalized ads.
- **Data flows to parent company Automattic.** Tumblr is owned by Automattic, and any information Tumblr collects can be shared with Automattic and its other companies like WordPress.com.
- **Uses Google Analytics and comScore.** Tumblr uses third-party analytics services like Google Analytics and comScore to track how people use the platform, and these services may place their own cookies on your device.
- **Camera settings extracted from your photos.** When you upload photos, Tumblr may read the hidden metadata in them — like what camera you used, when the photo was taken, and where — unless you strip it first.

### Green Flags
- **Privacy settings for blogs available.** You can make your blog password-protected or private, so only people you choose can see your posts.
- **Can opt out of personalized ads.** You can turn off personalized advertising in your privacy settings, though you'll still see generic ads.
- **Can download your data and content.** Tumblr lets you download a copy of your personal data and export all the content you've posted.
- **No direct sale of personal data.** Tumblr says they never share your information unless they have your permission, give you notice, or the data is anonymized.
- **Contact lists discarded after use.** If you upload your contacts to find friends on Tumblr, they say they discard the list immediately after checking.

### Verdict
Tumblr collects your posts, likes, reblogs, device info, IP address, and location data to personalize content and show you targeted ads. Your content is public by default and can be reshared by others indefinitely. Data is shared with parent company Automattic and advertising partners. However, Tumblr offers privacy settings for blogs, opt-out controls for personalized ads, and tools to download or delete your account.
