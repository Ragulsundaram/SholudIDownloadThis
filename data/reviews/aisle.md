## Summary

Aisle is an Indian dating app from Aisle Network Private Limited. Compared to most dating apps, Aisle has notably stronger privacy practices: it explicitly states it does not sell personal data to third parties, keeps chat messages confidential, and does not use advertising SDKs or behavioral tracking for ads.

Key concerns: external face recognition tool processes uploaded photos, profile data is visible to all users by design, chat messages are monitored with content filters, and approximate location is shared with nearby users.

Key positives: explicit no-sale policy, confidential chat messages, easy account deletion, no advertising or ad tracking, strong 18+ age requirement.

## Methodology

This review was conducted by analyzing the Aisle Privacy Policy and Terms of Use, accessed via jina.ai text extraction on 2026-05-12.

### Data Collection Observed

- **Personal:** Name, gender, date of birth, height, relationship status, religion, ethnicity, city of residence, interests, story, philosophy, work, icebreaker
- **Contact:** Mobile number (identifier), email address
- **Photos:** User-uploaded photos processed by external face recognition tool
- **Location:** Approximate geolocation (optional, shared with other users)
- **Device:** Browser type, device type, OS, ISP
- **Social Login:** Facebook/LinkedIn data linked to account
- **Activity:** Login information, chat messages, customer service correspondence
- **Behavioral:** App usage for match suggestions and promotional notifications

### Notable Excerpts

> "We will only disclose your Personal Data to third parties if there is a legal obligation to do so by a statutory authority from the appropriate jurisdiction. We do not sell your Personal Data to third parties."

> "We may use an external face recognition tool and other techniques to select the best pictures for Your Account."

> "Your chat messages are strictly confidential and is only processed in order to give you access to your chat-history."

> "We may also send you push notifications with suggestions of people you might like and other promotional material."
