---

## Summary

Aisle is an Indian dating app from Aisle Network Private Limited. It has notably stronger privacy practices than most dating apps: it explicitly states it does not sell personal data to third parties, keeps chat messages confidential, and allows easy account deletion.

Key concerns: the policy states it may use an external face recognition tool to analyze uploaded photos, profile data is visible to all users by design, chat messages are scanned with content filters, and approximate location may be shared with nearby users. The policy is silent on camera/microphone access.

Key positives: explicit no-sale policy, confidential chat messages, easy account deletion, strong 18+ age requirement with commitment to delete minor data.

---

## Methodology

This re-analysis was conducted with strict legal-safe rules: every claim is backed by a direct verbatim quote, cautious language is used, and categories not addressed by the policy are marked as unknown with needs_review.

### Data Collection Observed

- **Personal:** Name, gender, date of birth, height, relationship status, religion, ethnicity, city of residence, interests, story, philosophy, work
- **Contact:** Mobile number (identifier), email address
- **Photos:** User-uploaded photos; policy states they may be analyzed by an external face recognition tool
- **Location:** Approximate geolocation (optional, shared with other users if enabled, limited to 0.5 km accuracy)
- **Device:** Browser type, device type, OS, ISP, device ID
- **Social Login:** Facebook/LinkedIn data linked to account
- **Activity:** Login information, chat messages, customer service correspondence
- **Behavioral:** App usage for match suggestions and promotional notifications; tracking cookies with consent

### Notable Excerpts

> "We will only disclose your Personal Data to third parties if there is a legal obligation to do so by a statutory authority from the appropriate jurisdiction. We do not sell your Personal Data to third parties."

> "We may use an external face recognition tool and other techniques to select the best pictures for Your Account."

> "Your chat messages are strictly confidential and is only processed in order to give you access to your chat-history."

> "You can choose to share your geolocation... Please note that we never share accurate geolocations with Aisle users – the accuracy is limited to 0.5 kilometers at the most."

> "Aisle shall not collect data from minors. Should a Member lie about his/her date of birth... Aisle commits to deleting all data on the minor in question as soon as possible."

## Strict Rules Applied

- Camera & Microphone marked as **unknown** because the policy does not explicitly address in-app camera or microphone access.
- Storage & File Access marked as **safe** because the policy only describes user-initiated photo uploads, not broad file access.
- Financial & Payment marked as **safe** because the policy states in-app purchases are processed through the Software Store and does not claim Aisle stores payment data.
- Contacts marked as **safe** because the policy mentions selecting a contact to send an invitation but does not explicitly state Aisle collects or stores the contacts list.
- All plain-english summaries use cautious language ("states that," "may," "claims to") and are backed by verbatim quotes.
