## Summary Card

**App:** Sololearn: Learn to Code  
**Developer:** SoloLearn Inc.  
**Overall Risk:** Caution  
**Score:** 69/100  
**Grade:** C

### Red Flags

- **Shares data with advertisers:** Sololearn shares your information with advertisers in "non-personally identifiable" form. This includes your general location (like your city) and usage patterns. Advertisers use this to show you targeted ads, and if you click on an ad, they may be able to figure out you were part of their target audience.

- **AI chats sent to OpenAI:** When you use the AI chatbot "Kodie" or AI features in courses, everything you type is sent to OpenAI (the company behind ChatGPT). The app warns that if you include personal information in your messages, it will be transmitted to OpenAI and is governed by their privacy policy, not just Sololearn's.

- **Cross-site tracking via web beacons:** The app uses "web beacons" which allow ad networks to track your activity even after you leave Sololearn. These can monitor what websites you visit and serve targeted ads to you on other sites across the internet.

- **Multiple analytics companies track you:** Sololearn uses Google Analytics, Facebook analytics, and Fabric (also owned by Google) to track how you interact with the app. These services collect your IP address, browser info, pages viewed, time spent, and links clicked to build a profile of your behavior.

- **Data kept even after account deletion:** When you delete your account or information, Sololearn admits they may still keep copies of your data in their records. They also keep unrevised versions of information when you update your profile, meaning old data isn't fully replaced.

### Green Flags

- **Does not sell personal information:** Sololearn explicitly states they do not sell your personal information to third parties. This is a positive commitment compared to many apps that monetize by selling user data directly to data brokers.

- **Strong user rights under GDPR/CCPA:** Users have comprehensive rights including access, deletion, data portability, and the right to opt out of marketing. The app provides clear instructions for exercising these rights under GDPR (Europe), LGPD (Brazil), and CCPA (California).

- **Granular privacy settings available:** You can control what profile information other users see through account privacy settings. You can also opt out of marketing emails and manage cookie preferences through the app's cookie banner.

- **Encryption and security measures used:** The company states they use data encryption, anonymization, and restrict employee access to personal information. They also have strict confidentiality requirements for service providers who handle user data.

### Verdict

Sololearn offers valuable free coding education, but the cost is your data. While they don't sell your personal information directly, they extensively share anonymized data with advertisers and use multiple tracking technologies to monitor your activity both within the app and across the web. The AI chatbot "Kodie" sends all your queries to OpenAI, which is a significant privacy concern for a learning platform where users might unknowingly share personal details. The app provides good user rights and privacy controls, but privacy-conscious learners should be aware of the extensive data collection and consider whether the trade-off is worth it.

**Risk Level:** Caution  
**Access Type:** Collected automatically

**Concerns:**
- Location used for ad targeting
- Collected automatically via IP address

**Risk Level:** Safe

**Risk Level:** Risky  
**Plain English:** Your data is shared with advertisers, analytics companies (Google, Facebook), and service providers. While they claim it's "anonymized," advertisers can still use it to target you with ads based on your location and usage patterns.

**Policy Excerpt:**
> "we may provide any of the information we have collected from you in non-personally identifiable form to an Advertiser, in order for that Advertiser to select the appropriate audience for those advertisements"

**Concerns:**

- Data may be de-anonymized by advertisers

### Account & Identity Data
**Risk Level:** Caution  
**Plain English:** You need to provide your name, email, and age to create an account. You can also sign in with Facebook or Google, which shares your name and email with Sololearn. Your profile information is visible to other users unless you change your privacy settings.

**Policy Excerpt:**
> "we may collect information such as your name, age, email address, and third-party account credentials... when you log-in through your Google account, we automatically receive your name and email address"

**Concerns:**
- Profile visible to other users by default

**Policy Excerpt:**
> "Through cookies we place on your browser or device, we may collect information about your online activity after you leave our Services... cookies placed by third parties may continue to track your activities online even after you have left our Services"

**Concerns:**
- Cross-site tracking via web beacons
- Third-party ad cookies
- Activity tracked across multiple websites
