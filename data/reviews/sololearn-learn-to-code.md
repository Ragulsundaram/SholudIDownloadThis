## Summary Card

### Sololearn: Learn to Code
- **Overall Risk:** Caution
- **Score:** 65 / 100 (Grade C)

**Red Flags:**
- **AI chatbot sends your messages to OpenAI.** Anything you type in the AI chatbot Kodie or the AI playground is sent to OpenAI to generate responses. If you include personal info in those messages, OpenAI receives it too.
- **Targeted ads and cross-site tracking.** The app uses cookies and web beacons to show you targeted ads and can track your online activity even after you leave the app or website.
- **Demographic data shared with advertisers.** Your general location and usage patterns are shared with advertisers in a non-personally identifiable form so they can pick who sees their ads.
- **Some data kept after you delete your account.** Even if you ask Sololearn to delete your information or deactivate your account, some data may stay on their servers for legal, research, or business reasons.
- **Analytics providers track you across websites.** Google Analytics, Facebook, and other analytics services monitor how you use the app and other websites over time to build a profile of your interests.

**Green Flags:**
- **Explicitly says it does not sell personal data.** The company clearly states it does not sell your personal information to third parties, which is better than many free apps.
- **Offers GDPR and CCPA privacy rights.** If you live in the EU, Brazil, or certain US states, you can request access, deletion, or export of your data, and the company provides a contact email and form to do so.
- **Accounts are password protected.** Your account is secured with a password, and the company says it uses encryption and anonymization to help protect your information.
- **You can control what others see on your profile.** Privacy settings let you limit which other users can see your profile details, giving you some control over your public information.

**Verdict:** Sololearn is a useful coding education app, but its privacy practices have real drawbacks. Your data is shared with advertisers and analytics networks, your online activity is tracked across websites, and anything you share with its AI chatbot goes to OpenAI. While the app does offer data rights for GDPR and CCPA residents and claims it does not sell personal information, the extensive tracking and data retention after deletion mean privacy-conscious users should think carefully before signing up.

### 7. Security Measures

- Password protection for accounts

### 8. Children's Privacy

- Services not intended for users under 13

- No age verification mechanism described

### 9. Policy Changes

- Material changes notified by posting notice on website and app
- Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance
- No active re-consent required

### 10. International Data Transfers

- Personal data transferred to United States for processing and storage
- Standard contractual clauses or other lawful tools used for transfers
- Users consent to cross-border transmission by using the service
