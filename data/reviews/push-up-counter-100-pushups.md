## Summary Card

**App:** Push Up Counter - 100 Pushups  
**Overall Risk:** Caution  
**Score:** 67/100  
**Grade:** C

### Red Flags
- **Ad partners track you across websites and apps** — Foxyfitness allows advertising and analytics companies to use cookies, device identifiers, and web beacons to follow your activity on this app and other websites, then show you targeted ads based on what you do.
- **Device identifiers collected for advertising and analytics** — The app collects your device identifiers, IP address, mobile network info, and browsing behavior to share with advertising and analytics partners.
- **Your gender is guessed from your first name** — Foxyfitness uses your first name to make an educated guess about your gender. This inferred data is then used to personalize your experience and ads.
- **You must email support to delete your account** — There is no in-app or self-service account deletion. You have to contact help@foxyfitness.app to request deletion, and they may keep some data for legal or caching purposes.
- **Your data could be transferred in a business deal** — If Foxyfitness is sold, merges with another company, or raises financing, your personal information may be shared with counterparties or transferred to the new owner.

### Green Flags
- **Camera data for rep counting never leaves your device** — The app uses your TrueDepth camera and ARKit to count push-ups, but the data is not stored on your device, not sent to Foxyfitness servers, and not shared with anyone. It is processed in real time and immediately discarded.
- **Apple Health data stays entirely on your phone** — With your permission, the app reads your date of birth, height, weight, and sex from Apple Health only to show you stats inside the app. Foxyfitness explicitly promises this data is not collected or sent outside your local app installation.
- **Does not sell personal information under CCPA** — Foxyfitness states they do not sell the personal information they collect and will not do so in the future without giving you the right to opt out.
- **Full GDPR and CCPA rights available** — Depending on where you live, you can request access to your data, ask for corrections, request deletion, opt out of direct marketing, and file a complaint with your local data protection authority.

### Verdict
Push Up Counter is a simple fitness app that handles your most sensitive data remarkably well: camera data for rep counting is processed entirely on-device and discarded immediately, and Apple Health data is kept strictly local. The privacy downsides are the cross-app advertising trackers and the lack of self-service account deletion. It is a reasonable choice for a basic workout tracker if you are comfortable with the ad-tracking ecosystem.
