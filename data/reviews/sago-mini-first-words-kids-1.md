### Red Flags

- **Data shared with many service providers** — The app sends your data to a long list of companies including Mixpanel, Firebase, BigQuery, AppsFlyer, OneSignal, and Amazon Web Services for analytics, crash reporting, hosting, and marketing attribution.
- **Collects children's names and ages** — When you create a child profile, the app asks for your child's first name and age to customize difficulty levels and track progress.
- **Collects multiple device identifiers** — The app collects persistent identifiers like IDFV, App Set ID, and AppsFlyer ID to track how you use the app and measure the success of marketing campaigns.
- **Optional camera and microphone access** — Parents can choose to turn on camera, microphone, and speech recognition to improve the app's speech exercises. The good news is this data stays on your device and is never uploaded.

### Green Flags

- **COPPA and kidSAFE certified** — The app has been independently certified as compliant with COPPA (the US children's privacy law) and carries the kidSAFE seal, meaning it meets strict standards for protecting kids' privacy.
- **Camera and microphone data stays local** — If you allow camera, microphone, or speech recognition, that audio and video is processed right on your device and never sent to Sago Mini's servers.
- **No ads and no in-app purchases** — There are no advertisements inside the app and no extra purchases for your child to accidentally make while playing.
- **Explicitly says it does not sell data** — The company clearly states it does not sell your personal information to data brokers or third parties for marketing purposes.

### Verdict

Sago Mini First Words is a well-designed speech-learning app for very young children with strong safety certifications and no ads. Parents should feel reasonably confident, but be aware that the app collects more data than strictly necessary—including your child's name and age, plus extensive device tracking for analytics. The local-only processing of camera and microphone data is a significant positive.
