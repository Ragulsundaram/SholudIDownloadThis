## Summary Card

### Red Flags
- **Sexual orientation is mandatory to create an account** — The policy states that your gender and sexual orientation are mandatory information required to create a PURE account. This sensitive data is collected before you can use the app.
- **Staff may manually review your chat messages and photos** — The policy states that content shared in chat conversations, including pictures, videos, and text messages, may be reviewed by automated systems and human moderators to detect unlawful or prohibited content.
- **Your data may be transferred to many countries globally** — The policy states that your personal data may be processed by third parties in countries including Russia, India, Vietnam, the United States, and others.
- **Age verification shares data with third-party Veriff** — The policy states that age verification is carried out through Veriff, which may retain session data for up to 90 days in active storage and up to 3 years in archive. Veriff may also process verification data for its own purposes including machine learning training.
- **Staff may read your AI chatbot conversations** — The policy states that a limited number of PURE's personnel may manually access, review, and utilize your inputs to the AI chatbot to refine and enhance it.

### Green Flags
- **Biometric data from photo verification is not shared** — The policy states that biometric data collected during the photo verification comparison process will be securely stored on their servers and will not be shared with any third parties.
- **AI chatbot data is not used for marketing or ads** — The policy states that under no circumstances will PURE share users' inputs, collected data, or generated content from the AI chatbot for marketing or advertising purposes.
- **You can access, correct, delete, and export your data** — The policy states you have the right to access, rectify, delete, restrict processing, and obtain a portable copy of your personal data.
- **Policy states commitment to data minimization** — The policy states they adhere to the principles of data minimization and purpose limitation.

### Verdict
PURE's privacy policy states it collects gender, sexual orientation, date of birth, and optional biometric data for photo verification. The policy states that chat messages, photos, and videos may be reviewed by automated systems and human moderators for safety purposes. Your data may be transferred to service providers in countries including Russia, India, and the United States. The policy states it does not share user inputs or collected data for marketing or advertising purposes through its AI chatbot, and biometric data from photo verification is not shared with third parties.
