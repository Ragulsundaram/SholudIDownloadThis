## Summary Card

**App:** WhatsApp Messenger
**Overall Risk:** Caution (Grade C)
**Score:** 59/100

### Red Flags
- **WhatsApp shares information with Meta Companies for ads** - Your contacts, device data, and activity patterns are shared across the Meta ecosystem
- **You can provide phone numbers from your address book on a regular basis** - Contact upload includes non-users who haven't consented
- **WhatsApp collects device identifiers linked to Meta Products** - Hardware model, OS, battery level, signal strength, and identifiers unique to Meta Company Products
- **WhatsApp estimates your location from your IP and phone number** - Passive location inference even without using location features
- **No clear opt-out from Meta data sharing** - Policy does not explain how to reject data sharing while continuing to use the service

### Green Flags
- **End-to-end encryption on messages** - WhatsApp cannot read your personal messages or listen to calls
- **Delivered messages are deleted from servers** - Only undelivered messages retained for up to 30 days
- **No third-party banner ads inside the app** - WhatsApp does not allow third-party banner ads on its Services
- **You can delete your account and data** - Removes account info, profile photo, groups, and message history
- **Can opt out of Meta AI messaging** - Optional feature, users can choose whether to message with Meta AI

### Verdict
WhatsApp offers genuine end-to-end encryption for messages and calls, which is a significant privacy protection. However, the trade-off is substantial data sharing with Meta Companies including your contacts, usage patterns, and device information for advertising purposes. Consider whether you're comfortable with Meta's ecosystem integration.

## Analysis Notes

### Key Findings

**1. End-to-End Encryption**
- WhatsApp explicitly states: "Your personal messages and calls are end-to-end encrypted. No one else, not even WhatsApp, can read or listen to them."
- This applies to text, images, audio, video, documents, precise location sharing, and status updates
- Profile information (name, photo, "about") is NOT encrypted — WhatsApp can see this

**2. Meta Data Sharing (The Core Privacy Trade-off)**
WhatsApp shares data with Meta Companies for:
- Operational Support: Customer surveys, account security, query responses
- Technical Support: Service development, customization, troubleshooting  
- Safety & Security: Account removal, suspicious activity investigation
- Cross-app features: Showing relevant offers and ads across Meta Company Products

**What Meta does NOT see:**
- Your personal message content
- Your call content
- Precise location you share in chats

**What Meta CAN see:**
- Your phone number (if linked to Facebook/Instagram)
- How you use WhatsApp (usage patterns, not content)
- Device and connection information
- Contacts you upload
- Information when interacting with businesses using Meta services

**3. Contact Upload Feature**
- Policy states: "You can use the contact upload feature and provide us... with the phone numbers in your address book on a regular basis, including those of users of our Services and your other contacts."
- Includes contacts who don't use WhatsApp (non-users)
- Policy claims non-users "cannot be identified by us"
- Data may be shared with Meta Companies

**4. Passive Location Collection**
- Even without using location features: "we use IP addresses and other information like phone number area codes to estimate your general location (e.g., city and country)."
- No opt-out for this passive location inference

**5. Device Information Collection**
Extensive device data collected:
- Hardware model, OS information, battery level, signal strength
- App version, browser info, mobile network, connection type
- ISP, language, time zone, IP address
- Device identifiers unique to Meta Company Products associated with the same device or account

**6. 2025 Policy Updates**
- Meta AI: New optional messaging feature with Meta AI, users can choose whether to use it
- Calling features: New calling and media effects with updated privacy information
- Contact storage: Updated information on how contacts are securely saved for device restoration
- Customer support: Additional details on information used when contacting customer support

**7. Data Retention**
- Messages stored on your device, not WhatsApp servers
- Undelivered messages: Retained for max 30 days in encrypted form, then deleted
- Media: Temporarily stored (up to 30 days) for efficient delivery
- Account deletion: Information deleted within 90 days, some log records remain but are disassociated from personal identifiers

**8. User Rights**
- Access and portability: "Request Account Info" feature
- Deletion: "Delete My Account" feature in app
- Update: Can change profile information in settings
- Opt-out of marketing: Can unsubscribe from marketing emails

**9. No Third-Party Banner Ads**
WhatsApp explicitly states: "We still do not allow third-party banner ads on our Services. We have no intention to introduce them."

**10. Age Requirements**
Policy does not clearly state age requirements in the main privacy policy text.

### Policy Change Notification
- Policy may be amended with notice "as appropriate"
- No clear mechanism for rejecting changes
- No explanation of consequences of continuing to use after changes

### Legal Basis for Processing
1. Contractual Necessity: Operating services, account management, message delivery
2. Consent: Camera/photo gallery access, precise location sharing
3. Legal Obligation: Responding to valid legal requests
4. Legitimate Interests: Business intelligence, service improvement, safety and security

## Verbatim Policy Excerpts

**On End-to-End Encryption:**
> "Your personal messages and calls are end-to-end encrypted. No one else, not even WhatsApp, can read or listen to them."

**On Meta Sharing:**
> "As part of the Meta Companies, WhatsApp receives information from, and shares information (see here) with, the other Meta Companies. We may use the information we receive from them, and they may use the information we share with them, to help operate, provide, improve, understand, customize, support, and market our Services and their offerings, including the Meta Company Products."

**On Contact Upload:**
> "You can use the contact upload feature and provide us, if permitted by applicable laws, with the phone numbers in your address book on a regular basis, including those of users of our Services and your other contacts."

**On Passive Location:**
> "Even if you do not use our location-related features, we use IP addresses and other information like phone number area codes to estimate your general location (e.g., city and country)."

**On Device Information:**
> "We collect device and connection-specific information when you install, access, or use our Services. This includes information such as hardware model, operating system information, battery level, signal strength, app version, browser information, mobile network, connection information (including phone number, mobile operator or ISP), language and time zone, IP address, device operations information, and identifiers (including identifiers unique to Meta Company Products associated with the same device or account)."

**On No Third-Party Ads:**
> "We still do not allow third-party banner ads on our Services. We have no intention to introduce them."

**On Account Deletion:**
> "When you delete your WhatsApp account, your undelivered messages are deleted from our servers as well as any of your other information we no longer need to operate and provide our Services."
