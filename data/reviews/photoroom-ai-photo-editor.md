## Summary Card

**App:** PhotoRoom: AI Photo Editor
**Overall Risk:** Caution (Grade B)
**Score:** 77/100

### Red Flags
- **Your photos may train AI models by default** - By using PhotoRoom, you acknowledge that uploaded images are processed and used to improve, train, and develop their products, services, and models. You can opt out but it doesn't undo prior processing.
- **Cookie data kept for 25 months** - Social network and audience measurement cookies preserve your IP address and pages viewed for up to twenty-five months with consent.
- **Data transferred outside Europe** - Personal data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area due to service providers located outside the EEA.
- **Long retention periods** - Purchase records retained for 10 years, archived account data for 5 years after 2 years of inactivity.

### Green Flags
- **AI training opt-out available** - You can opt out of having your uploaded photos used to train AI models at any time through account settings.
- **Extensive GDPR rights** - Comprehensive rights including access, rectification, erasure, data portability, restriction, objection, and post-mortem data choices.
- **Does not sell data under CCPA** - Explicitly states it does not "sell" or "share" personal data as defined by CCPA.
- **API uploads exempt from AI training** - Images processed through the Application Programming Interface (API) are exempt from model improvement.

### Verdict
PhotoRoom offers powerful AI photo editing but uses your uploaded photos for AI model training by default. While opt-out is available and the app provides strong privacy rights under GDPR, be aware that your images may contribute to AI training unless you change settings. Avoid uploading sensitive personal photos.

## Analysis Notes

### Key Findings

**1. AI Model Training with User Photos**
PhotoRoom's most significant privacy consideration is that uploaded photos are used for AI model training by default. The policy states users can opt out at any time, but this opt-out does not have retroactive effect on processing already carried out. API uploads are exempt from this training.

**2. Data Sharing with Service Providers**
PhotoRoom shares data with multiple service providers:
- Apple and RevenueCat receive browsing/interaction data for refund validation (6 months retention)
- Dub receives email addresses for referral programs (with consent)
- Technical providers for hosting, notifications, user tracking, and security

**3. Long Cookie Retention**
Social network and audience measurement cookies collect IP addresses and page views, retained for up to 25 months with user consent.

**4. International Data Transfers**
Personal data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area, particularly due to service providers located outside the EEA. GDPR safeguards are applied.

**5. Strong GDPR Compliance**
PhotoRoom provides comprehensive user rights:
- Right of access
- Right to rectification
- Right to erasure/right to be forgotten
- Right to restriction of processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object
- Right to determine post-mortem data handling
- Right to withdraw consent

Responses within one calendar month (up to three for complex requests).

**6. Data Retention Periods**
- Account data: Duration of use or 2 years inactivity, then archived 5 years
- Purchase records: 10 years (accounting requirements)
- Cookie data: Up to 25 months with consent
- Navigation data: Duration of session
- Device/activity data: Duration of use or 30 days after deletion request

### User Rights
- **Access/Know** - Request information about data collected
- **Delete** - Request deletion with responses within one month
- **Correct** - Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Portability** - Receive copy of data in portable format
- **Opt-out of AI training** - Available in account settings
- **GDPR rights** - Full suite of European privacy rights available

### Privacy Controls
- Account settings opt-out for AI model training
- Cookie consent management
- Data portability requests
- Account deletion available
