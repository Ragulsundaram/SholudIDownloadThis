## Summary Card

**App:** Photoroom: AI Photo Editor
**Overall Risk:** Caution (Grade C)
**Score:** 69/100

### Red Flags
- **Your photos are used to train AI models** - By default, Photoroom uses the images you upload to improve and train its AI products and models. You can opt out, but this only stops future use and does not undo any training that already happened.
- **Browsing data shared for refund checks** - Photoroom sends your browsing and interaction data to Apple and RevenueCat to check if you qualify for refunds.
- **Data kept for years after you stop using the app** - Even if you delete your account, Photoroom may keep some data for a long time. Your account info can be kept up to 2 years after your last login and then archived for 5 more years. Purchase records are kept for 10 years.
- **Personal data may leave the European Union** - Photoroom is based in France, but your data may be sent to countries outside Europe because some of their service providers are located there.

### Green Flags
- **You can opt out of AI training** - Photoroom lets you opt out of having your uploaded images used for AI model training by changing a setting in your account.
- **Photoroom never sees your credit card** - When you make a purchase, your credit card details are handled only by the payment service provider. Photoroom does not store or process your card information.
- **Explicitly says it does not sell your data** - Photoroom clearly states in its privacy policy that it does not sell or share personal data for advertising purposes as defined by California privacy law.
- **Strong European privacy rights available** - Because Photoroom is based in France, you have rights under European privacy law including the right to access, correct, delete, and move your data. They must respond within one month.

### Verdict
Photoroom is a capable AI photo editor, but be aware that your uploaded photos are used to train its AI models by default. While you can opt out and the app offers strong privacy protections, your images may still contribute to commercial AI training unless you change settings. Avoid uploading sensitive personal photos.

## Analysis Notes

### Key Findings

**1. AI Model Training with User Photos**
Photoroom's most significant privacy consideration is that uploaded photos are used for AI model training by default. The policy states users can opt out at any time, but this opt-out does not have retroactive effect on processing already carried out. API uploads are exempt from this training. Photoroom claims training images are selected and annotated without any additional identity information.

**2. Data Sharing with Service Providers**
Photoroom shares data with multiple service providers:
- Apple and RevenueCat receive browsing/interaction data for refund validation (6 months retention)
- Dub receives email addresses for referral programs (with consent)
- Technical providers for hosting, notifications, user tracking, and security

**3. Long Cookie Retention**
Social network and audience measurement cookies collect IP addresses and page views, retained for up to 25 months with user consent.

**4. International Data Transfers**
Personal data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area, particularly due to service providers located outside the EEA. GDPR safeguards are applied.

**5. Strong GDPR Compliance**
Photoroom provides comprehensive user rights:
- Right of access
- Right to rectification
- Right to erasure/right to be forgotten
- Right to restriction of processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object
- Right to determine post-mortem data handling
- Right to withdraw consent

Responses within one calendar month (up to three for complex requests).

**6. Data Retention Periods**
- Account data: Duration of use or 2 years inactivity, then archived 5 years
- Purchase records: 10 years (accounting requirements)
- Cookie data: Up to 25 months with consent
- Navigation data: Duration of session
- Device/activity data: Duration of use or 30 days after deletion request

### User Rights
- **Access/Know** - Request information about data collected
- **Delete** - Request deletion with responses within one month
- **Correct** - Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Portability** - Receive copy of data in portable format
- **Opt-out of AI training** - Available in account settings
- **GDPR rights** - Full suite of European privacy rights available

### Privacy Controls
- Account settings opt-out for AI model training
- Cookie consent management
- Data portability requests
- Account deletion available
