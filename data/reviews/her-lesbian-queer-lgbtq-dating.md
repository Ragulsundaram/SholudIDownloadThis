### Red Flags
- **Collects and shares your sexual orientation with advertisers:** The app asks for your sexual orientation, gender identity, and dating preferences as part of your profile. This sensitive LGBTQ+ data is then used to show you targeted ads and may be shared with advertising partners.
- **Precise location is collected automatically and shared with other users:** The app automatically collects your exact GPS location every time you open it and shows other users roughly how far away you are. You cannot use the Meet or Nearby features without turning this on.
- **Tracks your activity across the app for targeted ads:** The app uses third-party services like AppLovin, Braze, and Branch to track how you use the app, what ads you click, and where you came from. This is used to show you targeted ads and plan marketing campaigns.
- **Your profile is visible to all users by default:** The photos, bio, age, and personal details you add to your profile are visible to every other user of the app. To hide your profile, you need to pay for premium incognito mode or block individual users one by one.
- **Your messages may be analyzed by third parties:** The policy says third-party analytics services may analyze your messaging behavior, including who you message and how often. This data may be linked to your personal information.

### Green Flags
- **In-app controls for data export, deletion, and ad opt-out:** You can request a copy of your data, stop personalized ads, or delete your account entirely directly from the app settings without needing to email support.
- **Explicitly states it does not sell your personal data:** The policy says HER does not sell your personal data, though it does use it for targeted advertising through third-party partners.
- **Block and report features to protect your privacy:** You can block specific users from seeing or interacting with you, and report users who violate community guidelines or make you uncomfortable.
- **Users under 16 are removed if discovered:** If the app learns that someone under 16 has created an account, it will promptly delete their data and prevent them from using the service.

### Verdict
HER is a popular LGBTQ+ dating app that collects highly sensitive identity data including sexual orientation, gender identity, and precise geolocation, then shares this information with advertising networks and marketing platforms. While the app provides in-app controls for opting out of personalized ads and deleting your account, the default data practices are extensive. Users should be aware that their profile data is publicly visible within the app and that third parties analyze their messaging behavior for marketing purposes.

## Detailed Analysis

### Data Collection Overview

HER collects extensive personal data across multiple sensitive categories:

**Identifiers and Profile Data:** Name (can be fake), email, phone number, AppleID, Facebook/Instagram login, birth date, photos, bio, gender, sexual orientation, dating preferences, physical characteristics, education, and employment history.

**Sensitive Information:** The policy explicitly lists "information collected and analyzed concerning a consumer's sex life or sexual orientation" as a category of sensitive personal information. This is collected during profile creation and used to tailor the service and ads.

**Location Data:** Precise geolocation is collected automatically every time the app opens. It is shared as rough distance estimates with other users. Location is required for the Meet and Nearby features.

**Activity Data:** Browsing history, search history, clicks, session length, messaging patterns, interaction with advertisements, and other technical usage data.

**Device Data:** IP address, device type, operating system, browser type. Branch places a persistent identifier for marketing attribution.

### Data Sharing

HER shares data with multiple third-party services:
- **AppLovin:** For targeted advertising display
- **Braze:** For marketing campaign management and messaging behavior analysis
- **Branch:** For app attribution, placing persistent identifiers to track marketing leads, account creation, and purchases
- **Google Analytics:** For website analytics
- **Stripe & RevenueCat:** For payment processing and subscription management
- **Cloud Providers:** For data storage and hosting (TLS 1.2 in transit, AES at rest)
- **Facebook/Instagram:** Optional login integration
- **Spotify:** Optional profile music display

The policy states they do not "sell" personal data but acknowledges using it for personalized/targeted advertising, which may constitute "sharing" under privacy laws.

### User Rights and Controls

Users have several in-app privacy controls accessible via Settings > Privacy Options:
- **Send me my data:** Exports a copy of held data to verified email
- **Stop using my data:** Stops using data for personalized ads
- **Delete my account and data:** Deletes account and associated data

Additional rights include opt-out of interest-based advertising by emailing support@weareher.com, and using general opt-out tools at aboutads.info and networkadvertising.org.

### Profile Visibility

As a core feature of the dating app, most profile information is visible to other users by default. Users can make their profile "Incognito" through premium subscription settings. Blocking and reporting features are available for individual users.

### Children's Data

The app is strictly 18+ with birth date verification during signup. Users under 16 discovered on the platform have their data promptly removed.

### Data Security

The policy mentions industry-standard encryption (TLSv1.2 in transit, AES at rest) and cloud server security. However, it notes that "no website, application or transmission can guarantee security."

### Policy Changes

Changes are posted on the website with an updated date. Users are expected to check the policy periodically. No advance notice or active re-agreement is required.
