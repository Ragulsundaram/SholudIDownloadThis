## Summary Card

- **Overall risk:** Caution
- **Score:** 66 / 100

### Red flags
- **Data shared with over 20 advertising and analytics companies** — Headway shares your data with Facebook, Google, TikTok, Snapchat, Pinterest, Appsflyer, Amplitude, Firebase, and many other companies. Some of your data is stored and processed on their servers. (High)
- **Your reading habits are tracked and used to target ads** — Headway analyzes what you read, how long you read, and your reading goals. This information is used to show you personalized ads through partners like Facebook and Google. (High)
- **App builds a detailed fingerprint of your device** — Headway collects your device model, operating system, language, IP address, hardware ID, mobile carrier, and advertising identifiers (IDFA/AAID) to identify and track your device. (Medium)
- **Your IP address and activity logs are shared with ad partners** — Your IP address and detailed activity logs are collected automatically and shared with advertising partners and analytics providers to measure and target ads. (Medium)
- **App profiles you for marketing under 'legitimate interest'** — Headway claims it doesn't need your consent to analyze your behavior and send you marketing messages. It says this is in its 'legitimate interest' to promote the app. (Medium)
- **Terms and Conditions URL not clearly provided** — The privacy policy references Terms and Conditions of Use but does not provide a direct link to them. (Low)

### Green flags
- **Headway explicitly says it does not sell your personal information** — Under California privacy law, Headway states it does not sell your personal information and has no knowledge of selling data from minors under 16.
- **Payment card details are never stored by the app** — All payments are processed by third parties like Apple, Stripe, and PayPal. Headway says it will not store or collect your payment card details itself.
- **You can delete your account directly in the app** — You can delete your account using the Settings menu in the app or by contacting support. The process takes up to 30 days.
- **Detailed opt-out instructions provided for personalized ads** — Headway provides step-by-step instructions for iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, and web browsers on how to opt out of personalized advertising.
- **Specific privacy rights available for EEA, CA, VA, and NV residents** — Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights including data portability, the right to complain to a regulator, or the right to opt out of targeted advertising.

### Verdict
Headway offers useful book summaries and micro-learning content, but the privacy trade-offs are notable. The app collects extensive device and behavioral data and shares it with an unusually large number of third-party advertising and analytics companies. Your reading preferences, habits, and goals are analyzed and used to target ads. On the positive side, the app does not sell your data, does not store payment details, provides clear opt-out instructions, and offers regional privacy rights. Users who value privacy should consider the extent of tracking before signing up.
