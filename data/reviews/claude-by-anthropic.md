## Summary

Claude by Anthropic is a more privacy-conscious AI assistant compared to many alternatives. It does not sell personal data, provides meaningful deletion and export rights, and allows users to opt out of model training. The main privacy concern is that inputs and outputs may be used to train Anthropic's models by default unless the user disables this in account settings.

Key concerns: conversations may train AI models by default; IP-based location inference; third-party integrations collect data independently; conversations flagged for safety may be reviewed.

Key positives: does not sell personal data; opt-out available for model training; strong deletion and export rights; opt-out available for targeted advertising of Anthropic's own products.

---

## Methodology

This review was conducted by analyzing the Anthropic Privacy Policy text extracted on 2026-05-16. Strict legal-safe rules were applied: every claim is backed by a direct verbatim quote; silent topics are marked unknown.

### Data Collection Observed

- **Identity/Contact:** Name, email, phone number, indirect identifiers
- **Payment:** Payment info for optional paid products
- **Inputs/Outputs:** All prompts, files, images, audio, video uploaded to Claude
- **Feedback:** Ratings and related conversations stored as feedback
- **Communication:** Name, contact info, message contents when contacting support
- **Device:** Device type, OS, browser, network, ISP, time zone, IP address, device/advertising identifiers
- **Usage:** Dates/times of access, browsing history, search, links clicked, pages viewed
- **Cookies:** Used to manage services, personalize experience, and market Anthropic products

### Notable Excerpts

> "We may use your Inputs and Outputs to train our models and improve our Services, unless you opt out through your account settings."

> "Anthropic does not “sell” your personal data as that term is defined by applicable laws and regulations. You can opt-out of sharing your personal data for targeted advertising to promote our products and services."

> "You also are able to delete individual conversations, which will be removed immediately from your conversation history and automatically deleted from our back-end within 30 days."

> "Our Services may involve integrations with, or may direct you to, websites, apps, and services managed by third parties. By interacting with these third parties, you are providing information directly to the third party and not Anthropic."
