### Red Flags

- **Facebook ID collected automatically** — The app collects your Facebook ID as part of device data. This could be used to link your fitness activity to your social media profile, even if the app claims they don't sell data.

- **Data deletion requires email contact** — To fully delete your account data, you must email support@musclemonster.fit. Simply uninstalling the app only removes local data from your device, not what's stored on their servers.

- **Detailed interaction tracking** — The app logs every tap, feature you use, and how long you spend in each section. This creates a detailed profile of your fitness habits and app behavior.

### Green Flags

- **Explicit 'no sale' guarantee** — The privacy policy clearly states "We guarantee that the information we collect will never be sold" and repeats this for CCPA compliance. This is a strong positive commitment.

- **Limited third-party sharing** — The app only shares aggregated, de-identified data that cannot reasonably identify you. No personal data is shared with advertisers or data brokers.

- **No data collection from children under 13** — The app explicitly states they do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, which is a responsible practice for COPPA compliance.

- **Policy change notifications** — You'll be notified of material privacy policy changes through the app and asked to review them, rather than changes happening silently.

### Verdict

Muscle Monster Workout Planner has relatively transparent privacy practices with an explicit guarantee that your data will never be sold. While the app collects detailed usage analytics and your Facebook ID, it limits sharing to aggregated, non-identifiable data only. The main drawback is that fully deleting your data requires emailing support rather than being a self-serve in-app option. Overall, this is a reasonably privacy-conscious fitness app, though users should be aware of the behavioral tracking and Facebook integration.
