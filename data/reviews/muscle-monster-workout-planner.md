## Summary Card

**Muscle Monster Workout Planner**  
**Overall Risk:** Caution (C / 69)

### Red Flags
- **Facebook ID collected automatically.** The app grabs your Facebook ID without you necessarily signing in through Facebook. This is a unique identifier that can be used to track you across apps and websites.
- **Workout and body data stored on servers.** Your practice schedule, body improvement goals, and how you feel after workouts are saved on company servers. This is personal health information that could reveal sensitive details about your body and habits.
- **Uninstalling does not delete server data.** The policy says you can remove stored data by uninstalling the app, but that only deletes data on your phone. Your information likely stays on their servers unless you manually email them to request deletion.
- **Several privacy areas not mentioned.** The policy does not say anything about location tracking, camera access, contacts, or file storage. This means you cannot know whether the app uses these permissions or not.

### Green Flags
- **Explicit no-sell promise.** The developer guarantees twice in the policy that the information they collect will never be sold. This is a strong commitment that many apps do not make.
- **Only shares anonymized data.** When they do share information with others, they only share data that has been grouped together and stripped of details that could identify you personally.
- **California privacy rights honored.** Users in California can ask to see what data the company has collected about them and request deletion of that data. You will not be treated worse for exercising these rights.
- **No intentional data collection from children.** The app says it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 and requires users to be at least 16 years old.

### Verdict
Muscle Monster is a workout app that collects meaningful health data and your Facebook ID, but makes an explicit promise never to sell your information. While it only shares anonymized data and honors deletion requests, the policy leaves several key privacy categories completely unaddressed and misleadingly suggests that uninstalling removes all your data. Consider whether you are comfortable with the gaps in transparency before downloading.

## Category Breakdown

### Camera & Microphone — Unknown
The privacy policy does not mention camera or microphone access at all, so it is unclear whether the app uses them.

### Location & GPS — Unknown
The policy does not say whether the app tracks your location or GPS data.

### Contacts & Phonebook — Unknown
The policy does not mention whether the app can access your phone contacts or address book.

### Storage & File Access — Unknown
The policy does not explain what files or storage areas on your phone the app can access.

### Data Sharing with 3rd Parties — Caution
The developer promises never to sell your data and only shares information that has been stripped of personal details or is required by law.

### Account & Identity Data — Caution
The app collects your practice details and preferences tied to your account, plus your Facebook ID automatically.

### Behavioural & Ad Tracking — Caution
The app records how you tap around the interface, which features you use, and how long you stay in the app. Your Facebook ID is also collected, which can be used for tracking across apps.

### Children's Data Handling — Safe
The app says it does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 and requires users to be over 16.

### Network & Internet Activity — Safe
The policy does not suggest unusual tracking of your internet activity or network traffic.

### Device Info & Fingerprinting — Caution
The app collects your device language, model, settings, operating system, and Facebook ID automatically.

### Health & Biometric Data — Caution
The app collects your workout schedule, body improvement goals, and how you feel after practice, which is personal health information.

### Financial & Payment Data — Safe
The app tracks subscription orders but does not claim to store your payment card details directly.

### Data Deletion & User Rights — Caution
You can email the developer to request deletion, but uninstalling the app alone will not remove your data from their servers.

### Policy Change Notification — Caution
If the privacy policy changes, they will post a notice in the app. If you keep using the app, that counts as agreeing to the new terms.
