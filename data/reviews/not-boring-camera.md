## Summary Card

- **App:** (Not Boring) Camera
- **Overall Risk:** Safe
- **Score:** 87 / 100
- **Grade:** A

### Red Flags
- **Policy changes posted without advance warning.** The developer can update the privacy policy anytime and only promises to post the new version on the page. They don't promise to email you or show a popup when rules change.
- **Your IP address is stored in server logs.** When you use the app, your IP address is saved in the server logs for a short time. This is common practice, but it means your approximate location can be guessed.
- **Legal terms contradict the privacy policy.** The license agreement says the developer can access your personal information and photos, while the privacy policy says they don't collect any personal data. These two documents say different things.
- **No formal data deletion process.** Since the developer claims they don't store personal data, there's no form or email to request deletion. But there's also no clear confirmation of what happens to server logs.

### Green Flags
- **Developer explicitly collects no personal data.** The privacy policy clearly states: "We neither store nor collect any personal data." This is one of the strongest privacy promises you'll find in a camera app.
- **No sharing with outside parties.** The developer says they don't share your data with anyone outside the company.
- **No ads and no behavioural tracking.** The developer says they don't want to know how you use the app, and there's no mention of ads or tracking companies.
- **All connections use HTTPS encryption.** Every bit of data sent between your phone and the developer's servers is encrypted.
- **App not aimed at children under 13.** The developer clearly says their apps are not meant for kids under 13.

### Verdict
(Not Boring) Camera is a refreshing outlier in the camera app category. The developer makes an explicit, unambiguous promise not to collect or store any personal data — something almost unheard of for a photo app. The main concerns are technical: IP logging, policy changes without advance notice, and some contradictory boilerplate language in the legal terms. For privacy-conscious users, this is one of the safer camera apps available.
