## Summary Card

**App:** Tata CLiQ Luxury Shopping App  
**Overall Risk:** Dangerous  
**Score:** 39 / 100 (Grade F)

### Red Flags
- **Government ID and thumb impression collected.** The app asks for your government ID documents and thumb impression. This sensitive identity and biometric data is stored on their servers and shared with Tata Group companies and business partners.
- **Data shared across Tata Group and partners.** Your personal information is shared with Tata Group Entities, Partners, and Service Providers for marketing, profiling, loyalty programs, and business operations. This includes your identity, financial, and biometric data.
- **Financial data used to build your profile.** The app stores your payment details, transaction history, and spending patterns. It uses this financial data to generate insights about your behavior and send you targeted offers, including financial products.
- **Device fingerprinting for tracking.** The app uses cookies as unique identifiers to recognize your device across sessions. It collects your IP address, operating system, browser type, and device details to track and profile you.
- **Extensive behavioral tracking for ads.** Tata CLiQ uses cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to track your browsing and app usage. It analyzes this data to understand your preferences and deliver personalized marketing and recommendations.

### Green Flags
- **You can request data deletion.** You have the right to request deletion or removal of your data from their systems. You can also request correction of inaccurate information.
- **Opt-out available for marketing.** You can choose not to receive marketing emails by clicking the unsubscribe link, and you can opt out of some uses of your data for marketing purposes.
- **Service providers bound by security contracts.** Third parties that process your data must follow express instructions, implement technical and organizational security measures, and maintain confidentiality obligations.
- **Data stored on servers in India.** The company states that data collected under this privacy policy is hosted on servers located in India.

### Verdict
Tata CLiQ Luxury Shopping App collects and stores highly sensitive personal information including government ID documents and thumb impressions. This data is shared across the Tata Group ecosystem and with partners for marketing, profiling, and loyalty programs. The app also builds detailed profiles of your financial behavior and tracks you using device fingerprinting. While you can request deletion, data may persist on backups indefinitely. Given the collection of biometric and government identity data on external servers, this app represents a serious privacy risk.
