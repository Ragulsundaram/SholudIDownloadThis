## Summary Card

**App:** Speechify – Text to Speech PDF  
**Developer:** Speechify Inc.  
**Overall Risk:** Caution  
**Score:** 74 / 100 (Grade B)

### Red Flags

- **Loads many ad and analytics trackers.** The app and website use Meta Pixel, Google Global Site Tag, Google Conversion Tracking, Bing Event Tracking, TikTok Pixel, and several analytics tools to monitor your activity and measure ads.
- **Your documents live on their servers.** Any text, PDFs, or documents you upload are synced to Speechify's servers in the United States so you can access them across devices.
- **Policy changes auto-apply if you keep using.** If Speechify updates its privacy policy, your continued use of the app counts as agreeing to the new terms. You must delete your account if you disagree.
- **Data stored in the United States.** All your information is transferred to and stored on servers in the US, which has weaker privacy protections than many other countries.

### Green Flags

- **Does not sell or rent your data.** Speechify explicitly promises it does not sell or rent your personal data to anyone.
- **No sharing for third-party ads.** The company says it does not share your personal data with third parties so they can show you advertisements.
- **Keyboard contacts stay on your device.** If you give the Speechify Keyboard access to your contacts, those names are stored only on your phone and are never sent to Speechify's servers.
- **You can delete your account.** You can delete your account and personal data through the app's Settings page at any time.

### Verdict

Speechify takes a stronger-than-average stance on data sales and third-party advertising, explicitly promising not to sell or rent your personal information. However, the app deploys a wide array of marketing and analytics trackers, stores your uploaded documents on US servers, and binds you to policy changes through continued use. For a text-to-speech utility, the privacy posture is reasonable but not exceptional. Users handling sensitive documents should be aware that content is synced to the cloud.

## Category Breakdown

### Camera & Microphone — Unknown
The privacy policy does not mention camera or microphone access. The app likely uses the microphone for voice typing and the camera for document scanning, but this is not addressed in the policy.

> "No mention of camera or microphone access in the privacy policy."

### Location & GPS — Caution
Speechify infers your general location from your IP address when you use the app or website.

> "Location information. This is the geographic area where you use your computer and mobile devices (as indicated by an Internet Protocol (IP) address or similar identifier)."

### Contacts & Phonebook — Safe
The Speechify Keyboard can access your contacts' names, but they are stored only on your device and never sent to Speechify's servers.

> "Names of user contacts in Speechify Keyboard (on-device storage only)... These names will be stored solely on your device within the Speechify Keyboard app and are never sent to Speechify servers."

### Storage & File Access — Caution
You upload documents, PDFs, and text so the app can read them aloud. This content is synced to Speechify's servers.

> "User Content. This consists of all text, documents, or other content or information uploaded, entered, or otherwise transmitted by you in connection with your use of the Services and/or Software."

### Data Sharing with 3rd Parties — Caution
Speechify shares data with service providers for hosting, analytics, and customer support, but promises not to sell it or share it for third-party advertising.

> "Speechify does not share your Personal Data with third parties for the purpose of enabling them to deliver their advertisements to you. No, Speechify does not sell or rent your Personal Data."

### Account & Identity Data — Caution
You must provide an email and username to create an account. Social login data is collected only with your permission.

> "Your username, email address, and contact and language preferences. If you log into Speechify with a social networking credential... we will ask permission to access basic information from that account."

### Behavioural & Ad Tracking — Caution
Speechify uses cookies, Meta Pixel, Google tags, Bing, and TikTok trackers to measure the effectiveness of its own advertising and marketing campaigns.

> "Speechify uses cookies and similar technologies... to help deliver ads for relevant Speechify products and services to you when you visit certain pages on the Site and then visit certain third-party sites."

### Children's Data Handling — Caution
Speechify says it does not knowingly collect data from children under 13, except through school licenses.

> "Unless your Speechify account is part of a Speechify for Education license, Speechify does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13."

### Network & Internet Activity — Caution
Speechify logs your IP address, browser type, and how you use the app to analyze traffic and improve features.

> "This log data may include IP address, browser type and settings, date and time of use, information about browser configuration, language preferences, and cookie data."

### Device Info & Fingerprinting — Caution
Speechify collects information about your device type, operating system, and unique device identifiers.

> "Device information. This is data from your computer or mobile device, such as the type of hardware and software you are using (for example, your operating system and browser type), as well as unique device identifiers for devices that are using Speechify Software."

### Health & Biometric Data — Safe
Speechify does not collect health or biometric data.

### Financial & Payment Data — Caution
If you pay for a subscription, Speechify collects payment information. You can also pay through PayPal, which handles your card details directly.

> "Your payment information, if you are a paying customer. This is required to complete a commercial transaction on the Site. If you choose to use PayPal... you will provide your credit card number directly to PayPal."

### Data Deletion & User Rights — Caution
You can delete your account through Settings, but some data may be kept for fraud prevention and legal reasons.

> "You can remove your Personal Data from Speechify at any time by logging into your account, accessing the Settings page, and then deleting your account. However, we may keep some of your Personal Data for as long as reasonably necessary for our legitimate business interests."

### Policy Change Notification — Caution
Speechify will email you or post updates on its website if the privacy policy changes, but continuing to use the app means you accept the new terms.

> "We will notify you of any material changes that impact your rights under this Policy by email... Please note that your continued use of Speechify after any change means that you agree with and consent to be bound by the new Policy."
