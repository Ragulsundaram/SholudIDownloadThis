## Summary Card

**App:** Twitch: Live Streaming  
**Developer:** Twitch Interactive, Inc. (Amazon subsidiary)  
**Overall Risk:** Caution (Score: 62/100, Grade: C)

### Red Flags
- **Data combined with Amazon customer profiles:** Twitch shares everything it knows about you with Amazon — its parent company. Amazon then combines your Twitch data with your Amazon shopping profile and uses it to improve both companies' products, services, and advertising.
- **Tracked across the web for targeted ads:** Twitch works with outside ad companies to show you ads both on Twitch and on other websites and apps you visit. They share your mobile ad ID and sometimes your email address with these companies to match you across services.
- **Advertisers collect data directly from your device:** When advertisers show ads inside Twitch, they automatically receive your IP address and use cookies to measure how well their ads work. Twitch says it cannot control what these advertisers do with that data.
- **Do-Not-Track signals are ignored:** If you turn on the Do-Not-Track setting in your browser, Twitch explicitly says it does not recognize or respond to it. They will keep tracking you anyway.
- **Pulls data from your social media accounts:** If you link your Twitch account to Discord, Steam, YouTube, or other services, Twitch collects extra information about you from those platforms — including what you watch and how you engage with content — to build a more detailed profile.

### Green Flags
- **Dedicated Privacy Choices page with opt-outs:** Twitch provides a specific page where you can manage your privacy settings, including opting out of certain types of advertising and data sharing.
- **Clear account deletion process:** You can close your account and delete your profile information through a dedicated page. Twitch promises to delete data it is not legally required to keep.
- **Has a Data Protection Officer:** Twitch has a dedicated Data Protection Officer who handles privacy questions and data rights requests. You can contact them directly at privacy@twitch.tv.
- **Honors GDPR and CCPA rights:** You can ask Twitch to show you your data, correct it, delete it, restrict how it's used, or move it to another service. They promise to respond within 30 days.
- **Explicitly prohibits users under 13:** Twitch clearly states that children under 13 are not allowed to use the service at all, and will delete accounts if they discover underage users. This is stronger protection than many platforms.

### Verdict
Twitch is a massively popular live-streaming and social platform owned by Amazon. It collects a wide range of personal information including your name, email, phone number, address, date of birth, payment details, voice, image, chat messages, and everything you broadcast or upload. It also automatically logs your IP address, device identifiers, browsing behaviour, and mobile ad identifiers. This data is shared with Amazon and its subsidiaries (where it may be combined with your Amazon shopping profile), third-party ad networks, analytics providers, and Extension developers. Twitch tracks you across the web to serve targeted ads both on and off the platform, does not honor Do-Not-Track signals, and shares your email address or device ID with advertising partners for cross-service matching. On the positive side, Twitch provides a Privacy Choices page with opt-outs, offers account deletion, has a Data Protection Officer, honors GDPR/CCPA rights, and explicitly prohibits users under 13. However, the sheer scale of data collection, sharing, and tracking makes this a platform to use with eyes wide open.
