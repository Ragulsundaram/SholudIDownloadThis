## Summary Card

**App:** Microsoft Word
**Overall Risk:** Caution (Grade C)
**Score:** 59/100

### Red Flags
- **AI model training** - Your Copilot conversations may be used to train AI models unless you opt out
- **Cross-device tracking** - Microsoft uses cookies, MUID cookies, and advertising IDs to track you across devices and services
- **Organization access** - If using a work or school account, your organization can access your data and documents
- **Cloud document processing** - Documents stored in OneDrive or processed by connected experiences are sent to Microsoft cloud servers
- **Data sharing with advertisers** - Your data is shared with third-party advertising platforms for personalized ads

### Green Flags
- **No content-based ads** - Microsoft states it does not use your documents, emails, or photos to target ads
- **AI training opt-out** - You can opt out of having your Copilot conversations used to train AI models
- **Data deletion controls** - Microsoft provides a privacy dashboard to access and clear some of your data

### Verdict
Microsoft Word is a powerful word processing app with reasonable privacy protections for your document content, but significant concerns around AI training, cross-device tracking, and organization access for work accounts. While Microsoft states it doesn't use your documents to target ads, the extensive data collection, AI model training, and tracking across services warrant caution.

## Analysis Notes

### Key Findings

**1. AI Model Training (Major Concern)**
Microsoft Copilot uses your prompts and related information. In certain markets, your conversation data is used to train AI models unless you choose to opt out.

**2. Extensive Cross-Device Tracking**
Microsoft uses:
- Cookies and similar technologies for tracking
- MUID cookies for cross-device recognition
- Advertising IDs for personalized ads
- Device identifiers like IMEI

**3. Organization Access for Work/School Accounts**
If you use Word with a work or school account:
- Your organization can control privacy-related settings
- Your organization can access and process your data
- Your organization can access the contents of your communications and files
- If you lose access to your work/school account, you may lose access to products and content

**4. Cloud Document Processing**
Documents stored in OneDrive or processed by connected experiences are sent to and stored on Microsoft cloud servers.

**5. No Content-Based Ad Targeting (Positive)**
Microsoft explicitly states: "Microsoft does not use what you say in email, human-to-human chat, video calls, or voice mail, or your documents, photos, or other personal files to target ads."

**6. Data Sharing**
Microsoft shares data with:
- Microsoft-controlled affiliates and subsidiaries
- Vendors working on Microsoft's behalf
- Third-party advertising platforms
- When required by law

### User Rights
- **Access and control** - Microsoft privacy dashboard available
- **Opt-out of personalized ads** - Available through Microsoft's opt-out page
- **AI training opt-out** - Can opt out of conversation data being used for AI training
- **Data deletion** - Some data types can be deleted through the dashboard
- **Communication preferences** - Can choose whether to receive promotional emails

### Privacy Controls
- Microsoft privacy dashboard for data access and deletion
- Opt-out page for personalized advertising
- Settings to control personalized ads within products
- Family Safety features for child accounts

## Verbatim Policy Excerpts

**On AI Training:**
> "In certain markets, we use conversation data to train the generative AI models in Copilot, unless you choose to opt-out of such training."

**On Document Processing:**
> "Content — Files, communications, emails, photos, songs, documents, and other media stored or processed via Microsoft cloud services."

**On No Content-Based Ads:**
> "Microsoft does not use what you say in email, human-to-human chat, video calls, or voice mail, or your documents, photos, or other personal files to target ads."

**On Organization Access:**
> "If you use a Microsoft product with an account provided by an organization you are affiliated with, such as your work or school account, that organization can: Access and process your data, including the interaction data, diagnostic data, and the contents of your communications and files associated with your Microsoft product and product accounts."

**On Cross-Device Tracking:**
> "We use cookies and similar technologies for storing and honoring your preferences and settings, enabling you to sign in, providing interest-based advertising, combating fraud, analyzing how our products perform, and fulfilling other legitimate purposes."

**On Data Sharing:**
> "We share your personal data with your consent or to complete any transaction or provide any product you have requested or authorized. We also share data with Microsoft-controlled affiliates and subsidiaries; with vendors working on our behalf; when required by law or to respond to legal process."

**On Policy Changes:**
> "Microsoft may update this privacy statement. You are encouraged to review this privacy statement periodically. If you disagree, you must stop using the Services, close your Microsoft account."
