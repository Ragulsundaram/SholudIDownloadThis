## Summary Card

### Braille Academy: Play & Learn
- **Overall Risk:** Safe  
- **Score:** 84 / 100 (Grade A)  
- **Recommendation:** Recommended

**Red Flags**
- **Policy claims app is not for children** — The app is rated 4+ and teaches Braille, but its privacy policy says it is not meant for anyone under 18. This is a strange contradiction for an educational app.
- **Firebase analytics tracks usage** — The app uses Firebase, a Google service, to track how you use the app, including your device information.
- **Data transferred to Switzerland** — Your data may be sent to and stored on computers in Switzerland.

**Green Flags**
- **No ads in the app** — The developer explicitly states there are absolutely no disturbing ads.
- **Works completely offline** — The app functions entirely without an internet connection, keeping most activity on your device.
- **You can request data deletion** — You can email the developer to ask for your data to be deleted.
- **No sensitive permissions needed** — The app does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, or other sensitive features.

**Verdict**  
Braille Academy is a clean, well-designed educational app for learning Unified English Braille. It works offline, has no ads, and collects minimal data through Firebase analytics. The main concern is the privacy policy's claim that the app is not for children under 18, which contradicts its 4+ App Store rating and educational purpose. Despite this oddity, the app appears safe to use.

## Full Analysis

### What it does
Braille Academy teaches Unified English Braille through 59 levels and 29 challenges covering letters, numbers, punctuation, contractions, and short-form words. It includes a Contrast theme for visually impaired users and works completely offline.

### Data collected
- **Personal Data:** Cookies and Usage Data
- **Usage Data:** IP address, browser type, browser version, pages visited, time and date, time spent, unique device identifiers, diagnostic data, mobile device type, mobile device unique ID, mobile IP address, mobile OS, mobile browser type
- **Tracking:** Cookies, beacons, tags, and scripts for activity tracking

### Who it is shared with
- **Firebase/Google** — Analytics service for monitoring and analyzing app usage
- **Service Providers** — Third-party companies employed to facilitate the Service, with access limited to performing tasks on the developer's behalf

### Data Transfer
Your information may be transferred to and maintained on computers in Switzerland, where data protection laws may differ from your jurisdiction.

### Children's Data
The app is rated 4+ and is an educational tool for learning Braille. However, the privacy policy states: "Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 18 (Children). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 18." If data from children is discovered, they commit to removing it.

### Retention
Not explicitly addressed in the privacy policy.

### User Rights
- Data deletion available via email: data-deletion@dong.digital (requires mentioning app and platform, may need additional verification data)
- No self-service data deletion or automated export mentioned

### Policy History
- **Last updated:** September 28, 2018
- The policy is over 7 years old, though the app continues to receive updates (latest version 2.4.1 from January 2025)

### Notes for reviewer
- The developer is based in Switzerland (data transfer to Switzerland mentioned explicitly)
- The app description mentions it is "not specially designed for people with severe visual impairment, but with feedback and suggestions from them, we are certainly working in that direction"
- No HealthKit, biometric, location, contacts, camera, or microphone access
- In-app purchases handled via Apple's standard system
- The policy appears to be a template generated through "Free Privacy Policy" service
- Contact email: braille@dong.digital
