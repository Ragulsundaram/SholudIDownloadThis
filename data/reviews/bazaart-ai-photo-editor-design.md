## Summary Card

### Bazaart AI Photo Editor Design
- **Overall Risk:** Caution
- **Score:** 64/100 (Grade C)

**Red Flags:**
- **Data shared with many tracking and analytics companies:** Bazaart shares your information with more than ten third-party services including Facebook, AppsFlyer, Google Analytics, Hotjar, Amplitude, and Firebase for analytics, tracking, and notifications.
- **Photos and videos sent to external AI services:** Your photos and videos are processed on Bazaart's servers and may also be sent to external AI services including OpenAI, Google Gemini, and Replicate for editing and generation features.
- **Personal information used for promotional offers:** Bazaart uses your email address and phone number to send you promotional offers and newsletters, and may contact you about surveys.
- **Broad license to use your likeness for promotion:** By using Bazaart, you give them permission to use your name, image, and likeness to promote content you create on the platform.
- **Multiple tracking and analytics tools installed:** The app includes several analytics and tracking services such as Facebook, AppsFlyer, Google Analytics, Firebase, and Hotjar that monitor how you use the app.

**Green Flags:**
- **No mandatory signup required:** You can use Bazaart without creating an account. Signing up is only required if you want to use the app on multiple devices or subscribe to premium features.
- **Server copies of content deleted after a few days:** Photos and videos processed on Bazaart's servers are deleted periodically after a short period of time, usually a few days.
- **Users can request deletion of their information:** You can review your information in the app and request to delete all relevant information.
- **Face detection data is not saved:** Bazaart uses Apple's built-in Vision Framework on your device to detect faces in photos, but they do not retain any face data from this process.

**Verdict:** Bazaart offers useful creative tools and allows anonymous use, but the extensive data sharing with tracking and AI services, combined with promotional marketing and broad content licenses, creates meaningful privacy risks. Consider whether you are comfortable with your photos being processed by multiple external AI services and your activity being tracked by numerous analytics providers.

## Methodology

This analysis was conducted with strict legal-safe rules: every claim is backed by a direct verbatim quote, cautious language is used, and categories not addressed by the policy are marked as unknown with needs_review where appropriate.

### Data Collection Observed

- **Personal:** Name, photo, email address (if registered); IP address, operating system, technical device info
- **Content:** Photos and videos uploaded for editing; designs created in the app
- **Server-Side:** Copies of photos and videos processed through the service, retained for a few days
- **Device:** IP address, operating system, technical information
- **Activity:** App usage data, survey responses
- **Social Login:** Google, Meta, Apple sign-in data (optional)

### Notable Excerpts

> "We collect some raw information which may be considered as personally identifiable, which is technical information about your device, such as your IP address, your operating system and other technical information. Moreover, when you register, we may retain the information you provided us at registration, including your name, photo and email address."

> "If you use one of our server side services, we may also retain a copy of the data submitted to our server for a limited time. This Server Side Data may include a copy of the photos and videos processed through the service, as well as technical data relating to it. Server Side Data is deleted periodically after a short period of time, which is usually a few days."

> "We use the following third party services: (i) Apple Analytics; (ii) Facebook; (iii) AppsFlyer; (iv) Amplitude; (v) OneSignal; (vi) Google Payments; (vii) Google Analytics; (viii) Firebase; (ix) Hotjar Analytics; (x) Sentry.io."

> "We may also use the following services to process images, videos or provide you parts of the services: (i) Unsplash; (ii) Noun Project; (iii) Pexels; (iv) Replicate Inc.; (v) Soundstripe; (vi) Google Gemini and Cloud Vertex AI's Imagen; (vii) OpenAI ChatGPT and GPT Image."

> "We may use the information you provided us, including your email address and phone numbers to provide you with promotional offers and newsletters."

> "You hereby grant us permission to use your name, image, information and likeness in relation to the content you posted. Meaning, that we may use your face, photos and profile photo to promote the Content."

> "We use Apple's Vision Framework on iOS to inspect whether photos contain a person's face or not. However, in such use, your face data is not retained if the photo is found to include face data."

> "There Is No Mandatory Signup. We do not coerce you into signing up for the service."

> "You can always review your information using our software and panel. You can also request to delete all relevant information."
