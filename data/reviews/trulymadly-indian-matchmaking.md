## Summary Card

**App:** TrulyMadly: Indian MatchMaking  
**Developer:** CRESCERE TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED  
**Overall Risk:** Caution (Score: 64/100, Grade: C)

### Red Flags
- **Matches can share your profile with their social networks:** The privacy policy says TrulyMadly could add features that let your matches share your profile information — including your photos — with people on their social networks outside the app. You have no control over this.
- **Collects very personal and sensitive data:** TrulyMadly collects your email, phone number, home address, age, sex, partner preferences, personality assessment answers, photos, videos, and verification documents.
- **Your full profile and verification results are shown to matches:** TrulyMadly shows your profile details, photos, videos, and even your verification check results to the people it recommends as matches for you.
- **Privacy policy appears outdated:** The privacy policy carries a 2022 copyright date and does not mention many important modern privacy topics.
- **Third-party products within the app also collect your data:** The policy says TrulyMadly's products and services may include third-party offerings, and when you use those, they may collect and use your information too.

### Green Flags
- **Contact information never shared with matches:** TrulyMadly explicitly promises that your email address and phone number are never shown to the people you are matched with.
- **Credit card details handled by payment gateway:** When you make purchases, your credit card information goes directly to PayU or HDFC payment gateway. TrulyMadly does not store your card details, except if you have auto-renewal enabled.
- **You can request deletion of your data:** You can email TrulyMadly's support team from your registered email address and ask them to delete your data from their records.
- **Can opt out of marketing emails:** You can choose to stop receiving promotional emails, newsletters, and special offers from TrulyMadly.

### Verdict
TrulyMadly is one of India's largest matchmaking apps. It collects a wide range of personal information including your name, email, phone number, address, age, photos, videos, partner preferences, and detailed personality profile from a compatibility assessment. This profile data — including verification results and photos — is shown to your matches. Most concerningly, the app may enable matches to share your profile information and photos with members of their social networks. The app uses cookies, web beacons, and mobile analytics for tracking, and may share non-personally identifiable demographic data with advertisers. On the positive side, payment card details are handled by third-party payment gateways (not stored by TrulyMadly except for auto-renewal cases), contact information is never shared with matches, and you can request data deletion via email. However, the privacy policy is dated (2022), vague on several key categories, and does not explain how policy changes are communicated.
