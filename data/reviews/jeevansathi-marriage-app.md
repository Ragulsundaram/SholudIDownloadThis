## Summary Card

**App:** Jeevansathi.com: Marriage App  
**Overall Risk:** Caution — **Score: 56 / Grade: C**

### Red Flags
- **Profile details visible to all website visitors.** Your age, height, education, income, and other profile details can be seen by anyone who visits the website, even people who haven't signed up.
- **Collects caste, religion, and income details.** The app asks for highly personal information including your religion, sub-caste, ethnic origin, physical and mental conditions, and income, which are considered sensitive personal data.
- **Government IDs required for verification.** To verify your profile, the app may ask you to upload official documents like your passport, driver's license, or income proof.
- **Phone calls may be recorded.** When you talk to customer support on the phone, your conversation may be recorded and monitored to check if it follows their rules.

### Green Flags
- **Does not store payment card details.** The app does not store your credit or debit card information. Payments are handled by external payment processors.
- **You can delete your profile.** You have the option to delete your profile and associated data from the platform.
- **Only for adults 18 and older.** The app is meant only for people who are 18 years or older, and minors must use it under parental supervision.

### Verdict
A popular Indian matrimony app that asks for deeply personal details including religion, caste, income, and family information, and shares your profile data with advertisers and business partners. While it provides a genuine matchmaking service, users should understand the extent of personal and sensitive data collected.

## Category Breakdown

### Camera & Microphone — Caution
You can upload photos and videos to your profile. The app may ask to access your camera or photo album when you do this.

### Location & GPS — Risky
The app collects your current location, city, and pin code. It also gets device location when you use the app.

### Contacts & Phonebook — Caution
If you sign in using Facebook or Google, the app may get access to information from your social media profile, which could include your contacts.

### Storage & File Access — Caution
You can upload documents like your passport, income proof, or address proof for profile verification. This is optional.

### Data Sharing with 3rd Parties — Risky
Your data is shared with group companies, advertisers (including banks and insurance companies), service providers, and law enforcement when required.

### Account & Identity Data — Risky
You must provide extensive personal details including your name, email, phone number, date of birth, gender, height, occupation, income, religion, and caste to create a profile.

### Behavioural & Ad Tracking — Risky
The app tracks your activity on the platform using cookies and pixel tags, and shows you targeted ads from third-party advertisers including banks and insurance companies.

### Children's Data Handling — Caution
The app is only for adults 18 and older. Minors can use it only if a parent or guardian is supervising them.

### Network & Internet Activity — Caution
The app logs your IP address, browser type, operating system, and what you click on while using the website.

### Device Info & Fingerprinting — Caution
The app collects technical information about your device and system when you access the platform, including device location.

### Health & Biometric Data — Caution
You can optionally share information about your physical and mental conditions on your profile. The app considers this sensitive personal data.

### Financial & Payment Data — Safe
The app does not store your credit or debit card information. Payments are handled by external processors.

### Data Deletion & User Rights — Risky
You can delete your profile, but the app does not say exactly how long they keep your data after that. They say they keep it only as long as necessary for legal and business reasons.

### Policy Change Notification — Unknown
The privacy policy says it can change at any time and the new version applies from the date it is published. It does not say how you will be told about the changes.
