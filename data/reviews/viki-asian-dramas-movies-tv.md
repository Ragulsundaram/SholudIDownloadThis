## Summary Card

**Overall Risk:** Caution
**Score:** 59 / 100
**Grade:** C

### Red Flags
- Your browsing is recorded with session replay and shared with third-party advertisers
- Your data flows across the entire Rakuten corporate family, including the Rakuten ID program
- AI algorithms profile your preferences and characteristics based on everything you watch
- Your approximate location is inferred from your IP address and shared with ad partners

### Green Flags
- You can opt out of targeted ads and data sale via a Do Not Sell link and Global Privacy Control
- You can fully delete your account and exercise data portability rights
- No health, biometric, or contact data is collected

### Verdict
Viki tracks your viewing habits extensively and shares your data with Rakuten affiliates and advertising networks. While opt-out tools exist for targeted ads, the default experience involves significant behavioral profiling and data sharing.

## Category Breakdown

### Camera & Microphone — Safe
No camera or microphone access is required for core streaming functionality.

### Location & GPS — Risky
IP address is used to infer city/country/zip for content personalization and regional licensing. This inferred location is shared with third-party advertisers.

### Contacts & Phonebook — Safe
No contact or address book access is mentioned in the policy.

### Storage & File Access — Caution
Optional temporary downloads for offline viewing on supported subscription plans.

### Data Sharing with 3rd Parties — Risky
Extensive sharing with service providers, business partners, third-party advertisers, Rakuten Group affiliates, and law enforcement. The policy explicitly acknowledges this may constitute a sale under California law.

### Account & Identity Data — Risky
Account data is linked to the broader Rakuten ID program when using the same credentials across Rakuten services. Social login options share profile data from Facebook and Google.

### Behavioural & Ad Tracking — Risky
Cookies, pixels, session replay, and AI are used to track viewing history, searches, and browsing behavior. Data is shared with third-party ad networks for targeted advertising.

### Children's Data Handling — Caution
Explicit 18+ policy, but no robust age verification mechanism. Some content may appeal to teenagers.

### Network & Internet Activity — Risky
Extensive logging of pages viewed, content consumed, search queries, and browsing behavior. Session replay technology records detailed on-screen interactions.

### Device Info & Fingerprinting — Risky
Collects advertising IDs, IP addresses, user agents, device models, OS versions, browser types, and local storage data. Shared with third-party advertisers.

### Health & Biometric Data — Safe
Explicitly states it does not intentionally collect health or biometric data.

### Financial & Payment Data — Caution
Partial card data (last 4 digits, expiry, issuing bank country) is collected. Full payment processing is handled by Stripe.

### Data Deletion & User Rights — Caution
Account deletion and data rights are available, but some data may be retained for legal obligations or claims.

### Policy Change Notification — Caution
Changes are notified via email or in-app, but continued use constitutes acceptance.

## Raw Policy Notes

### Key Excerpts

> "Identifiers, such as unique identifiers for devices you use to access the Rakuten Viki Service (for example, the IP address, an app/device identifier, user agent, and advertising industry standard unique identifier)."

> "Internet and other electronic network activity information, such as how and when you use or access the Rakuten Viki Service (for example, date and time of visits, pages you view, types and frequency of content or genres you view, expressed content preferences or favorites you identify, subtitles or segments of content you edit, browsing you do, and searches you make)."

> "Records of your browsing sessions and other activities on the Rakuten Viki Service using session replay technology."

> "We permit advertisements to be delivered to you on the Rakuten Viki Service by third-party advertisers... advertisements may be tailored to your interests by the third-party advertisers based on preferences inferred from information they collect about your behavior on other sites and services, as well as Personal Information we may share with them according to our contractual terms."

> "If you choose to connect to the Rakuten Viki Service and another Rakuten ID participating service using the same username or email address, Viki will securely share your account credentials with the Rakuten ID program for future account authentication purposes."

> "In California, the law identifies Personal Information disclosure for the purposes of cross-context behavioral advertising as a 'sale' or 'sharing' regardless of whether money is exchanged for it."

### Notable Terms of Use Provisions

- Mandatory binding arbitration with class action waiver
- Session monitoring and tracking technology disclosed
- Broad perpetual license for user-generated content and subtitle submissions
- Non-refundable payments except where required by law

### Review Notes
- The policy is detailed and transparent about data practices, including explicit acknowledgment of California "sale" definitions
- Opt-out mechanisms are clearly provided but jurisdiction-dependent
- The Rakuten corporate family data sharing is a significant privacy consideration
- Session replay technology is an unusually invasive tracking method for a streaming service
