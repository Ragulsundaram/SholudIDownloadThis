## Summary Card

### Red Flags
- **Data shared widely with advertisers and affiliates.** Meesho shares your personal information with holding companies, subsidiaries, outside advertisers, and third-party marketers who may contact you about their own products and services.
- **Tracks your other apps and device ID for ads.** Meesho collects which other apps are installed on your phone and shares your device advertising ID with ad companies so they can track what you do and show you more ads.
- **Contacts and address book accessed from device.** Meesho may access the personal contacts and address book information stored on your mobile device, and it collects contact details of people you add to orders.
- **Government IDs like Aadhaar and PAN collected.** Meesho collects sensitive identity documents including your Aadhaar number, PAN, and other tax identification numbers as part of its identity verification process.
- **Last visited website and browsing activity logged.** Meesho records the last web page you visited before opening their app, along with your clicks, search queries, and pages viewed, and uses this to show you ads.

### Green Flags
- **Deletion request available via Grievance Officer.** You can request deletion of your personal information by contacting the Grievance Officer, and the company promises to delete or de-identify your data after the retention period.
- **Payments handled by PCI compliant processors.** Your credit or payment card information is shared only with PCI compliant payment gateway processors to complete your orders.
- **Claims to delete underage accounts if discovered.** Meesho says its services are not for users under 18 and promises to delete the account and related information if it learns a user is underage.

### Verdict
Meesho collects an unusually broad range of personal data for a shopping app, including government IDs, your contacts, and even a list of your other installed apps. It then uses this information for advertising and shares it with a wide network of affiliates and third-party marketers. While the app offers basic protections like PCI-compliant payments and a Grievance Officer for deletion requests, the overall privacy posture is weak. If you value keeping your identity documents, contacts, and app habits private, you should think carefully before installing.
