## Summary

SHEIN India's privacy policy reveals broad data collection including your identity, financial details, location, browsing behavior, and even camera/microphone access. While the app uses third-party payment gateways for transactions and offers standard security measures, it explicitly collects financial information, tracks your location via Bluetooth and WiFi, and shares data with affiliates and external organizations. The policy changes are accepted automatically through continued use, and data is stored and processed in India.

Key concerns: explicit collection of financial data, broad sharing with affiliates and third parties, location tracking via multiple signals, camera/microphone access, and automatic acceptance of policy changes.

Key positives: dedicated grievance officer, opt-out for marketing, secure third-party payment gateways, no health/biometric data collection, and industry-standard security measures.

## Summary Card

### SHEIN India Fast Fashion
- **Overall Risk:** Caution
- **Score:** 64/100 (Grade C)

**Red Flags:**
- **Collects and stores your financial information.** SHEIN India explicitly collects sensitive financial data including your bank account, credit card, and debit card details as part of its Sensitive Personal Data collection.
- **Shares data with affiliates and third parties.** Your personal information is shared with affiliates for a seamless shopping experience, and with third-party service providers like payment gateways and logistics partners. Data may also be disclosed for legal or business transfer reasons.
- **Tracks your location via Bluetooth and WiFi.** SHEIN India collects your location data through Bluetooth, WiFi signals, and GPS. While they claim this is anonymous, it can still be used to track where you are.
- **Policy changes accepted automatically.** If SHEIN updates its privacy policy, your continued use of the app counts as accepting the new terms. They won't ask you to agree again.
- **Accesses camera and microphone.** SHEIN India may request access to your camera and microphone for features like voice search and image uploads. Refusing may limit some services.

**Green Flags:**
- **Has a dedicated grievance officer.** SHEIN India has appointed a Grievance Officer (Savan Bekal) with a dedicated email for handling privacy complaints.
- **Easy opt-out for marketing.** You can unsubscribe from promotional emails by clicking the unsubscribe link or contacting customer care.
- **Payments handled by secure third-party gateways.** Your financial transactions are processed through approved third-party payment gateways that use encryption.
- **No health or biometric data collected.** The app does not collect health information or biometric data like fingerprints.
- **Uses industry-standard security.** SHEIN India claims to use firewalls, transport layer security, and other physical and electronic security measures to protect your data.

**Verdict:** SHEIN India's privacy policy reveals broad data collection including your identity, financial details, location, browsing behavior, and even camera/microphone access. While the app uses third-party payment gateways for transactions and offers standard security measures, it explicitly collects financial information, tracks your location via Bluetooth and WiFi, and shares data with affiliates and external organizations. The policy changes are accepted automatically through continued use, and data is stored and processed in India.
