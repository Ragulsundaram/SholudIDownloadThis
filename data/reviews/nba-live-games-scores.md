### Red Flags
- **Data shared with advertisers and the NBA Family:** The policy says your personal data is shared with third-party advertising services, business partners, and across all NBA Family companies for marketing and building profiles about your interests.
- **Tracks you across other websites and apps for ads:** The policy says they use cookies and tracking tools to follow your activity across other websites and apps, then use that information to show you targeted ads. They also build predictive models about what you might do next.
- **Collects your precise location:** The policy says they collect your exact GPS location and use it to deliver location-based services, show ads, and comply with territorial restrictions. If you deny location access, some features may not work.
- **Collects detailed device data for profiling:** The policy says they collect your device IP address, unique device identifiers, device model, browser settings, software installed, and other device characteristics to identify you and target ads.
- **May collect government identification:** The policy lists government identification as part of the contact information they may collect, which is unusually sensitive for a sports streaming app.

### Green Flags
- **You can request deletion of your data:** The policy says you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by visiting privacy.nba.com or calling their support line.
- **You can opt out of targeted advertising:** The policy says you can opt out of targeted ads and certain data sales by using the Global Privacy Control signal in your browser or visiting the Your Privacy Choices link.
- **Does not knowingly collect data from children under 13:** The policy says their services are not directed at children under 16 and they do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without parental consent.

### Verdict
The NBA app collects extensive personal data including precise location, detailed device identifiers, and comprehensive browsing activity. It shares this data across the NBA Family and with third-party advertising partners to build profiles and show targeted ads. While users have some rights to opt out and request deletion, the default data practices are broad and heavily oriented toward marketing. If you are privacy-conscious, consider whether the benefits of using the app outweigh the extensive tracking.

## Detailed Analysis

### Data Collection Overview

The NBA privacy policy covers the entire NBA Family of products and services, including the NBA app. It describes extensive data collection across multiple categories:

**Contact and Identity Data:** Name, email address, postal address, phone number, government identification, username, password, and demographic data (age, gender). Notably, government ID is listed as a collected data type, which is unusual for a sports entertainment app.

**Payment Data:** Credit card information and billing address for League Pass subscriptions and other purchases.

**Device and Activity Data:** Comprehensive collection through automated means including cookies, web beacons, web server logs, and other tracking technologies. The policy explicitly mentions collecting clicks, keystrokes, cursor movements, page scrolls, emails opened, content engagement, referring URLs, and clickstream data.

**Location Data:** Both general and precise geolocation data is collected, with a warning that declining location access may disable certain features.

**Inferred Interests:** The NBA builds predictive models and infers interests and preferences from collected data, including favorite teams, home city, and communication preferences.

### Data Sharing

The policy discloses sharing personal data with:
- The entire NBA Family (NBA, WNBA, G League, NBA 2K League, Basketball Africa League, and all 30 teams)
- Third-party advertising and analytics vendors
- Business partners for sweepstakes, media, retail, and technology
- Data brokers and public sources (as sources of data)
- Social media platforms when accounts are connected

The policy explicitly acknowledges that some sharing activities "may be considered 'sales' under certain laws" and provides opt-out mechanisms.

### User Rights

Users have the ability to:
- Opt out of targeted advertising via Global Privacy Control or the "Your Privacy Choices" link
- Request access, correction, or deletion of personal data via privacy.nba.com or (844) 717-3601
- Adjust communications preferences in account settings
- Disconnect social media account connections

However, the policy notes that data may be retained for legal obligations, dispute resolution, and agreement enforcement even after deletion requests.

### Policy Change Handling

Changes are posted on the policy page with an updated date. The policy encourages periodic review but does not promise advance notice or require active re-agreement.

### Children's Data

The policy states services are not directed at children under 16 and they do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Youth program registrations are directed at parents/guardians.
