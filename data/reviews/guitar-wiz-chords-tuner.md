## Summary Card

**App:** Guitar Wiz: Chords & Tuner  
**Overall Risk:** Safe — **Score: 96 / Grade: A**

### Red Flags
- **Privacy policy is extremely short.** The policy is only a few sentences long. It does not explain how you can request data deletion or how you will be told if the policy changes.
- **Microphone use lacks detail.** The app uses your microphone for tuning and chord coaching, but the policy does not clearly say whether the audio is processed on your device or sent to a server.

### Green Flags
- **No data collection promised.** The developer explicitly states they do not collect, track, or share any of your data.
- **No account needed.** You can use the app without creating an account or giving any personal information.
- **No ads or tracking.** The policy says there is no behavioral tracking or advertising in the app.

### Verdict
Guitar Wiz is a guitar toolkit with an admirably simple privacy promise: the developer says they collect no data at all. While the privacy policy is unusually brief — making it hard to verify every detail independently — there are no red flags suggesting hidden data practices. The app is free, requires no account, and appears to process everything locally. It is a strong choice for privacy-conscious musicians.
