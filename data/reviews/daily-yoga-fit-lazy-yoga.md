## Summary Card

### Daily Yoga: Fit & Lazy Yoga®
**Risk Level:** Caution | **Score:** 78/100 | **Grade:** B

#### Red Flags
- **Reserves right to sell data in the future:** Daily Yoga says it does not sell your personal information right now, but it explicitly reserves the right to start doing so in the future. If that happens, you would need to proactively opt out via email.
- **Privacy policy can change without any notice:** Daily Yoga can update its privacy policy at any time without telling you beforehand. Changes take effect immediately when posted on the website.
- **Device ID collected for account binding:** Daily Yoga reads your phone state and collects your device ID to link it with your account, which helps them recognise your device across sessions.
- **Third-party ad companies track your visits:** Advertising companies that work with Daily Yoga may track your visits to the app and website using cookies to show you ads based on your behaviour.

#### Green Flags
- **Does not currently sell personal data:** Daily Yoga explicitly states that it does not sell or rent your personal information to any third parties at this time.
- **Delete Account available in Profile Settings:** You can delete your account and request data removal directly inside the app through the Profile Settings, or by emailing support.
- **Promises permanent anonymization on deletion:** When you request deletion, Daily Yoga says it will permanently and irreversibly anonymize your data so it can never be used to identify you again.
- **Uses encryption and secure storage:** Daily Yoga uses advanced encryption for data transmission and storage, and restricts access to sensitive data to authorized personnel only.
- **GoogleFit data adheres to limited-use policy:** If you connect GoogleFit, Daily Yoga says it follows Google's strict limited-use policy for any health data received through that connection.

#### Verdict
Daily Yoga is a well-established yoga app with relatively restrained data practices. It does not currently sell personal information, offers a clear in-app 'Delete Account' option, and promises permanent anonymization upon request. However, it collects your device ID, IP address, and browsing behaviour through third-party ad companies. Most concerning is the explicit reservation of the right to sell personal data in the future and the ability to change the privacy policy without any advance notice.

## Category Breakdown

### Camera & Microphone — Caution
Daily Yoga asks for camera access only if you want to take and share photos in the community. It is not required for yoga classes.

**Policy excerpt:** *"Daily Yoga will require permission to access your camera which is used to take photos when you voluntarily share your photos in the Daily Yoga Community."*

**Concerns:**
- Camera access requested for community photos
- Photos shared publicly in community

### Location & GPS — Caution
Daily Yoga asks for location access only if you want to geotag your posts in the community. If enabled, others can see your location on your posts.

**Policy excerpt:** *"Location permission is required for geotagging of your posts. If enabled, your location will be shown to others when you make a post in the Daily Yoga Community."*

**Concerns:**
- Location shown to others in community posts
- Geotagging optional but visible

### Contacts & Phonebook — Safe
The privacy policy does not mention any access to your contacts or phonebook.

### Storage & File Access — Safe
The privacy policy does not mention any broad file or storage access beyond camera photos for community sharing.

### Data Sharing with 3rd Parties — Caution
Daily Yoga does not currently sell or rent your personal data, but it reserves the right to do so in the future. It also uses third-party advertising companies and may disclose data to law enforcement.

**Policy excerpt:** *"DailyYoga.com does not sell or rent personal information about its customers to any third parties at this time. In the event DailyYoga.com does engage in such commerce in the future, you will have an opportunity to remove yourself from any such lists before your inclusion therein."*

**Concerns:**
- Future data selling reserved
- Third-party ad companies used
- Law enforcement disclosure
- GoogleFit data sharing if connected

### Account & Identity Data — Caution
Daily Yoga collects your name, email, address, phone number, and specific location only when you voluntarily provide them, such as during registration.

**Policy excerpt:** *"DailyYoga.com does not collect personal information -- names, addresses, phone numbers, e-mail addresses, and specific locations -- about you except when you specifically provide such information voluntarily"*

**Concerns:**
- Personal data collected on voluntary basis
- Profile info shared in community if user opts in

### Behavioural & Ad Tracking — Caution
Daily Yoga uses cookies and works with third-party advertising companies that track your visits to show you interest-based ads. You can opt out through the Network Advertising Initiative.

**Policy excerpt:** *"DailyYoga.com uses third-party advertising companies to serve ads and the third-party advertising companies may use non-personally identifiable information about a user's visits to DailyYoga.com to provide advertisements about goods and services"*

**Concerns:**
- Third-party ad tracking
- Cookies used for tracking
- Interest-based ads

### Children's Data Handling — Caution
Daily Yoga says children are not eligible and minors should use the app only with parents or guardians. It promises to delete data if a child under 13 is discovered.

**Policy excerpt:** *"Children are not eligible to use our services and we ask that minors do not submit any personal information to us. If you are a minor, you can use this service only in conjunction with your parents or guardians. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personally identifiable Information, we will delete such information from our files."*

**Concerns:**
- No technical age verification described
- Minors can use with parents only

### Network & Internet Activity — Caution
Daily Yoga logs your IP address for server diagnostics, to gather broad demographic data, and to determine your country and region for activity rankings.

**Policy excerpt:** *"DailyYoga.com uses your Internet Protocol (IP) address to help diagnose problems with our computer server and to administer our website. Your IP address is used to help identify you and to gather broad demographic data."*

**Concerns:**
- IP address logged
- Used for demographic profiling
- Used for rankings region detection

### Device Info & Fingerprinting — Caution
Daily Yoga reads your phone state to collect your device ID and network type. The device ID is used to bind your phone to your account, and the network type helps reduce unnecessary data usage.

**Policy excerpt:** *"Daily Yoga requires permission to read your Phone State, such as Device ID to bind your device with your Daily Yoga Account and Network Type to help avoid unnecessary data consumption."*

**Concerns:**
- Device ID collected
- Phone state accessed
- Network type tracked

### Health & Biometric Data — Safe
Daily Yoga does not explicitly collect health or biometric data like heart rate or body measurements. Optional GoogleFit connection follows limited-use policy.

**Policy excerpt:** *"DailyYoga's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the requirements for limited use."*

**Concerns:**
- None

### Financial & Payment Data — Safe
The privacy policy does not mention any collection or storage of payment or financial information.

### Data Deletion & User Rights — Caution
You can delete your account through Profile Settings in the app, or email support. Daily Yoga promises to permanently and irreversibly anonymize your data so it can never identify you again.

**Policy excerpt:** *"To request the deletion of your personal data that we have in the Daily Yoga App, please find the 'Delete Account' in the Profile Settings. Or you can email us at support@dailyyoga.com to request the deletion, and we will respond to your request in a reasonable timeframe. Upon your request, Daily Yoga will permanently and irrevocably anonymize your data such that it can never be reconstructed to identify you as an individual."*

**Concerns:**
- Deletion is anonymization, not erasure
- No specific timeframe given

### Policy Change Notification — Risky
Daily Yoga can change its privacy policy at any time without warning you. Changes take effect immediately when posted on the website.

**Policy excerpt:** *"We reserve the right, at any time and without notice, to add to, change, update, or modify this Privacy Policy, simply by posting such change, update, or modification on the Site. Any such change, update, or modification will be effective immediately upon posting on the Site."*

**Concerns:**
- No advance notice
- Changes effective immediately
- No explicit re-consent required
