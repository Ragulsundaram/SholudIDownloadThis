## Summary Card

**Overall Risk:** Caution  
**Score:** 64 / 100 (Grade C)

### Red Flags
- **Data shared across Tata Group and ad partners:** Westside shares your personal data with Tata Group Entities, marketing agencies, analytics providers, and advertising partners to show you personalised ads and offer products from other Tata companies.
- **Shopping habits power ads across the internet:** Westside uses your purchase and browsing history to show you targeted ads on social media and other websites, and combines data from multiple sources to build a richer profile of you.
- **Data may be processed outside India:** Westside says your personal data may be processed in countries other than India, which could mean different data protection standards apply.
- **Privacy policy can change without warning:** Westside reserves the right to change its privacy policy with or without notice, and your continued use of the app counts as acceptance of any changes.
- **Broad device and browsing data collected:** Westside collects your IP address, device connection information, browser type, the websites you come from and go to, and email patterns.

### Green Flags
- **Explicit no-sale commitment:** Westside explicitly states that it does not trade or sell personal data in any manner, except as specified in the policy or with your express consent.
- **DPDP Act rights clearly listed:** Westside explicitly lists your rights under India's DPDP Act, including access to your data, correction, erasure, consent withdrawal, and grievance redressal.
- **Deletion available on request:** You can request deletion of your personal data, and Westside says it will delete it unless required to keep it by law.
- **Encryption and access controls mentioned:** Westside mentions using encryption, access controls, and periodic audits to protect your personal data.

### Verdict
Westside's privacy policy reveals broad data collection including your purchase history, browsing habits, device information, and contact details. This data is shared with Tata Group Entities, marketing agencies, analytics providers, and advertising partners to show you personalised ads across the internet and social media. Data may also be processed outside India. On the positive side, Westside explicitly states it does not trade or sell personal data, provides clear rights under India's DPDP Act, and implements encryption and access controls to protect your information.
