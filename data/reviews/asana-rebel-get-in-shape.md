## Summary Card

**App:** Asana Rebel: Get in Shape  
**Overall Risk:** Caution  
**Score:** 69 / 100 (Grade C)

### Red Flags
- **Data shared with extensive third-party ecosystem.** Asana Rebel shares your data with Google (Analytics, Ads, Firebase, Crashlytics), Facebook, Adjust, Amplitude, and Pinterest. This includes your device info, usage patterns, and browsing behavior.
- **Extensive behavioral advertising and tracking.** The app uses cookies, tracking pixels, DoubleClick, Google AdWords, Dynamic Remarketing, Firebase advertising IDs, Facebook Custom Audiences, and Adjust to track your behavior across apps and show you personalized ads.
- **Camera access requested for profile photos.** The app asks for access to your camera or photos so you can upload or change a profile photo. This is optional but means the app can access your photo library.
- **Privacy policy is outdated.** The privacy policy was last revised in January 2022 and may not accurately reflect the app's current data collection practices, especially if new features have been added since then.
- **Data may be stored outside your country.** The company uses cloud services and stores data on servers that may be located outside the European Union or European Economic Area, including in the United States.

### Green Flags
- **Strong GDPR rights listed.** You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, and export your data. You can also object to processing and revoke consent at any time.
- **Data sharing limited to necessary purposes.** The company states that your personal data is only transferred to third parties with your consent or when necessary for the service, and the extent is kept to a minimum.
- **Policy changes require notification and acceptance.** The company says they will notify you of privacy policy changes and ask you to read and accept them before they take effect, rather than making changes silently.
- **No explicit mention of selling data.** The privacy policy does not state that the company sells your personal data to third parties for profit.

### Verdict
Asana Rebel collects detailed workout and usage data and shares it with a broad network of advertising and analytics partners including Google and Facebook. While the app provides strong GDPR rights and limits data transfers to the minimum necessary, the extensive behavioral tracking and advertising integration, combined with an outdated privacy policy from 2022, make it a mediocre choice for privacy-conscious users.
