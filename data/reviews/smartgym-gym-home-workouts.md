# SmartGym: Gym & Home Workouts — Privacy Review

**Analyzed:** 2026-05-18
**Policy URL:** https://smartgymapp.com/privacy/
**Developer:** Mateus Abras
**Platform:** iOS

## Summary Card

**App:** SmartGym: Gym & Home Workouts  
**Overall Risk:** Safe  
**Score:** 87 / 100 (Grade A)

### Red Flags
- **Several privacy categories are not addressed.** The privacy policy is very short and does not mention whether the app accesses your camera, microphone, location, or contacts. We have to assume these are not used, but the app does not explicitly say so.
- **User data may be transferred in a business sale.** If SmartGym is ever sold, merges with another company, or partners with another business, your user information could be handed over as part of the deal.
- **Privacy policy can change without notice.** SmartGym says it can change the privacy policy at any time. It does not promise to tell you directly when this happens.

### Green Flags
- **Explicit promise: data is never sold or shared.** SmartGym makes a clear promise that any information about you will never be provided, published, marketed, or sold under any circumstances. This is one of the strongest privacy commitments we have seen.
- **You can keep all data on your device only.** You have the option to store all your workout data only on your phone, without ever uploading it to the cloud. If you do use cloud sync, the developer still promises not to share that data.
- **HealthKit data only collected manually with consent.** The app only reads your Apple Watch health data (heart rate, calories, distance, activity rings) if you explicitly approve it and only after you manually start a workout. It never automatically begins tracking you.
- **Analytics are completely anonymous.** The app uses Mixpanel to understand how people use the app, but it only sends anonymous statistics. SmartGym says it never shares any of your personal data with Mixpanel.
- **You can erase all data completely at any time.** You can delete all your data from SmartGym whenever you want. The developer also promises they have never bought user databases and never will.

### Verdict
SmartGym is a rare gem in the fitness app world. Its privacy policy is short but unambiguous: your data is never sold, never shared, and you can keep it entirely on your device if you prefer. HealthKit integration is handled responsibly with manual consent. The trade-offs are a policy that leaves some categories unaddressed and the possibility of data transfer in a business sale. For privacy-conscious users who want to track workouts without handing over their health data to a data-hungry corporation, SmartGym is an excellent choice.
