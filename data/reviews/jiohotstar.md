## Summary of Changes
This is a strict legal-safe re-analysis of the JioHotstar privacy policy.

- All claims now have direct verbatim quotes in `policy_excerpt`.
- Removed strong verbs and invented purposes. Wording softened to "states that," "may," "claims to."
- No "sells your data" language (policy does not use the word "sell").
- No "tracks you across apps" language unless explicitly stated (policy mentions cross-device linking and third-party ad exchanges).
- Score unchanged at 56 / Grade C / Risk `caution`.

## Key Strict Findings

1. **Ad Tracking:** Explicitly supported by text: "We or our service providers may also share your advertisement identifiers such as IDFA, GAID etc. with advertising / data exchanges..."
2. **Cross-device linking:** Explicitly supported: "linking some or all of your Personal Information with your activity on our Platform across all your streaming devices..."
3. **Data Sharing:** Extensive list of service providers explicitly stated.
4. **Location:** Automatic collection of general location via IP/SIM explicitly stated; precise GPS requires permission.
5. **Partial card storage:** Explicitly stated: first 6 + last 4 digits retained.

## Remaining Ambiguities

- The policy does not explicitly explain why kids mode exists alongside an 18+ registration requirement.
