## Summary Card

**App:** JioHotstar  
**Overall Risk:** Risky (Grade D)  
**Score:** 45/100

### Red Flags
- **Extensive behavioral tracking** - The policy states they track everything you watch, when you watch it, your device info, and use advertising identifiers (IDFA, GAID) to show you personalized ads across all your devices
- **Data shared with advertising partners** - Your information is shared with service providers, advertising networks, business partners, analytics providers, and group companies
- **Precise GPS location collected** - With your permission, the app collects your exact GPS location for localized content and ads
- **Unique device identifiers used** - Device IDs, advertising identifiers, and unique customer identifiers track your activity across devices
- **IP address used for location** - Your IP address is continuously logged and used to determine your approximate location
- **Long data retention** - Marketing data is explicitly kept for the maximum period allowed by law

### Green Flags
- **Users can opt out of interest-based ads** - The policy states you can opt out via device privacy settings
- **Account deletion available** - Users can request erasure of personal information and close their account
- **Data portability rights** - In some jurisdictions, users can request copies of their data in machine-readable format
- **Kid's mode feature** - Provides access exclusively to children-friendly content when activated

### Verdict
JioHotstar collects extensive data for targeted advertising and shares it with numerous third parties. While some opt-out controls exist, the default settings enable broad tracking of your viewing habits and device activity. The app is rated Risky (Grade D) due to dangerous levels of data sharing and behavioral tracking. Consider if the content is worth the privacy trade-off.

## Analysis Notes

### Key Findings

**1. Extensive Data Sharing (Dangerous)**
The policy explicitly states advertising identifiers (IDFA, GAID) are shared with "advertising / data exchanges" to "decide which ads you see." Data is also shared with service providers, business partners, affiliates, and group companies.

**2. Behavioral Ad Tracking (Dangerous)**
Comprehensive tracking includes: pages viewed, load times, watch times, content selection, watch history, search queries, transactions, and platform features used. This data is linked across all your streaming devices.

**3. Location Collection (Risky)**
General location is collected automatically via IP address and SIM card. Precise GPS location is collected with explicit permission and combined with other information for personalized ads.

**4. Device Fingerprinting (Risky)**
Collects device ID, model, OS, installed version, time zone, carrier, screen resolution, and advertising identifiers. Also collects information about apps frequently used on your device.

**5. Data Retention**
Marketing data is retained for "the maximum period allowed by law in your territory." Some data is retained for fraud detection and legal obligations even after deletion requests.

### Areas of Concern
- No opt-out from sharing with service providers
- Activity linked across devices via unique platform identifier
- Policy changes effective immediately with continued use = acceptance
- Partial credit card digits (first 6 + last 4) retained for support

### Positive Aspects
- Some device-level opt-out controls available for ads
- Data deletion request process exists
- Data portability rights in applicable jurisdictions
- Kid's mode for child-friendly content
