## Summary Card

### Seeing AI
- **Overall Risk:** Caution  
- **Score:** 80 / 100 (Grade A)  
- **Recommendation:** Recommended

**Red Flags**
- **Every photo you take is sent to Microsoft** — When you point the camera at something, the photo is sent to Microsoft's computers over the internet so their AI can describe it. The app does not work without sending your photos to the cloud.
- **Facial recognition data stored by Microsoft** — When you save photos of friends or coworkers to recognize them later, those faces are stored on Microsoft's servers. The privacy policy does not say how long this facial data is kept.
- **No app-specific privacy policy** — The privacy policy is Microsoft's massive corporate document with over 320,000 characters. It is not tailored to this app and does not explain how your camera images or facial data are handled.
- **Can access photos from other apps** — The app can describe photos and videos from other apps on your phone, including Mail, Photos, and WhatsApp, sending them to Microsoft's cloud.

**Green Flags**
- **No ads** — The app is completely free and does not show any advertisements.
- **GDPR and data rights available** — As a Microsoft product, you have rights to access, delete, or move your data under European privacy laws.
- **Microsoft has strong security practices** — Microsoft operates major data centers worldwide with security certifications and legal compliance frameworks.
- **Designed with the blind community** — The app was developed in partnership with the blind and low vision community, suggesting genuine accessibility intent.

**Verdict**  
Seeing AI is a powerful accessibility tool that genuinely helps blind and low vision users navigate the world. However, users must accept that every image they capture is sent to Microsoft's cloud for analysis, including photos of people, documents, and personal surroundings. The privacy policy is a generic corporate document that provides almost no detail about how this sensitive visual data is handled.

## Full Analysis

### What it does
Seeing AI is a free app that narrates the world around you using AI. It reads text, describes scenes, recognizes people, scans barcodes, identifies currency, finds personal objects, and provides audio augmented reality navigation. It is designed for and with the blind and low vision community.

### Data collected
- **Camera images:** Every photo captured by the app is sent to Microsoft cloud servers for AI analysis
- **Facial data:** Photos saved for person recognition are stored on Microsoft's servers
- **Photos from other apps:** When using Share sheet or Browse Photos, existing photos are sent to cloud for analysis
- **Device information:** Device type, OS version, capabilities (LiDAR, iOS version)
- **Usage data:** App interaction data, feature usage, diagnostic data
- **Network data:** IP address, browser type, connection data

### Who it is shared with
- **Microsoft cloud services** — All image processing happens on Microsoft servers
- **Third-party service providers** — Fraud prevention, credit risk, legal compliance
- **Law enforcement** — Under legal obligation when required

### Children's Data
The app is rated 4+. Microsoft's privacy policy includes a section on collection of data from children, but parents should be especially cautious given that all camera images (potentially of children) are uploaded to the cloud.

### Data Transfer
Microsoft operates data centers in Australia, Austria, Brazil, Canada, Finland, France, Germany, Hong Kong, India, Ireland, Japan, Korea, Luxembourg, Malaysia, Netherlands, Singapore, South Africa, UK, and US. Data may be stored and processed in any of these locations.

### User Rights
- Microsoft Privacy Dashboard allows viewing and managing some personal data
- GDPR rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection
- Contact: Microsoft privacy support
- No self-service deletion of specific images captured through the app

### Policy History
- **Last updated:** March 2026
- The policy is recent but is a generic Microsoft corporate statement, not tailored to Seeing AI

### Notes for reviewer
- The app is completely free with no in-app purchases
- Microsoft explicitly states they do not use personal files (documents, photos) for ad targeting
- The app requires constant internet connectivity — there is no offline mode
- The 'World' feature uses LiDAR for spatial audio, which is processed locally on the device
- Contact: SeeingAI@Microsoft.com
