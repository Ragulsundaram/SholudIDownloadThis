## What's Good

- **Strong Security Practices**: Skyscanner uses industry-standard security measures and provides data protection training to all employees.
- **Genuine Data Rights**: You can access, correct, delete, or restrict your data through self-service tools or data privacy requests.
- **Transparent Sharing**: The company clearly explains what data is shared with whom and provides links to travel providers' privacy policies before booking.
- **Data Minimization**: Skyscanner aims to use the minimum data necessary and does not use directly identifying information for machine learning training.
- **Adult-Only**: The platform is explicitly not for children under 18.
- **Recent Policy**: The privacy policy was last updated in May 2025, showing active maintenance.

## What's Risky

- **Extensive Third-Party Sharing**: Your data is shared with airlines, hotels, travel agents, 143+ cookie partners, and advertising networks.
- **Facebook Advertising**: Skyscanner creates "custom audiences" and "lookalike audiences" with Facebook for targeted ads, meaning your profile is used to find similar users.
- **Profiling for Ads**: The company combines your searches, bookings, and account data to build a profile of your interests for personalized advertising.
- **Group Company Sharing**: Data is shared with other Skyscanner companies in Brazil, China, India, Japan, Singapore, Spain, the UK, and the US.
- **Passport/ID Collection**: You may need to provide passport or other identification details when booking, which is shared with travel providers.
- **Business Transfer Risk**: If Skyscanner is acquired, your data may be transferred as part of the transaction.
