## Summary Card

**App:** Myntra - Fashion Shopping App  
**Overall Risk:** Caution  
**Score:** 56 / 100 (Grade C)

### Red Flags
- **Terms of Use masquerades as privacy policy.** Myntra links to its Terms of Use page as the privacy policy on the App Store. This document is mostly about shopping rules and seller terms, with very little information about how your personal data is actually collected, used, or protected.
- **Personal data shared with sellers and partners.** When you buy products or use customization services, Myntra shares your name and contact details directly with the sellers and brands. You may also need to share additional personal data with them directly.
- **Government ID required for large purchases.** If you make a purchase of INR 2,00,000 or more, you must upload a scanned copy of your PAN card. For some orders, sellers may also ask for government ID and address proof before delivery.
- **Policy can change without any notice.** Myntra can change, modify, add, or remove any part of its terms at any time without giving you any prior written notice. Your continued use means you automatically accept the changes.
- **Content you upload becomes their property.** Any photos, reviews, comments, or other content you post on Myntra becomes their property. They get a worldwide, perpetual, and transferable right to use it for any purpose including advertising, forever.

### Green Flags
- **Data auto-deleted after 2 years of inactivity.** If you don't use the app for 2 years, Myntra will delete your data including transaction history from their active records.
- **Payments handled by banks and gateways.** Myntra says it is not a bank or financial service. Payments are processed through existing banking infrastructure and credit card payment gateways, not stored directly by Myntra.
- **Phone calls and SMS limited to order updates.** Myntra states it will not use your personal information to send promotional phone calls or SMS. Any contact is limited to order, shipment, or delivery updates.

### Verdict
Myntra is one of India's largest fashion shopping apps, but its privacy practices are concerning. The App Store links to a Terms of Use document as the privacy policy, which contains almost no meaningful privacy disclosures. Many standard privacy categories like camera access, location tracking, and behavioral advertising are not addressed at all. While some positives exist—like automatic data deletion after 2 years and payments through secure gateways—the overall lack of transparency and the broad sharing of your data with sellers should give privacy-conscious users pause.
