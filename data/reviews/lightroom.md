## Summary Card

**App:** Lightroom: AI Photo Editor
**Overall Risk:** Risky (Grade D)
**Score:** 47/100

### Red Flags
- **Content analytics with AI** - Your cloud-stored photos may be analyzed using automated techniques and machine learning for product improvement (though you can opt out)
- **Extensive data sharing** - Your information is shared with Adobe family companies, advertising networks, resellers, data brokers, and third-party service providers
- **Long data retention** - Personal information related to contracts and business transactions is retained for **10 years** after your last interaction
- **Biometric data collection** - Faceprints and voiceprints are collected if you enable face/voice organization features (off by default)
- **Public content remains accessible** - Content shared publicly or on forums may remain visible even after deletion requests
- **Social login data exposure** - Signing in with social media shares your name, country, demographics, and potentially friend lists

### Green Flags
- **Opt-out available** - You can opt out of machine learning analysis of your content at http://www.adobe.com/go/machine_learning_opt_out
- **Biometric features off by default** - Face and voice recognition features are opt-in and can be disabled at any time
- **Content deletion available** - You can delete files, photos, and your Adobe account
- **Privacy controls** - Comprehensive privacy settings, marketing opt-outs, and recognition of Global Privacy Control signals
- **Data Privacy Framework compliance** - Adobe complies with EU-U.S., UK, and Swiss Data Privacy Frameworks

### Verdict
Lightroom offers professional-grade photo editing but comes with significant privacy trade-offs. Adobe collects extensive data about you and your content, shares data broadly with third parties, and retains business records for 10 years. Your photos may be analyzed by AI systems unless you opt out. Consider whether you need the cloud sync features or if local-only editing alternatives would better suit your privacy needs.

## Analysis Notes

### Key Findings

**1. Content Analytics & Machine Learning (Major Concern)**
Adobe performs analytics on cloud-stored content using automated techniques to "improve Services and Software" and provide recommendations. This includes analyzing your content's characteristics. While you can opt out, this analysis is enabled by default.

**2. Extensive Data Sharing (Dangerous)**
Data is shared with:
- Adobe family of companies
- Resellers and sales partners
- Advertising networks and platforms
- Data brokers and information service providers
- Social media platforms (your activity data helps target ads)
- Fraud monitoring providers

**3. Long-Term Data Retention**
While some data is deleted when no longer needed, contract and business transaction data is retained for **10 years** after your last interaction. This is an unusually long retention period.

**4. Biometric Data (Optional but Concerning)**
Faceprints and voiceprints are collected only when you enable specific features that organize photos by face/voice. These features are OFF by default, which is positive. Data is deleted when you disable the feature.

**5. Public Content Persistence**
Content posted on forums, message boards, or shared publicly "can appear in search engine results" and may remain accessible even after Adobe fulfills a deletion request for your personal information.

**6. Comprehensive Tracking**
Adobe tracks:
- IP address, browser, device information
- Search terms that led you to Adobe
- Use and navigation of services
- Interactions with communications
- Content you access and download

### User Rights
- **Access, correction, deletion, restriction rights** - Available under applicable law
- **Data portability** - Can request your data in machine-readable format
- **Objection to direct marketing** - Absolute right to opt-out
- **Global Privacy Control** - Adobe recognizes this browser signal
- **Machine learning opt-out** - http://www.adobe.com/go/machine_learning_opt_out

### Privacy Controls
- Marketing preference settings in Adobe ID profile
- Unsubscribe links in emails
- Cookie controls (though disabling may limit functionality)
- Browser-based opt-outs for advertising
- Data Privacy Framework protections for international transfers

## Verbatim Policy Excerpts

**On Content Analytics:**
> "Subject to your opt-out rights, including those described here, we may perform analytics with Cloud Content to help us to understand how our users are using our Services and Software to allow us to improve your Services and Software experience, provide recommendations to you and customise your experience."

**On Data Sharing:**
> "The types of third parties your information may be disclosed to include: our resellers and other sales and advertising partners, retailers, advertisers, advertising agencies, advertising networks and platforms, information service providers"

**On Data Retention:**
> "Additionally, there is some personal information we need to retain even after you close your account to comply with business and legal requirements, such as personal information related to our contract and business transactions with you, which we retain for ten years after your last interaction with us"

**On Biometric Data:**
> "Biometric identifiers or information defined under United States laws or other applicable laws (e.g., faceprints and voiceprints etc.)... These features are off by default and, should you choose to enable them, you can always disable these features"

**On Public Content:**
> "Such information can appear in search engine results or through other publicly available platforms... Some online content may remain accessible publicly even after Adobe fulfils a request to delete the poster's personal information"
