### Red Flags
- **Shares your email and device ID with marketing partners** — The app shares your username, email address, and Device ID with preferred business partners to send you promotional and marketing materials.
- **Uses device ID to track clicks and show targeted ads** — Your Device ID is utilized to track clicks and other ad fulfillment metrics, which is shared with advertising networks to show you customized ads.
- **Precise location shared with partner apps if opted in** — If you opt in to location services, the app shares your precise real-time geographic location with third-party mobile application providers.

### Green Flags
- **No registration required to use basic workouts** — You can participate as a non-registered user, which limits data collection to only your Device ID and keeps your personal information private.
- **Apple Health data is not mined or sold** — Data synced from your iOS Health App is used solely to provide and improve the workout features, and is never shared with advertisers or third parties without your consent.
- **Face photos are encrypted and never sold** — Uploaded face photos for AI features are encrypted in transit and at rest, processed temporarily, and never shared or sold to advertisers.

### Verdict
Yoga Fit allows you to practice yoga and meditation, sync with Apple Health, and utilize optional AI features by uploading face photos. However, the app collects device IDs and logs IP addresses, and it shares your username, email, and device ID with business partners for marketing. Users can use the app without registering to limit personal data collection. Consider using the app without registering to keep your personal info private, and be mindful of optional features that upload photos or track location.
