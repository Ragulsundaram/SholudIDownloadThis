## Summary Card

**App:** Freeletics: Workouts & Fitness  
**Overall Risk:** Caution  
**Score:** 66/100  
**Grade:** C

### Red Flags
- **App behavior tracked by Google, Meta, TikTok, Snapchat, Reddit, and Remerge** — Freeletics shares your app usage and advertising identifiers with at least six major marketing platforms to show you personalized ads across the internet. Some platforms may use your data for their own purposes too.
- **Subscription prices may be personalized based on your data** — Freeletics uses cookies to analyze your usage patterns, location, and preferences to potentially offer you a different subscription price than another user.
- **Detailed health and body metrics stored on company servers** — With your consent, Freeletics stores weight, height, heart rate, workout history, and even body-area training preferences on AWS servers in the USA.
- **AI coach sends anonymized fitness metrics to OpenAI** — If you use Coach+, your age, gender, body weight, and training goal are sent to OpenAI to generate fitness advice. OpenAI claims a zero-retention policy, but Freeletics keeps the chat history to improve the feature.
- **Camera access required for motion-tracking feedback** — The optional motion-tracking feature uses your device's camera to watch your exercises in real time. While the video is processed locally and never leaves your phone, the app still needs camera permission.

### Green Flags
- **Health data only collected with clear, revocable consent** — Freeletics explicitly asks for your permission before reading or writing health data through Apple HealthKit, Google Health Connect, or Strava. You can withdraw this consent at any time.
- **Full account deletion removes all data permanently** — You can delete your Freeletics account from the app or website. This permanently erases all your training progress, nutrition data, and profile information across every Freeletics product.
- **Motion-tracking video never leaves your device** — When you use the motion-tracking feature, your camera feed is analyzed entirely on your phone. No images, videos, or biometric data are stored or sent to Freeletics or any third party.
- **Comprehensive GDPR rights and a data-protection officer** — Freeletics provides full GDPR rights including access, correction, erasure, and data portability. They also have an external data-protection officer you can contact directly.
- **Does not sell personal information under CCPA** — Freeletics states they do not sell the personal information they collect, as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act, and will not do so without giving you the right to opt out.

### Verdict
Freeletics is a polished, feature-rich fitness app with strong GDPR compliance and excellent workout personalization. Its privacy practices are generally transparent and user-friendly—health data requires explicit consent, account deletion is thorough, and motion-tracking is handled entirely on-device. However, the sheer number of third-party advertising and analytics integrations significantly drags down its privacy score. Users who sign up should carefully review all consent toggles in the app settings to limit how much of their behavior is shared with Google, Meta, TikTok, and other marketing platforms.
