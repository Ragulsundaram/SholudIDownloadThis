## Summary Card

**App:** Perplexity - AI Search & Chat
**Overall Risk:** Caution (Grade C)
**Score:** 58/100

### Red Flags
- **Data shared with advertising partners** - Perplexity discloses information to Advertising Partners and receives data from consumer marketing databases to customize advertising
- **Email sync accesses content** - If you sync your email, Perplexity has access to your contacts and the content of your email messages
- **Extensive tracking** - Uses cookies, pixels, tags, and tracking technologies to collect usage data including clickstream and ad impressions
- **Device fingerprinting** - Automatically collects unique device identifiers, IP address, and device information
- **Business transaction sharing** - Data shared with prospective buyers during mergers, acquisitions, or other transactions

### Green Flags
- **Email content not used for AI training** - Explicitly states email content is not used to create, train, or improve AI models
- **Account deletion available** - Can delete account with aim to delete personal information within 30 days
- **AI training opt-out** - Can opt out of information collection for AI model improvement
- **Recognizes GPC signals** - Global Privacy Control signals from browsers recognized as valid opt-out

### Verdict
Perplexity offers powerful AI search but collects extensive usage data and shares it with advertising partners and data enrichment companies. While it provides strong privacy controls including opt-outs for AI training and targeted advertising, the broad data sharing and tracking warrant caution. Consider using without syncing personal email accounts.

## Analysis Notes

### Key Findings

**1. Extensive Third-Party Data Sharing**
Perplexity shares data with:
- Company Group (affiliates)
- Service Providers
- Business Partners
- Advertising Partners
- Professional Advisors
- Data enrichment companies (receives data from)

**2. Email Sync Privacy Risk**
If you sync your email account:
- Access to your contacts
- Access to content of email messages
- Access to calendar appointments
- Important: Email content NOT used for AI training (positive)

**3. Comprehensive Tracking**
Automatically collects via cookies, pixels, tags:
- Device information (type, OS, unique identifier, IP)
- Location (approximate automatically, precise with consent)
- Clickstream data
- Interactions with marketing emails
- Ad impressions
- Browser type, log data, timestamps

**4. Data Enrichment for Advertising**
Perplexity receives information from:
- Consumer marketing databases
- Data enrichment companies
- Used to customize advertising and marketing

**5. Strong User Rights**
- Account deletion (30-day aim)
- Access, correction, deletion, portability rights
- Opt-out for AI training
- Opt-out for targeted advertising
- GPC signal recognition

**6. Business Transaction Sharing**
Data shared during:
- Mergers and acquisitions
- Asset sales
- Restructuring
- Bankruptcy proceedings

### User Rights
- **Access/Know** - Request information about data collected
- **Delete** - Request deletion of personal information
- **Correct** - Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Portability** - Receive copy of data in portable format
- **Opt-out of AI training** - Settings page option
- **Opt-out of targeted ads** - Cookie settings or "Do Not Share" link
- **GPC signals** - Recognized for targeted advertising opt-out

### Privacy Controls
- Settings page opt-out for AI training
- Cookie settings for targeted advertising
- "Do Not Share" link on homepage
- Account deletion via settings or support

## Verbatim Policy Excerpts

**On Advertising Partners:**
> "We may disclose your information to third parties subject to this Privacy Policy, including the following categories of third parties: Advertising Partners"

**On Data Enrichment:**
> "Information we receive from consumer marketing databases or other data enrichment companies, which we use in our legitimate interests to better customize advertising and marketing to you"

**On Email Sync:**
> "If you choose to sync these accounts, we will have access to your contacts and information from the email messages and calendar appointments in your email account, including the content of your emails... we do not use or disclose Email Service Information to create, train, improve or fine-tune AI models"

**On Tracking:**
> "We and certain third parties also automatically collect certain information about your interaction with the Services ('Usage Data') through the use of cookies, pixels, tags and other tracking technologies"

**On Device Information:**
> "Device information, such as device type, operating system, unique device identifier, and internet protocol (IP) address"

**On Account Deletion:**
> "If you delete your account, we aim to delete your personal information from our servers within 30 days"

**On GPC:**
> "We recognize Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals broadcast from web browsers as a valid opt-out request where required by applicable law"

**On Business Transactions:**
> "We will share personal information with a prospective buyer, seller, new owner, or other relevant third party as necessary while negotiating or in relation to a change of corporate control such as a restructuring, merger, asset sale or purchase"
