## Summary Card

**App:** Shaadi.com Matrimony App
**Overall Risk:** Caution (Grade C)
**Score:** 58/100

### Red Flags
- **Government ID and identity proofs required** - Collects Aadhaar and other identity documents as "sensitive personal data"
- **Your profile is shared with other users by design** - Personal information is published to other registered users for matchmaking
- **Collects bank account, credit card, and UPI details** - Financial data collected for paid services
- **The platform operates as an advertising service** - Policy states: "Shaadi.com is an advertising platform providing targeted advertising services"
- **Extensive personal data collection** - Name, gender, DOB, contact details, education, employment, photos, marital status, interests

### Green Flags
- **No sale of personal information** - Shaadi.com states it does not sell, rent, share, trade or give away personal information except as described in policy
- **Payment data encrypted** - Sensitive payment information encrypted per industry standards
- **Access and deletion rights available** - Users can access, rectify, update, and delete information through account settings
- **GDPR-style rights for EU/UK/California users** - Right to review, delete, and object to processing

### Verdict
Shaadi.com collects extensive personal data including government IDs and financial information as a core function of its matrimonial service. While it provides data access controls and doesn't sell data, it operates as an advertising platform and shares user profiles widely with other users.

## Analysis Notes

### Key Findings

**1. Data Collection Categories**
Shaadi.com gathers three types of information:
- Information voluntarily submitted (name, gender, DOB, contact, education, employment, photos, marital status, interests)
- Sensitive personal data (identity proofs like Aadhaar)
- Information not directly submitted (user activity, device information)
- Information from others (social media logins)

**2. Sensitive Data Collection**
The policy explicitly collects:
- Identity proofs (Aadhaar, etc.) classified as "sensitive personal data"
- Bank account, debit/credit card, UPI details for paid services
- Photos and testimonials
- Messages exchanged with other users

**3. Advertising Platform Model**
The policy states: "Shaadi.com is an advertising platform providing targeted advertising services for the purpose of matchmaking." This is unique language compared to typical social apps.

**4. Data Sharing**
- With other users: Profile information published to other registered users
- With service providers: Website development, hosting, maintenance, backup, storage, payment processing, analysis
- With law enforcement: When required by law or to protect rights

**5. Data Retention**
- Personal data retained only as long as necessary to provide services
- Information may be retained for record keeping, completing transactions, or legal compliance
- Retained information handled with same level of security as active data

**6. International Data Transfer**
- Information collected globally, primarily stored in India and USA
- Data may be transferred outside country of residence
- Standard contractual data protection clauses used

**7. User Rights**
- Access and update information directly through account
- Request copy of personal information
- Request deletion or object to processing
- Withdraw consent (results in account deletion)
- EU/UK/California users have additional GDPR-style rights

**8. Security**
- Encryption for sensitive information (credit card numbers)
- Access limited to authorized employees
- Acknowledges no security system is impenetrable

### Policy Change Notification
- Changes may be made to reflect legal/regulatory changes or data processing practices
- Material changes notified "in a clear and accessible manner as required under applicable law"
- No explicit "continued use = acceptance" language in privacy policy

## Verbatim Policy Excerpts

**On Platform Nature:**
> "Shaadi.com is an advertising platform providing targeted advertising services for the purpose of matchmaking ("Service")."

**On Data Collection:**
> "While registering for our service, you share with us your personal data, such as name, your gender, date of birth, contact details, educational qualification, employment details, photos, marital status and your interests and sensitive personal data such as your Identity proofs."

**On Financial Data:**
> "When you avail for a paid service, you provide us or our payment service provider with the sensitive personal data such as your Bank account, debit or credit card number or UPI."

**On No Sale of Data:**
> "Except where you are expressly informed on the site or as described in this privacy policy we do not sell, rent, share, trade or give away any of your personal information."

**On Profile Sharing:**
> "we publish the information shared by you with other users to provide the services. The information so published are provided by you and be cautious as what you share with other users."

**On International Transfer:**
> "We collect information globally and primarily store that information in India and USA. We may transfer, process and store your information outside of your country of residence."

**On Deletion Rights:**
> "If you believe that the information we hold about you, are no longer entitled to use it and you want to delete or object to its processing, please contact us here."

**On Data Retention:**
> "The Company shall retain Personal Data only for such period as is necessary to provide the Services for which it was collected, and thereafter only for such additional period as may be required or permitted under applicable law."
