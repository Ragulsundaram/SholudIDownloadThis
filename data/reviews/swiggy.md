## Summary Card

**App:** Swiggy: Food Instamart Dineout
**Overall Risk:** Dangerous (Grade F)
**Score:** 34/100

### Red Flags
- **Extensive data sharing with third parties** - Shared with vendors, consultants, marketing partners, advertisers, AI service providers, and group companies
- **You cannot opt out of advertising on the Platforms** - Explicit statement that advertising opt-out is not possible
- **Checks what other apps are installed on your device** - Collects "installed applications on device"
- **Data stored outside India** - Data stored on Amazon Web Services servers which may be located outside India
- **AI service providers receive your data** - Order details, delivery information, preferences, and interaction data shared for AI features
- **May collect prescription and blood group data** - Health-related data collected for Instamart pharmaceutical services and delivery partners

### Green Flags
- **Tokenized payment data** - Uses vault and tokenization services; PCI compliant
- **Data deletion available** - Can request deletion via support@swiggy.in
- **Grievance officer appointed** - Dedicated contact for privacy concerns

### Verdict
Swiggy collects extensive data and shares it widely across its ecosystem and with third parties. The explicit denial of ad opt-out and collection of installed apps list are major privacy concerns. Health data collection for prescriptions adds additional sensitivity.

## Analysis Notes

### Key Findings

**1. Extensive Data Collection**
Swiggy collects:
- Device information: hardware models, OS versions, installed applications, advertising identifiers, phone state
- Account details: full name, email, address, phone number, gender
- Location: real-time device location
- Communications: messages between users, service providers, merchants
- Transactional data: billing information in tokenized form
- Usage data: search terms, ads clicked, features used
- File metadata: metadata from files stored on device

**2. Delivery Partner Data Collection**
For PDPs/Pickers, Swiggy additionally collects:
- Government IDs: PAN card, Aadhaar/Voter ID, driving license
- Photographs and real-time selfies
- Bank details
- Blood group
- Nominee/spouse/emergency contact details
- Home address for inventory delivery

**3. Cannot Opt Out of Advertising**
Explicit policy statement: "You cannot opt out of advertising on the Platforms." While interest-based advertising opt-out is available for third-party sites, Swiggy's own advertising cannot be opted out of.

**4. Data Sharing**
Shared with:
- Service providers, vendors, consultants
- Marketing partners and research firms
- Retail Pharmacies and Medical Practitioners
- Delivery partners and business partners
- Payment processing companies
- Academic partners (anonymized)
- AI service providers (for AI-assisted features)
- Advertisers and advertising networks
- Group companies and affiliates
- Partner restaurants/merchants

**5. AI Data Sharing**
For AI-assisted features, Swiggy shares:
- Personal data and transaction-related information
- Order details and delivery information
- Preferences and interaction data

**6. Data Storage Outside India**
Platforms store data with Amazon Web Services cloud platform, which may store data on servers located outside India.

**7. Data Retention**
Upon deletion request, personal information is retained in "anonymized or aggregated form, for as long necessary" for analytics, improving products/services, and other business purposes.

### User Rights
- **Access/Correction** - Can update information through account
- **Deletion** - Can request deletion via support@swiggy.in (takes up to 5 business days)
- **Communication Preferences** - Can unsubscribe from commercial emails, but not administrative/service notices
- **Opt-out** - Cannot opt out of Swiggy advertising; can opt out of third-party interest-based advertising

### Privacy Controls
- Location permissions can be managed in device settings
- Cookie settings adjustable in browser
- Account deletion available via email request

## Verbatim Policy Excerpts

**On No Advertising Opt-Out:**
> "You cannot opt out of advertising on the Platforms, you may opt out of much interest-based advertising on third party sites and through third party ad networks"

**On Installed Apps Collection:**
> "information about the devices you use to access our Services, including... installed applications on device and phone state"

**On Data Sharing with AI Providers:**
> "To enable AI-assisted features, we may share certain personal data and transaction-related information with third-party AI service providers, including order details, delivery information, preferences, and interaction data"

**On Delivery Partner Data:**
> "Specifically from PDPs/Pickers we may collect: photographs, phone number, name, Pan card, Aadhar/Voter ID, driving license, Bank details, Location... blood group"

**On Health Data:**
> "Additionally, we may also collect information relating to you (including your prescriptions), to facilitate through the Instamart Platform, your purchase of pharmaceutical drugs from retail pharmacies"

**On Data Storage Location:**
> "the Platforms store your data with the cloud platform of Amazon Web Services provided by Amazon Web Services, Inc., which may store this data on their servers located outside of India"

**On Data Retention:**
> "We may, however, retain and store your personal information in anonymized or aggregated form, for as long as necessary, and use it as permitted under applicable law"

**On Policy Changes:**
> "your continued use of the Services following the changes to this Privacy Policy will constitute your consent and acceptance of those changes"

**On Extensive Third-Party Sharing:**
> "We may share your information with our vendors, consultants, marketing partners, Retail Pharmacies, Medical Practitioners, delivery partners, research firms and other service providers, business partners, payment processing companies"
