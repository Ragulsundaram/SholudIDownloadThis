## Summary Card

**Overall Rating:** Safe (A) — 85/100  
**Recommendation:** Recommended

### Red Flags
- **Some data stored on US servers:** Your information is stored on computers in both Europe and the United States. The United States has weaker privacy laws than Europe, which means your data may not be protected as strongly.
- **Optional medical diagnosis collected:** When setting up a user profile, you can optionally enter a medical diagnosis. While this helps with support, it means sensitive health information is stored on their servers.
- **Policy changes accepted automatically:** If they update the privacy rules, you will get a notification. But if you keep using the app after that, it counts as agreeing to the new rules — you don't have to click "I agree".

### Green Flags
- **No ads and data is never sold:** The company explicitly promises they will never sell your personal data, and they will never use it for advertising or marketing purposes.
- **Your messages stay on your device:** The sentences and messages you create to communicate are processed entirely on your phone or tablet. They are never sent to AssistiveWare or anyone else.
- **Strong protections for children:** The app follows strict child privacy laws like COPPA and GDPR. Parents or guardians control what data is collected and who can see it.
- **Extra data sharing requires your consent:** If they want to collect more than basic anonymous usage stats, they ask for your explicit permission first. You can say no without breaking the app.

### Verdict
Proloquo is an augmentative communication app designed for people who need help speaking. Its privacy policy is unusually clear and respectful: the company explicitly promises never to sell your data or use it for ads, your actual communication messages never leave your device, and there are strong protections for children including parent-controlled data sharing. However, some data is stored on US servers, optional health diagnosis information can be collected, and policy changes are accepted passively if you keep using the app.

### Key Policy Excerpts
> "We will never sell your personal data to third parties, nor will we use or share your personal data for third party advertising, -profiling or -marketing purposes."

> "Communication messages are created on the hardware device itself and do not use any connection with AssistiveWare or other parties in the generation process. ### Reviewer Notes
- The privacy policy is unusually clear and well-structured for an AAC app.
- Explicit no-selling and no-advertising commitments are rare and noteworthy.
- Local-only message processing is a significant privacy win for an app that handles sensitive communication.
- Strong COPPA/GDPR compliance with parent/guardian control mechanisms.
- Optional diagnosis field is the main health data concern, but it is genuinely optional and used for legitimate support purposes.
- Data transfer to US servers is disclosed with mention of Standard Contractual Clauses.
- Needs review: false — the policy is clear and consistent.
