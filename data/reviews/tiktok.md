# TikTok Review

**App:** TikTok
**Analyzed:** 2026-05-16
**Policy URL:** https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy
**Terms URL:** https://www.tiktok.com/legal/terms-of-service

---

## Summary Card

- **Overall risk:** Risky (Score: 49, Grade: D)

### Red Flags
- **Infers your interests, gender, and age range.** TikTok states that it infers your attributes, including your interests, gender, and age range, for the purpose of personalising content.
- **Shares data with advertisers and business partners.** TikTok explicitly shares your information with advertisers, advertising partners, publishers, and third-party measurement companies.
- **Tracks your activity across devices.** TikTok explicitly uses your device IDs and user ID to identify your activity across devices, and may associate you with information from devices other than those you use to log in.
- **Analyzes objects and faces in your videos.** TikTok may collect information about the videos, images, and audio in your User Content, such as identifying objects, scenery, face and body features, and the text of spoken words.
- **Keeps your data for broad purposes.** TikTok states that it retains your information for as long as necessary to provide the Platform and for other purposes, including legal, safety, and business interests.

### Green Flags
- **You can delete your account.** TikTok states that you may delete your entire account in Settings.
- **Privacy controls available.** TikTok provides tools in Settings to control who can view your videos, send you messages, or post comments.
- **Data rights request portal.** You may submit a request to exercise your data rights through its privacy portal.

### Verdict
TikTok collects extensive data about your behavior, location, contacts, and device, and shares it widely with advertisers and partners. While the app offers deletion and privacy controls, its broad data collection, cross-device tracking, and content analysis pose significant privacy risks. Think twice before downloading if privacy matters to you.
