## Summary Card

**App:** TikTok
**Overall Risk:** Risky (Grade D)
**Score:** 53/100

### Red Flags
- **Collects keystroke patterns and rhythms** — TikTok collects "keystroke patterns or rhythms" as part of device technical information
- **Analyzes face and body features in videos** — Collects "existence and location within an image of face and body features and attributes" for effects, moderation, and ads
- **Infers sensitive attributes** — Explicitly infers your interests, gender, and age range for content personalization
- **Tracks across devices** — Uses device IDs to identify activity across devices, may associate you with devices you don't log in from
- **Extensive data sharing** — Shares with advertisers, business partners, corporate affiliates, researchers, and law enforcement
- **Broad data retention** — Keeps data as long as necessary for Platform plus legal, safety, and business purposes

### Green Flags
- **Account deletion available** — You can delete your entire account in Settings
- **Data rights portal** — Submit requests for access, deletion, update, or rectification through privacy portal
- **Privacy controls** — Settings tools to control who can view videos, send messages, or post comments

### Verdict
TikTok's data collection is exceptionally broad, including keystroke patterns, face/body analysis, and cross-device tracking. Combined with extensive sharing with advertisers and corporate partners (including ByteDance), this poses significant privacy risks.

## Analysis Notes

### Key Findings

**1. Keystroke Pattern Collection**
TikTok explicitly collects keystroke patterns:
> "keystroke patterns or rhythms"

This is highly unusual and concerning from a privacy perspective.

**2. Face and Body Feature Analysis**
TikTok analyzes uploaded content for biometric features:
> "We may collect information about the videos, images and audio that are a part of your User Content, such as identifying the objects and scenery that appear, the existence and location within an image of face and body features and attributes"

Used for: special video effects, content moderation, demographic classification, content and ad recommendations

**3. Inferred Sensitive Attributes**
TikTok explicitly infers personal characteristics:
> "We also infer your attributes, including your interests, gender and age range for the purpose of personalising content."

**4. Cross-Device Tracking**
Sophisticated tracking across devices:
> "Where you log-in from multiple devices, we use your information such as your device IDs and user ID to identify your activity across devices. We may also associate you with information collected from devices other than those you use to log-in to the Platform."

**5. Extensive Data Sharing**
Shared with:
- Business Partners (including social networks)
- Service Providers (cloud, moderation, marketing)
- Advertisers, Advertising Networks, Publishers, Measurement Partners
- Independent Researchers
- Corporate Group (ByteDance affiliates)
- Law Enforcement (when required)
- Sellers and Payment Providers (for shopping)

**6. Content Pre-Loading**
Content is collected before explicit upload:
> "We collect User Content through pre-loading at the time of creation, import, or upload, regardless of whether you choose to save or upload that User Content"

**7. Third-Party Data Matching**
TikTok receives data from outside the platform:
> "Advertisers, publishers, and measurement and other partners share information with us about you and the actions you have taken outside of the Platform, such as your activities on other websites and apps or in stores"

**8. Approximate Location Auto-Collection**
Location collected automatically without permission:
> "We collect information about your approximate location, including location information based on your SIM card and/or IP address."

Precise GPS only with permission.

**9. Broad Retention Policy**
Data kept for multiple purposes:
> "We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the Platform and for the other purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. We also retain information when necessary to comply with contractual and legal obligations, when we have a legitimate business interest to do so... and for the exercise or defence of legal claims."

**10. M&A Data Transfer**
Data may transfer in business transactions:
> "in the event that we sell or buy any business or assets... we will disclose your data to the prospective seller or buyer... if we sell, buy, merge, are acquired by, or partner with other companies or businesses... user information may be among the transferred assets."
