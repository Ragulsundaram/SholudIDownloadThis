## Summary Card

### Yoga for Beginners, Pilates+
**Overall Risk:** Caution (Score: 73 / Grade: B)

**Red Flags:**
- **Privacy policy appears copy-pasted from an Android app** — The privacy policy repeatedly mentions "Android applications" and the "Google Play Platform" even though this is an iOS app, suggesting it is a generic template that was not properly reviewed or customized.
- **Ads and analytics track your device** — Although the developer says they do not collect your personal information, the app uses advertising providers and Google Analytics that can identify your device and monitor how you use the app.
- **Policy silent on camera, contacts, and files** — The privacy policy does not say whether the app can access your camera, microphone, contacts, or the files on your phone, leaving you to guess what permissions it actually requests.
- **Unverified audit claim in privacy policy** — The policy claims its no-sharing practices were "Verified by BBC & Privacy International," but provides no proof, link, or date for this supposed verification.

**Green Flags:**
- **No account or registration required** — You can use the app without creating an account or providing your name, email, or phone number.
- **Claims not to store personal data on servers** — The developer states they do not have a server to store your personal information, meaning most of your data stays on your device.
- **Health data import is optional** — If you want to connect the app to Apple Health or Google Fit to import workout data, you must explicitly give permission first.

**Verdict:** Yoga for Beginners, Pilates+ is a simple workout app whose privacy policy is remarkably brief and appears copied from an Android template. The developer claims they do not collect personal information, do not require registration, and do not have a server to store data. However, the app does use advertising providers and Google Analytics, which can identify your device and track usage. The policy is silent on camera, microphone, contacts, and file access, and makes an unverified claim of being audited by BBC and Privacy International. While the data collection footprint appears small, the vague and generic policy makes it hard to be certain.

## Review Notes

- The privacy policy is extremely short (under 4,000 characters) and generic.
- The policy repeatedly references "Android applications" and the "Google Play Platform" despite this being an iOS app, strongly suggesting it is a copy-pasted template.
- The developer claims they do not collect PII and do not have a server, yet the app integrates ad providers and Google Analytics which do collect device identifiers and usage data.
- The claim of verification by "BBC & Privacy International" appears in the policy with no supporting evidence, link, or date.
- The policy is silent on camera, microphone, contacts, storage, children's data handling, data deletion, and policy change notifications.
- Health data import via Apple Health / Google Fit is explicitly optional.
- No account registration is required, and no payment processing is mentioned.
- The app likely functions primarily offline, with analytics and ads being the only network-dependent data flows.
