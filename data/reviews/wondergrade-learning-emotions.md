## Summary Card

### Wondergrade: Learning Emotions
- **Overall Risk:** Caution  
- **Score:** 65 / 100 (Grade C)  
- **Recommendation:** Think Twice

**Red Flags**
- **App targets children but claims it doesn't** — The app is advertised for kids ages 3 to 8, but its privacy policy says it is not meant for anyone under 13. This means the app may not follow the special rules that protect children's privacy online.
- **Admits to selling personal data** — Under California privacy law, the company says it may have sold personal information like your name, email, and online activity to other companies.
- **Multiple third-party trackers** — The app uses Google Analytics, Firebase, and Facebook to track how you use it. These companies can see your activity and may use it for ads.
- **Data shared with affiliates and partners** — Your information is shared with business partners and affiliated companies who may use it for their own promotions.

**Green Flags**
- **Users have data rights under GDPR and CCPA** — If you live in Europe or California, you have legal rights to see, correct, or delete your data.
- **Payments handled by Apple** — Your credit card details go straight to Apple and are never seen by the app developer.
- **Promises notice before policy changes** — The company says it will email you before making big changes to the privacy rules.

**Verdict**  
Wondergrade appears to be a thoughtful emotions-learning app for young children, but its privacy policy is deeply problematic. The app is explicitly marketed to children ages 3-8, yet the privacy policy claims it does not address anyone under 13 — a direct contradiction that raises serious COPPA compliance concerns. Additionally, the company admits it may have "sold" personal data under California law. Parents should think carefully before sharing their child's data with this service.

## Full Analysis

### What it does
Wondergrade is an interactive early childhood education app for children ages 3-8. It offers read-aloud books, calming games, mindfulness audio stories, and parent guidance tools for emotional learning and self-regulation.

### Data collected
- **Personal Data:** Email address, first name, last name
- **Usage Data:** IP address, browser type, browser version, pages visited, time and date of visit, time spent on pages, unique device identifiers, diagnostic data
- **Mobile Data:** Mobile device type, unique ID, mobile device IP address, mobile operating system, mobile browser type

### Who it is shared with
- **Service Providers:** For monitoring, analyzing, payment processing, and contacting users
- **Google Analytics:** Tracks and reports website/app traffic; data shared with other Google services and may be used for ad personalization
- **Firebase:** Google analytics service for app usage tracking
- **Mailchimp:** Email marketing service for newsletters and promotional communications
- **Facebook:** Facebook Insights for anonymized statistical data (places 2-year cookies)
- **Affiliates:** Parent company, subsidiaries, joint venture partners
- **Business Partners:** For offering products, services, or promotions
- **Potential Acquirers:** In case of merger, sale, or acquisition

### CCPA "Sale" of Data
The policy explicitly states: "We may sell and may have sold in the last twelve (12) months" the following categories of personal information:
- Category A: Identifiers
- Category B: Personal information categories listed in California Customer Records statute
- Category D: Commercial information
- Category F: Internet or other similar network activity

The policy defines "sale" broadly under CCPA to include sharing for "valuable consideration," not just monetary exchange.

### Children's Data
The App Store description explicitly targets "children ages 3-8." However, the privacy policy states:
- "Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13."
- "We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13."

This is a direct contradiction. Under COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act), apps directed at children under 13 must comply with strict parental consent and data minimization requirements. There is no mention of COPPA compliance anywhere in the policy.

### Retention
Personal data is retained "only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy." Usage data is generally retained for shorter periods except when used for security or functionality improvements.
