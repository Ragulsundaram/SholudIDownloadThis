## Summary Card

**Overall Risk:** Risky  
**Score:** 49 / 100 (Grade D)

### Red Flags
- **Explicitly reserves the right to sell your aggregated data.** The privacy policy states that aggregated personal information is the company's property and can be sold commercially to third parties without compensating you.
- **Shares your data across the entire MakeMyTrip group.** Your personal information is shared with affiliate and associate entities including MakeMyTrip and Goibibo.
- **Stores your banking and payment details.** redBus collects and stores credit/debit card numbers in encrypted form, banking details, wallet info, and complete payment history. It also verifies credit information selectively.
- **Tracks your device with a unique identifier.** The app collects your unique device identifier, Android ID, hardware model, OS version, and mobile network to identify you across sessions.
- **Allows third-party advertisers to track you.** Third-party advertising companies place cookies to track ad effectiveness and show you targeted ads on other websites.
- **Accesses your contacts for social features.** If permitted, redBus reads your contacts to share tickets, auto-fill emails, and select recharge numbers.
- **Your data goes with the company if it is sold.** In an acquisition, sale, or restructuring, your customer information will be transferred to the new owner.
- **App Store rating conflicts with transaction age requirement.** Rated 4+ on the App Store but requires users to be 18+ to transact.

### Green Flags
- **Encrypts payments with industry-standard security.** All payment information is transmitted using TLS encryption, and secure servers are used for account access.
- **You can access and edit some of your data.** You can log into your account to view, correct, or delete some personal information directly.
- **You can opt out of marketing emails.** An unsubscribe link is provided in promotional emails.
- **Service providers are restricted from reusing your data.** redBus does not authorize bus operators and hotels to use your information for purposes other than fulfilling their part of the service.

### Verdict
redBus is a convenient and popular travel booking app, but its privacy practices are concerning. The explicit reservation of rights to commercially sell aggregated personal information, combined with broad group company sharing, extensive financial data storage, and third-party advertising tracking, makes this a risky choice for privacy-conscious travelers. If you must use it, avoid storing payment details and limit permissions where possible.

## Review Notes

- The app is now operated by **MakeMyTrip (India) Private Limited**, following the merger of RedBus India Private Limited into MakeMyTrip. The privacy policy explicitly covers MakeMyTrip and its subsidiaries/affiliates.
- The most alarming clause: **"Any Personal Information which we collect and which we may use in an aggregated format is our property. We may use it, in our sole discretion and without any compensation to you, for any legitimate purpose including without limitation the commercial sale thereof to third parties."** This is an unusually explicit commercial data-sale reservation.
- **Cross-border data processing** is acknowledged: data is primarily processed in India and other jurisdictions where third parties operate. Users outside India are warned that data protection regulations may differ from their home country.
- **Financial data collection** is extensive: cardholder name, credit/debit card number (encrypted), expiration date, banking details, wallet details, payment history, and billing info. Credit information may be verified selectively for fraud detection and "bookings on credit."
- **Service provider sharing** is operationally necessary (bus operators, hotels need passenger details), and redBus does contractually restrict them from using data for other purposes. However, the policy disclaims accountability for how service providers actually use the data.
- **Session logging** is comprehensive: IP address, OS, browser type, and all activities conducted on the app are logged to analyze browsing patterns and diagnose problems.
- **Device fingerprinting** uses Android ID to uniquely identify users and pre-populate fields. This is explicitly acknowledged as a tracking mechanism.
- **Account deletion** requires manual email to privacy@redbus.com with identity verification. Identity proof is stored for 21 days post-deletion, and transaction records may be retained per local laws. The 12-month credential re-use block is an unusual restriction.
- The **4+ App Store rating** with an 18+ service requirement creates a problematic gap where children can install an app they are not legally supposed to use.
