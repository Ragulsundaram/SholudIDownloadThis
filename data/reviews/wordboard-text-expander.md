## Summary Card

### WordBoard: Text Expander
- **Overall Risk:** Safe  
- **Score:** 86 / 100 (Grade A)  
- **Recommendation:** Recommended

**Red Flags**
- **Privacy policy is extremely short** — The policy is only a few sentences long and does not explain what data is collected, how it is protected, or what your rights are.
- **App contradicts its own privacy policy** — The App Store description claims WordBoard does not use network access to collect data, but the privacy policy says it sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports over the internet.
- **Keyboard needs 'Full Access' to work** — iOS warns that Full Access lets the keyboard developer see everything you type. The developer promises not to, but the permission itself is broad.

**Green Flags**
- **Explicitly promises no keystroke logging** — The developer makes a clear promise not to collect your keystrokes or send them to a server.
- **Promises no access to passwords or cards** — The developer explicitly promises not to access passwords, credit card numbers, or other sensitive information.
- **Your text snippets stay on your device** — The text you save is stored locally on your phone, not on the developer's servers.

**Verdict**  
WordBoard makes unusually strong privacy promises for a keyboard app, explicitly committing not to log keystrokes or access sensitive data. Your snippets stay on your device. However, the privacy policy is barely more than a paragraph, contradicts the app's own description, and leaves critical questions about data deletion, children's privacy, and device information unanswered. The app appears trustworthy, but the documentation does not fully back that up.

## Full Analysis

### What it does
WordBoard is a custom iOS keyboard that lets users save and instantly insert text snippets, phrases, templates, images, and GIFs while typing in any app. It includes 6 free keys with a Pro upgrade for unlimited snippets.

### Data collected
- **Anonymous usage statistics** — "how many people are using the app etc" (unspecified data points, unspecified third-party service)
- **Anonymous crash reports** — for stability improvements (unspecified third-party service)
- **No account required** — the app works without any login or identity information

### Who it is shared with
- The policy mentions "anonymous usage statistics" and "anonymous crash reports" but does not name the specific third-party analytics or crash-reporting services used.
- The developer explicitly promises not to send keystrokes, passwords, credit card numbers, or other personal data to any server.

### Retention
Not mentioned in the privacy policy.

### User rights
Not mentioned in the privacy policy. No contact information, data deletion process, or rights explanation is provided.

### Policy history
- **Last updated:** Unknown (no date on the policy page)
- The policy simply says: "At time to time we may update this webpage, please visit here to see the latest version."

### Notes for reviewer
- The privacy policy is one of the shortest we have encountered (~1.5KB raw text).
- There is a direct contradiction: the App Store description says "WordBoard does not use network access to collect your data" while the privacy policy explicitly states it collects "anonymous usage statistics" and "anonymous crash reports" — both of which require network access.
- The app requires iOS "Full Access" for the keyboard, which Apple's system warning explicitly describes as allowing the developer to "transmit anything you type." The developer addresses this directly in the policy, which is positive, but users must trust this promise without detailed legal backing.
- No mention of GDPR, CCPA, COPPA, or any data protection framework.
- No HealthKit, biometric, location, contacts, or camera access.
- Payment for Pro is handled via standard App Store in-app purchases.
- The developer (Bytesize Systems Pty Ltd) is based in Perth, Australia, and also makes the 7 Minute Workout app already in our database.
