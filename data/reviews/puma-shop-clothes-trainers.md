## Summary Card

### PUMA | Shop Clothes & Trainers
**Risk Level:** Caution | **Score:** 68/100 | **Grade:** C

#### Red Flags
- **Uses device fingerprinting for tracking:** PUMA explicitly uses fingerprinting technology to recognise your device and track your actions across app sessions, making it harder to avoid tracking even if you clear cookies.
- **Shares data with retargeting partners:** Your shopping behaviour and personal data are shared with retargeting providers who show you ads on other websites and apps based on what you looked at in the PUMA app.
- **Data shared with linked social media platforms:** PUMA shares your data with social media platforms it is linked with, which can use this information to build advertising profiles about you.
- **Keeps your data for up to 10 years:** PUMA stores your personal data for up to 10 years to comply with commercial and tax laws, meaning your information remains on their systems long after you stop using the app.

#### Green Flags
- **Clear data subject rights available:** You can request access to, correction of, erasure of, or export of your personal data by emailing PUMA. They also have a grievance officer you can contact.
- **Analytics consent can be withdrawn:** You can withdraw your consent for analytics tracking at any time in your app account settings. If you do, PUMA will only analyse your use on an anonymous, aggregated basis.
- **IP addresses are anonymised:** PUMA shortens your IP address immediately after collection so it cannot be connected back to you, which limits location-based profiling.
- **App permissions are user-controllable:** You can change push notification, camera, and location permissions at any time through your app account settings or your phone's system settings.
- **Uses EU Standard Contractual Clauses:** When sharing your data with service providers outside the EU, PUMA uses legally binding EU Standard Contractual Clauses to protect your data.

#### Verdict
PUMA's shopping app is a GDPR-compliant fashion retail app with transparent data practices. It collects standard purchase and device data, uses your camera for virtual try-on, and tracks your behaviour with cookies and fingerprinting for analytics and retargeting. The app shares data with service providers including retargeting partners and social media platforms, and stores data for up to 10 years for legal compliance. On the positive side, it offers clear data subject rights (access, erasure, export), anonymises IP addresses, and lets you withdraw analytics consent in app settings.
