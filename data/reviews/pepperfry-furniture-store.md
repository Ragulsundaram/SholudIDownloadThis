## Summary

Pepperfry is a well-known Indian furniture and home decor marketplace. While it handles payments securely through third-party processors and protects passwords with standard measures, its privacy policy raises concerns. The company shares personal information broadly with affiliates, joint ventures, and third-party marketers, and may include your data in business sales or transfers. They actively track your behavior using cookies and web beacons for advertising and analytics. Deletion is possible but may cancel your account. Several key privacy areas are not addressed at all in the policy, including location, contacts, camera, and file access.

Key concerns: broad data sharing with corporate family and third parties, behavioral tracking for ads, automatic acceptance of policy changes, government data sharing, and data transfer to India.

Key positives: payment info handled by third parties (app never sees it), password protection mentioned, deletion available via email request, and a dedicated grievance officer listed.

## Summary Card

### Pepperfry Furniture Store
- **Overall Risk:** Caution
- **Score:** 58/100 (Grade C)

**Red Flags:**
- **Shares your data with many affiliates and partners.** Pepperfry shares your personal information with its parent companies, affiliates, joint ventures, agents, and third-party service providers for marketing and services. They also say your data may be sold or transferred during a merger, acquisition, or bankruptcy.
- **Tracks your behavior for ads and marketing.** Pepperfry collects how you use the app and website, uses cookies and web beacons to follow your activity, and sends you ads, promotions, and marketing materials from themselves and their business partners.
- **Policy changes accepted automatically.** If Pepperfry updates its privacy rules, your continued use of the app counts as agreeing to the new terms. They won't ask you to confirm or re-accept the changes.
- **May share data with government agencies.** Pepperfry says it will disclose your personal information to government, law enforcement, or tax authorities if they believe it is necessary or appropriate.

**Green Flags:**
- **Payment details stay with providers.** When you pay, your card and bank details go straight to payment providers. Pepperfry says it never sees or stores your actual financial information.
- **Passwords are reasonably protected.** The company says it uses reasonable security measures to protect your account password from being exposed or disclosed, even to their own staff.
- **You can request data deletion.** Although you have to email customer service, Pepperfry does offer a way to ask for your personal information to be deleted from their records.
- **Has a dedicated grievance officer.** Pepperfry has appointed a Grievance Officer with a name, address, phone number, and email for handling privacy complaints, as required by Indian IT laws.

**Verdict:** Pepperfry is a well-known Indian furniture and home decor marketplace. They actively track your behavior using cookies and web beacons for advertising and analytics. Deletion is possible but may cancel your account. Several key privacy areas are not addressed at all in the policy, including location, contacts, camera, and file access.
